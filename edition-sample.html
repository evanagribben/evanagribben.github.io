AT A GLANCE

Peak Bay summer is here, and this stretch is stacked: tomorrow Charley Crockett plays a sold-out-by-lottery Stern Grove; next weekend the San Francisco Marathon takes over the waterfront while Sausalito and San Jose throw festivals; and the weekend of August 7–9 is the single busiest of the summer — Outside Lands, San Jose Jazz Summer Fest, Pistahan, the Gravenstein Apple Fair and the Sonoma County Fair all at once. Tonight, if you're free: the Hayes Valley Carnival runs into the evening, the Glen Park Night Market goes 3–8pm, and the expansion-darling Valkyries tip off at Chase Center at 5:30pm.

Weekend weather: Classic July — gray mornings, afternoon clearing, highs near 62°F in the city Saturday and Sunday with only a 10% drizzle chance; warming into the upper 60s Monday and low 70s Tuesday. Outdoor picks are safe; bring layers for Stern Grove's eucalyptus chill (it runs 10°F colder than downtown). No rain contingencies needed this weekend.

⏰ Act-now clock: Stern Grove's finale weekend (Public Enemy Aug 15, Al Green Aug 16) is the only stretch of the season not marked sold out — GA is free by lottery, but entry windows opened July 4–5 and the festival says each lottery runs about a week, so check sterngrove.org/ticket-info today for remaining entry or waitlist chances (reserved tables are still on sale). Outside Lands single-day tickets (daily lineups are out) are moving at sfoutsidelands.com. SF Marathon (July 25–26) is one week out — register while entries last at thesfmarathon.com.

★ PICK OF THE MONTH
Stern Grove Festival Finale Weekend: Public Enemy & Al Green
Sat Aug 15 (Public Enemy w/ LaRussell) & Sun Aug 16 (Al Green w/ Goapele & the Glide Ensemble), afternoon shows · Sigmund Stern Grove, 19th Ave & Sloat Blvd, SF · Free (GA by lottery; reserved tables sold separately)
Why this one: a soul legend who rarely tours, singing "Let's Stay Together" for free in a eucalyptus grove, is about as only-in-SF as it gets — and it's the season finale: every earlier 2026 show is marked sold out, while the finale weekend still shows live ticket paths (the GA lotteries opened July 4–5 — check for remaining entry or waitlist — and reserved tables are on sale). Two chances, zero dollars if the lottery breaks your way, and nearly nine decades of Stern Grove tradition. Take the L Taraval or 23/28 Muni lines; parking is scarce. Fog is part of the experience — bring a blanket. Lottery & details: sterngrove.org/lineup2026 · ticket info

MUSIC · ARTS · NIGHTLIFE
MUSIC
Stern Grove Festival — the closing month — SF's storied free summer concert series in a natural amphitheater. Remaining shows: Sun Jul 26 Suki Waterhouse, Sun Aug 2 Violent Femmes w/ Tune-Yards, Sun Aug 9 Patti LaBelle, Sat Aug 15 Public Enemy, Sun Aug 16 Al Green. Sigmund Stern Grove, 19th Ave & Sloat, SF. Free via GA lottery — Jul 26–Aug 9 are marked sold out; Aug 15–16 are the only shows that aren't (lotteries opened Jul 4–5 with roughly one-week entry windows, so check for remaining entry/waitlist; reserved tables on sale). All ages. Muni over driving, always. Lineup & lottery good for: lawn picnics, legends on a budget
Outside Lands 2026 — the year's marquee splurge: Charli XCX, Rufus Du Sol and The Strokes headline 90+ acts, plus the usual Wine Lands/Outside Clams food universe. Fri–Sun Aug 7–9 · Golden Gate Park (Polo Field entrances on Fulton/MLK). 3-day and single-day GA on sale; daily lineups published — popular days sell out; don't wait. All ages. No festival parking: Muni 5/N or festival shuttles. Lineup & tickets good for: the one big blowout
San Jose Jazz Summer Fest — a dozen stages of jazz, Latin, R&B and salsa dancing spill across downtown San Jose the same weekend as Outside Lands, at a fraction of the price. Fri–Sun Aug 7–9 · Plaza de César Chávez & downtown San Jose. Day passes on sale. All ages. Caltrain/VTA to Diridon beats parking garages. Lineup & passes good for: the anti-Outside-Lands weekend
Lionel Richie & Earth, Wind & Fire — "Sing a Song All Night Long" — two catalogs of pure joy in one arena night. Thu Aug 6, 7:30pm · Chase Center, Mission Bay, SF. Tickets via Ticketmaster from the venue page. Chase Center events good for: multigenerational group outings
Meghan Trainor — The Get In Girl Tour — Sat Aug 8, 6:30pm · Chase Center, SF. An indoor pop counterweight to Outside Lands weekend. Tickets
★ Jerry Day 2026 — the Excelsior's homegrown Grateful Dead tribute in the amphitheater named for its own Jerry Garcia; an SF tradition tourists never find. Sat Aug 1 (daytime) · Jerry Garcia Amphitheater, McLaren Park, SF. Free, all ages. Limited parking — 29 Sunset or 54 Felton. Details good for: tie-dye, community vibes
Summer with the Symphony: Sutton Foster & Kelli O'Hara — two of Broadway's great leading ladies share the Davies stage. Tonight, Sat Jul 18, 7:30pm · Davies Symphony Hall, Civic Center, SF · from $30. Tickets good for: a dressed-up date night
ARTS
Free Shakespeare in the Park: "Antony & Cleopatra" — SF Shakes' 2026 touring production, directed by Katja Rivera; a tight 90 minutes, no intermission. Previews Sat–Sun Jul 25–26, then Aug 1–2 & Aug 8–9 (opening Aug 8), all 6pm at Memorial Park Amphitheater, Cupertino; Aug 15–16 it moves to Red Morton Park, Redwood City (SF parks in Sept). Free, all ages; bring low chairs. Schedule good for: golden-hour picnic theater
SF Arts & Crafts Show at the Presidio — open-air juried show of local artisans on the Main Post lawn. Sat–Sun Jul 18–19 & Sat Jul 25, 10am–6pm · 210 Lincoln Blvd, Presidio, SF. Free. Pair with the Tunnel Tops community arts market (Sat–Sun 10–4). Details
"Hairspray" at SF Playhouse — the big-hearted Broadway musical in an intimate 199-seat house; matinees and evenings most days through the summer. Ongoing; e.g. Sat 3pm shows · SF Playhouse, 450 Post St, Union Square · from ~$47. Tickets good for: fog-proof evenings
ChoreoFest — free outdoor dance festival — Bay Area companies perform on the Yerba Buena Gardens esplanade. Sat Jul 25 & Sat Aug 1 · Yerba Buena Gardens, SoMa, SF. Free. Details
NIGHTLIFE
Up Your Alley Fair (Dore Alley) — Folsom Street's rowdier, more local mid-summer leather & kink street fair; SoMa's longest-running warm-up to September's big one. Sun Jul 26, roughly 11am–6pm · Dore St & Folsom St, SoMa, SF. Free (donations at gates) · strictly adults-only. Details
★ SF Neo-Futurists: "The Infinite Wrench" — 30 plays in 60 minutes, order set by audience shouting, price set partly by a die roll; the city's best-kept late-night theater secret. Saturdays 9pm (incl. tonight) · 447 Minna St, SoMa, SF · $13–16 + die roll. Details good for: unpredictable second acts to a night out
Comedy in the Plaza — Brian Copeland hosts a free outdoor stand-up showcase; 400 seats, first-come. Sat Jul 18, 5–7pm · San Leandro Tech Campus, San Leandro (East Bay). Free. Details
FOOD · DRINK · FESTIVALS
FOOD
Italian Family Festa — the South Bay's 44th annual celebration of all things Italian: pasta, bocce, music, and nonna-level cannoli. Sat Jul 25 · History Park, San Jose. Free admission. Details good for: families, carb loading before marathon Sunday
★ Gravenstein Apple Fair — Sonoma County's "sweetest little fair" under the oaks of Ragle Ranch: heritage Gravenstein apples, cider tastings, farm demos, live bands. A west-county institution worth the drive. Sat–Sun Aug 8–9, 10am–6pm · Ragle Ranch Regional Park, Sebastopol. Ticketed (buy ahead online; kids discounted). Tickets good for: apple pie pilgrims, an OL-weekend escape hatch
Head West Marketplace at the Ferry Building — first-Sunday makers-and-food market along the Embarcadero waterfront. Sun Aug 2 · Ferry Building, SF. Free to browse. Details
DRINK
Sonoma County Fair — ten days of wine-country fair season: local wine and cider pours, fair food, free live music, the Wine Country Rodeo and Cirque Ma'Ceo, plus the famous Hall of Flowers show (preview Aug 6). Fri Aug 7 – Sun Aug 16 · Sonoma County Fairgrounds, Santa Rosa. Admission ticketed; check site for hours/prices. Details good for: a proper day trip north
Glen Park Night Market — a village-y evening market with local pours, food stalls, art and live music on a closed-off Kern Street. Tonight, Sat Jul 18, 3–8pm · Kern St & Diamond St, Glen Park, SF. Free entry. Glen Park BART is a block away. Details
FESTIVALS
Hayes Valley Carnival — the neighborhood's free annual carnival with Circus Bella performances, games and street food. Today, Sat Jul 18 · Hayes Valley (around Patricia's Green), SF. Free. Details
Bon Odori Street Dance Festival — Japantown's 95th annual obon street dance: everyone's invited into the circle, no experience needed. Sun Jul 26 · Japantown (Post St), SF. Free. Details good for: joining in, not just watching
52nd Nihonmachi Street Fair — Japantown's flagship summer fair: taiko, food booths, small-business vendors, and one of the city's oldest community-run festivals. Sat–Sun Aug 1–2, 11am–6pm · Post & Buchanan, Japantown, SF. Free. Details
33rd Pistahan Parade & Festival — the West Coast's largest celebration of Filipino food, dance and music; the lumpia and lechon alone justify the trip. Sat–Sun Aug 8–9; parade Sat 11am down Market St · Yerba Buena Gardens, SoMa, SF. Free. Details
Sausalito Art & Harbor Days — waterfront art showcase from the town's working artist community. Sat Jul 25 · Ferry Landing, Sausalito (Marin). Free. Take the Golden Gate Ferry and make it a car-free day. Details
OUTDOORS · SPORTS · ACTIVE
OUTDOORS
Presidio weekend double-header — the Arts & Crafts Show on the Main Post plus the Community Arts Market at Tunnel Tops (20+ BIPOC-led vendors, live performances), with Crissy Field a downhill stroll away. Sat–Sun Jul 18–19, 10am–4/6pm · Presidio, SF. Free. PresidiGo shuttle from downtown. Details good for: an easy no-plan Saturday
Marathon-weekend spectating — the SF Marathon course crosses the Golden Gate Bridge at dawn and finishes on the Embarcadero; the Fort Point and Ferry Building stretches are the best free show in town that morning. Sun Jul 26, from 5:15am · citywide, SF. Free to watch. Expect major road closures Sunday morning — Muni over driving. Course info

Golden State Valkyries — five home games this stretch — the WNBA's newest team has turned Chase Center into the Bay's loudest summer ticket. Sat Jul 18 5:30pm & Mon Jul 20 7pm vs Washington Mystics; Sun Aug 2 5:30pm & Tue Aug 4 7pm vs Toronto Tempo; Wed Aug 12 7pm vs Chicago Sky · Chase Center, Mission Bay, SF. Tickets via Ticketmaster; weekend games go fastest. T Third rail stops at the door. Schedule & tickets good for: post-work sports nights
Giants second-half baseball — Oracle Park hosts second-half homestands this window, but we couldn't confirm exact home dates from official schedule pages at press time — verify before you plan at mlb.com/giants/schedule.
ACTIVE
The San Francisco Marathon — run it — full 26.2 over the Golden Gate Bridge, plus two half options, a 10K and a 5K; one of the few marathons where fog is a feature. Sat–Sun Jul 25–26 (main races Sun, 5:15am first wave) · starts Embarcadero, SF. Paid registration — race week is here; register while entries last. Register good for: bucket-listers; the 5K is beginner-friendly
Global Climbing Day — free climbing, yoga and North Face giveaways for all comers. Sat Jul 18, 8am–8pm · Movement Gym, Sunnyvale (South Bay). Free. Details good for: trying climbing without commitment
