The Lead

Ligue 1 · Nice v PSG preview

Eleven straight wins, and the injured stars are almost back

PSG resume at Nice on Saturday chasing a 12th consecutive win. Dembélé, Marquinhos and Nuno Mendes are close, Doué is a doubt, and Roma and Lorient follow within eight days.

Eleven matches, eleven wins. When Paris Saint-Germain beat Troyes 1-0 at the Parc des Princes on November 7, they went into the international break top of Ligue 1 and top of the Champions League league phase, on the third-longest winning run in club history. Only streaks of 12 in 2015 and 16 in 2015-16 were longer, according to beIN SPORTS.

Luis Enrique put the run in context afterwards: it was the first time his side had won every match between two international breaks. It has come without a full squad. Since mid-October the coach has been without Ousmane Dembélé, Marquinhos and Nuno Mendes, and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia tore a knee ligament in the 4-2 win over Barcelona on October 20.

That is why Saturday's trip to Nice (8:15 AM Pacific) is more than a routine restart. It opens three matches in eight days: Nice away, Roma at the Parc des Princes in the Champions League on Wednesday, then Lorient at home on November 28. It is also the first real indication of how many of the injured are ready.

Who comes back, and how fast

Dembélé, Marquinhos and Mendes went back to individual work around November 6, Foot Mercato reported. On Wednesday, Le Parisien's reporting, relayed by Paris United and VIPSG, said the staff are optimistic that all three, plus Maghnes Akliouche (calf), can be in the squad at Nice. Starting places are not guaranteed, and the club has confirmed nothing.

The coach wants to watch the returning players in collective sessions before deciding, and the calendar argues for caution. Dembélé and Marquinhos were both hurt against Barcelona; Mendes went off at Strasbourg three days earlier. Rushing any of them into three games in eight days would risk a relapse just as the Champions League table tightens.

The new worry is Désiré Doué. He started France's 3-1 Nations League defeat to Italy on November 12, then felt tightness in his right hamstring in training and withdrew from the 2-1 win over Turkey on November 15. The French federation called it precautionary. VIPSG's report on Wednesday did not expect him to be involved at Nice.

“Eleven wins in a row, that's good. But what stands out for me is that we've managed to win all our matches between the two international breaks.”

Luis Enrique, after PSG 1-0 Troyes

The tactical questions

Without those players, Luis Enrique has rebuilt on the fly. Lucas Hernández has filled in at left-back, Senny Mayulu has led the line between Doué and Mika Godts or Akliouche, and Illia Zabarnyi has partnered Willian Pacho at centre-back. It has worked: Troyes was a seventh straight league win, although it took a set piece to break a stubborn low block.

The decision at Nice is whether to restore the old spine at once. Marquinhos beside Pacho would move Zabarnyi to the bench, even though his two league goals are already his best single-season league total. A fit Dembélé would push Mayulu wide or out. On the evidence of the coach's caution, a staggered return looks likelier: minutes off the bench on Saturday, more against Roma.

What is at stake

PSG lead Lille by two points and Paris FC by three after ten rounds; Nice sit sixth on 17. In Europe, four wins from four and a 20-5 goal record have them top of the league phase on goal difference from Real Madrid. A win at the Allianz Riviera would equal the 12-match run of 2015, with the club record of 16 in sight before Christmas.

Secondary feature: the set-piece machine

PSG have scored 15 set-piece goals in all competitions this season, the most of any club in Europe's top five leagues, beIN SPORTS reported after the Troyes win. It is a quiet shift for a side long defined by possession, and it has rescued the tight games.

The Troyes winner came from a quickly taken corner: Mayulu crossed and Zabarnyi headed in on 39 minutes, the only goal on a night of 73% possession and 836 completed passes. Achraf Hakimi's hat-trick at Le Havre on October 30 included a direct free kick, and Lucas Beraldo converted a penalty against Le Mans.

It matters on Saturday. One of the goals Nice conceded in their 2-1 defeat at Lyon was a header from a free kick, according to Nice Premium. Nice's height and organisation at dead balls may decide whether PSG need another set-piece breakthrough.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Nice: dangerous in form, leaky away from home

Sixth in Ligue 1 under a new coach, with four wins in five and a striker in a hot streak.

OGC Nice are sixth on 17 points (five wins, two draws, three defeats, goals 15-12) and arrive in good form: four wins in their last five, per Foot Mercato's results list. The only loss in that run was 2-1 at Lyon. Their latest result was a 2-0 win at Angers on November 8.

Olivier Pantaloni, formerly of Ajaccio and Lorient, is in his first season in charge on a two-year deal, per Ligue 1's season preview. We could not confirm his preferred formation from a reliable source; Hicham Boudaoui and Axel Witsel have played as a midfield pair.

The players to stop

Elye Wahi scored a hat-trick, including two penalties, in the 5-2 win over Rennes on October 30. Sofiane Diop scored after two minutes and created three chances at Angers, Sport News Africa reported, with Jonathan Clauss and Mohamed Amoura also among the goals in recent weeks.

The weak points are away form, with defeats at Paris FC (3-0) and Auxerre (1-0), and dead balls: Lyon's winning goal against them came from a free-kick header. Laurent Abergel is reported out with a knee injury (single-sourced). PSG won the last meeting 4-0 in Nice on March 21.

Transfer News

Marquinhos offered one more year as PSG move to lock down the core

No January signing is close. The real business is a wave of renewals, led by the captain.

RMC Sport reported on Wednesday that PSG have offered Marquinhos a one-year extension, taking his contract from June 2028 to June 2029, when he will be 35. Fabrice Hawkins described discussions as ongoing. The report was relayed by Foot Mercato and Paris Supporters. Certainty: offer made, talks under way.

A week earlier, Le Parisien had described only first contacts, with nothing decided partly because of his age. The RMC report is a step further on. The captain is currently recovering from a thigh injury.

Dembélé, Kvaratskhelia and Doué

Le Parisien's list of renewals in preparation, relayed by DailyMercato and Kick Football, has six names. Ousmane Dembélé (contract to 2028) has an agreement in principle to 2030 plus an option year, with details still open. Fan-account claims on Tuesday that he has already signed are unconfirmed, and the club has announced nothing.

Khvicha Kvaratskhelia (to 2029) is in advanced talks over an extension to 2032 with a significant raise, according to RMC via Le Quotidien du Sport; the terms agreed before his knee injury reportedly still stand. Désiré Doué (to 2029) is at the early-talks stage, and Quentin Ndjantou could extend to 2031.

David Boly: the academy contract that matters most

The 17-year-old right-back, who scored on his Champions League debut at Villarreal, is out of contract in June 2027. L'Équipe reports PSG have offered a five-year first professional deal described as financially very strong. Le Parisien adds Arsenal to Eintracht Frankfurt and Bayer Leverkusen in the race, via TOP Mercato. Certainty: offer made; rival interest concrete. He is reported to prefer staying.

Ferran Torres: a January exit already?

Spain's El Nacional reports PSG would listen to offers in January for Ferran Torres, signed from Barcelona in August for about 50 million euros, with Atlético Madrid interested and talks held. It is single-sourced, relayed by Foot01, and no French origin outlet has confirmed it. He leads PSG's Ligue 1 scoring with four goals but has not scored since September 20.

The Squad

A 4-3-3 held together by stand-ins

The injury list is finally emptying, with one long-term absence and one new doubt.

Availability

Out  Khvicha Kvaratskhelia: knee ligament tear on Oct 20, surgery in Doha on Oct 27; he estimated about six months out on a Twitch stream on Nov 14

Possible returns  Ousmane Dembélé (left thigh), Marquinhos (right thigh), Nuno Mendes (left thigh), Maghnes Akliouche (calf)

Doubt  Désiré Doué (right hamstring tightness on France duty)

Minor knocks (youth)  Dro Fernández (muscle discomfort with Spain U19, described as not serious); Alessandro Longoni (returned injured from Italy U19, single-sourced)

Suspended  None found

PSG have lined up in a 4-3-3 throughout the injury crisis. Lucas Hernández has played left-back, Mayulu has started at centre-forward, and Vitinha wore the armband against Troyes with Marquinhos out. Luis Enrique has rotated freely too: Lucas Digne, Dro Fernández and Ferran Torres all started the 5-1 win at Le Havre.

Kvaratskhelia was candid on his Twitch stream on November 14, per Paris United: he is struggling to accept that he cannot play. Hakimi phoned in during the stream, and the Georgian called him the best right-back of all time.

Form and set pieces

In Ligue 1, Ferran Torres leads PSG with four goals, ahead of Hakimi and Marquinhos on three. Zabarnyi and Vitinha are PSG's top-rated league players on FotMob (7.78 and 7.67). Beraldo is the scorer from his only penalty; Hakimi, Mendes and Kvaratskhelia have shared direct free kicks.

Training resumed on Tuesday with about a dozen players, before the internationals trickled back. Luis Enrique holds his pre-Nice press conference on Friday at 4:00 AM Pacific (13:00 in Paris), with the first 15 minutes of training open to media afterwards.

The Academy

Boly, Idder and a first France call for Mayulu

The Campus keeps feeding the first team, and the Youth League campaign hit its first bump.

David Boly, 17, scored on his Champions League debut in the 6-0 win at Villarreal on November 3. He was an unused substitute against Troyes and then joined France U19. His contract situation is covered in Transfer News.

Midfielder Younes Idder, 18, was named in a senior matchday squad for the first time against Troyes, AllezParis reported, after training with the first team. Senny Mayulu was promoted from France U21 to the senior squad on November 11 to replace Kylian Mbappé and came on for Doué against Italy on November 12.

Youth League and the loan army

PSG's U19s, coached by Jean-François Vulliez, began with a 6-1 win over Slovan Bratislava (Adam Ayari hat-trick), won 2-0 at Manchester City and drew 1-1 with Barcelona, then lost 3-0 at Villarreal on November 3. Roma visit the Campus next, on November 25.

On loan, Khalil Ayari is the standout: he scored a fine goal in Dunkerque's 3-0 win over Guingamp on November 7. Renato Marin has been first-choice goalkeeper at Nacional; Gabriel Moscardo (Espanyol) and Yoram Zagué (Pafos) have had mixed starts, per Foot Mercato, and Naoufel El Hannach is on a second spell at Montpellier. Dro Fernández scored for Spain U19 in an 8-0 win over Finland on November 17.

PSG Féminine

Champions League pressure at Chelsea tonight

Second in the league, but tenth in Europe and in need of a result in London.

Paulo César's side play Chelsea away in the Women's Champions League today at 12:00 PM Pacific (21:00 in Paris). PSG are tenth in the league phase with five points from four games, and UEFA's preview says they need a result to avoid a second straight league-phase exit. The result was not known at the time of writing.

Their European record: a 1-1 draw at Real Madrid, a 5-0 home win over OH Leuven, a late 1-1 draw at Manchester City, then a 2-1 home defeat to Bayern at the Parc des Princes on November 10, with Dudek's 65th-minute penalty not enough.

Domestic form

In the Arkema Première Ligue, PSG are second on 22 points from nine games, five behind OL Lyonnes, who have won all nine, per Stats Footofeminin. The one defeat was a 4-0 loss to Lyonnes at the Parc des Princes on October 17; a 1-1 draw with Paris FC followed on November 1.

Since then: a 4-0 win over Le Havre at the Campus on November 6 (Kanjinga, Feller on her 25th birthday, Mbock and a late academy goal), and a 2-0 win at Marseille on November 14, where Jourde scored on 76 minutes from Merveille Kanjinga's pass before a late own goal. Kanjinga, scorer of a 90+7 winner against Nantes, is the form player.

Injuries bite: Rasheedat Ajibade broke her fibula on international duty and Anaïs Ebayilin tore a knee ligament, per the club's medical update. Saint-Malo visit the Campus on Saturday (12:00 PM Pacific). Griedge Mbock and Sakina Samoura are named in France's squad for the late-November window (single-sourced).

Players Away

Hakimi stars, Doué limps out, Neves sets one up

More than a dozen Parisians were away during the November window. Here is how the senior players fared.

France, Spain and Portugal

France: Doué started the 3-1 defeat to Italy and was replaced by Mayulu on 77; Warren Zaïre-Emery was unused. Doué then missed the 2-1 win over Turkey with hamstring tightness, while Zaïre-Emery came on for the final eight minutes and France reached the Nations League quarter-finals.

Spain: Fabián Ruiz hit the post and Ferran Torres started in the 2-0 win in Czechia; Ruiz then started the 3-2 home defeat to England and went off at half-time, with Ferran unused. Portugal: Vitinha started and João Neves came on in a 4-1 home loss to Denmark; both started the 3-2 win in Wales, where Neves won the ball for Bruno Fernandes's opener.

Further afield

Morocco: Achraf Hakimi scored twice and set up another in a 5-1 win over Niger, then helped seal AFCON qualification with a 1-0 win in the return fixture, per psg.fr. Ukraine: Zabarnyi started a 1-0 defeat to Northern Ireland and a 1-0 win over Georgia.

Belgium: Godts came off the bench in a 2-1 win in Türkiye and again against Italy. Ecuador: Pacho started two 2-0 friendly wins and was rated 7/10 against Bolivia by Primicias. Russia: Matvey Safonov was at fault for both goals in a 3-2 win in Uzbekistan before a 2-0 win over Venezuela.

Off the Pitch

Stadium shortlist, a judge, and a TV tender

The new-stadium race tightens, and Nasser Al-Khelaifi spends time in a judge's office.

Three options remain for PSG's future home: an enlarged Parc des Princes, Poissy and Massy, with QSI expected to name a priority site before the end of 2026, per Le Parisien via Kick Football. This week a local group in Poissy launched a petition demanding public consultation, Massy said it would hold a binding referendum if chosen, and talks with the City of Paris over buying the Parc have resumed, Kick Football reported.

Nasser Al-Khelaifi was questioned on September 17 as an assisted witness, not a suspect, in the so-called digital army investigation, Le Parisien reported on Wednesday via Paris Supporters. He says he did not know of the club's 2018 contract with the agency involved. Separately, Le Figaro reports he is encouraging French federation president Philippe Diallo to stand for FIFA president (via Paris Supporters).

The Union of European Clubs filed an EU competition complaint on November 16 against the agreement that makes European Football Clubs, the body Al-Khelaifi chairs, UEFA's sole recognised club partner, Inside World Football reported. At home, the LFP has put Ligue 1 rights for 2027 to 2032 out to tender, with bids due December 3 (Maxifoot), and Bentley became PSG's official luxury car partner until 2028.
