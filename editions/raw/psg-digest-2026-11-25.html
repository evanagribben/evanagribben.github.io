The Lead

Without Doué and Kvaratskhelia, PSG test their 12-game run against Gasperini's Roma

Two torn ACLs in 32 days have taken both of Luis Enrique's first-choice wide forwards. Tonight's visit of the Serie A runners-up is the first real measure of what is left.

Twelve straight wins in all competitions. Four from four in the Champions League league phase, 20 goals scored in Europe and a three-point lead at the top of Ligue 1. Paris Saint-Germain have not lost since September 4. Tonight, at 12:00 PT, they welcome AS Roma to the Parc des Princes for matchday 5 with first place in the table already theirs on goal difference.

The run is intact. The squad is not. On Saturday in Nice, Désiré Doué went down in the 39th minute while challenging for the ball. PSG confirmed on Sunday a ruptured anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee, with surgery before the end of the month. He had scored from close range seven minutes earlier.

It is the second identical injury in a month. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia ruptured the ACL in his right knee in the 4-2 win over Barcelona on October 20. Goal reports that only the Georgian has a realistic chance of returning for the final weeks of the season in May.

How Luis Enrique replaces two wingers

Luis Enrique refused to dramatise it. "Are we going to start crying and complaining? No, that's not how a team operates," he told Tuesday's press conference. The answer, for now, is the players already there: Ousmane Dembélé, back from a thigh injury, plus Maghnes Akliouche, Senny Mayulu, Mika Godts and Ferran Torres.

“Are we going to start crying and complaining? No, that's not how a team operates.”

Luis Enrique, Nov 24

The French press broadly agrees on the XI. Le Parisien and L'Équipe both expect Safonov; Hakimi, Marquinhos, Pacho and Nuno Mendes; João Neves, Vitinha and Fabián Ruiz; Akliouche and Dembélé. The one split is on the left: Le Parisien names Mayulu, L'Équipe prefers Godts. Mayulu has four goals and two assists in his last five matches, per Goal.

The tactical puzzle: Gasperini's man-marking

Gian Piero Gasperini sets Roma up in a 3-4-2-1 with aggressive man-to-man marking across the pitch. That is a direct challenge to PSG's positional rotations, which rely on midfielders and forwards swapping zones to drag markers out of shape. Against a side that follows runners everywhere, the rotations either open huge spaces or get smothered.

Three duels will decide it. Vitinha and João Neves against Roma's midfield pair, likely Manu Koné and Bryan Cristante, decides whether PSG can play through the press. Hakimi against Roma's left wing-back decides whether PSG's best outlet stays free. And PSG's centre-backs must handle Donyell Malen, who scored four in Roma's 5-1 win at Parma last time out, per Forza Football.

Roma arrive in form: second in Serie A on 30 points, unbeaten after 12 league games, according to beIN Sports. Europe has been harder. They sit 20th in the league phase on four points, having lost 3-2 at Manchester United after leading 2-0. Gasperini's message on Tuesday, per Il Messaggero: "We can cause PSG problems."

What is at stake

A win would make it five from five and keep PSG on course for a top-eight finish and direct entry to the last 16. Avoiding defeat would also set a French record of 15 straight unbeaten Champions League games, beIN notes. It is the clubs' first competitive meeting. Luis Enrique coached Roma in 2011-12 and called that year "very special" to him.

The two-ACL month: what PSG actually lose

Doué and Kvaratskhelia were the two players who made PSG's attack unpredictable.

Before Nice, Doué had three goals and two assists in three Champions League games this season, per ESPN. beIN says the injury jeopardises his part in the Intercontinental Cup final in Qatar on December 16. The club has given no return date; ACL recoveries usually take six to nine months.

Luis Enrique said Doué was "sad and worried" and promised he "will come back twice as strong." The question for the coach is width. Dembélé has made only five starts this season, per Goal, and Torres has been inconsistent. Teenagers Quentin Ndjantou and Dro Fernández may get chances.

Player Ratings

Nice 2-3 PSG: the Digest's ratings

Ligue 1, matchday 11, Saturday November 21, Allianz Riviera. Mayulu (pen) and Doué turned an early deficit around before Torres won it late.

PSG fell behind on eight minutes when Mohamed Amoura scored from a Jonathan Clauss cross. Mayulu levelled from the spot after a VAR review for handball on 31 minutes and Doué made it 2-1 a minute later, assisted by Hakimi, per the ESPN report. Torres, on for the injured Doué, finished Dembélé's pass on 85 minutes. Abdi scored for Nice in the fifth minute of stoppage time.

PSG had 63 percent of the ball, 705 passes at 92 percent accuracy to Nice's 353, and 1.90 expected goals to 0.78, according to ESPN. A second PSG penalty before half-time was overturned because the ball had already gone out, Maxifoot reports. Luis Enrique: "I really liked the mentality today."

Scouting the Next Opponent

Scouting AS Roma: Malen, man-marking and a Serie A title push

Gasperini's side score freely in Italy but have stumbled in Europe.

Roma are second in Serie A on 30 points, two behind Lazio, unbeaten after 12 league games with 36 goals scored, according to beIN Sports. Their last five: a 5-1 win at Parma, 3-0 against Sassuolo, the 3-2 defeat at Manchester United, 5-2 at Udinese and 4-2 against Cagliari, per Forza Football.

Gian Piero Gasperini uses a 3-4-2-1 built on man-to-man duels all over the pitch. The probable XI per VIPSG's press round-up: Svilar; Mancini, Ndicka, Hermoso; Molina, Cristante, Koné, Wesley; Soulé, Dybala; Malen. La Roma reports Gasperini also considered Marten de Roon beside Koné with Cristante pushed forward.

The players to stop

Donyell Malen is the obvious one: four goals at Parma and, beIN says, 26 Serie A goals in calendar year 2026. Matías Soulé scored at Old Trafford. Paulo Dybala is back from a minor muscle tear and joined Gasperini at Tuesday's press conference.

Where Roma can be hurt

Man-marking leaves space behind when a duel is lost, and Roma conceded three times at Old Trafford after leading 2-0. Lorenzo Pellegrini and Devyne Rensch are out; Leonardo Balerdi travelled despite an ankle problem. PSG's quick switches of play and third-man runs are built to exploit exactly that. The clubs have never met in competition.

Transfer News

No panic buy: PSG weigh a smart January signing after the Doué blow

Luis Enrique rules out a six-month stopgap. The club's recruitment staff have a shortlist and one non-negotiable criterion: versatility.

Luis Enrique set the tone on Tuesday: "We don't have to go into the transfer market, and we won't sign a player for six months," per Goal. "We have a team." The coach's line matters because it rules out the obvious knee-jerk move, a short loan to fill a gap until Kvaratskhelia and Doué return.

RMC's Fabrice Hawkins reported this week that there is "no definitive decision" on the winter window. If PSG act, "the profiles are already identified and a shortlist exists," and versatility is "an important criterion." Very few names, he added, are realistically likely to arrive this winter. Certainty: no decision yet.

Le Parisien, via Sports Mole, says sporting director Luis Campos wants an attacker who is "more of a winger than a centre-forward," good in tight spaces and one against one, and that PSG will not overpay. Fabrizio Romano agrees: "PSG are not desperate," he said on Wednesday; any signing "has to be a smart signing."

The renewal wave

Canal+ reports Dembélé's extension is done, three more are set to follow and five more talks have started.

Canal+ reported on November 19 that Dembélé's extension to 2030 is done and that Kvaratskhelia, Doué and Safonov are set to extend to 2032, via Foot Mercato. Talks have opened with Marquinhos for one more year and with Zaïre-Emery, Vitinha, Hakimi and Nuno Mendes. None is official yet.

Safonov's case has a second source. Le Parisien reports the goalkeeper and the club are close on a deal carrying a big pay rise on his current contract, which runs to 2029, per Foot Mercato. Certainty: advanced talks.

Chevalier's January dilemma

Lucas Chevalier, signed from Lille for 40 million euros in August 2025, has not played a minute this season. Canal+ pundits said PSG could consider a loan or sale in January, but only if they find a top-level backup for Safonov, per Sports Mole. Canal+ also reported that Chevalier has not asked to leave, DailyMercato reports. Certainty: speculation.

The Squad

Dembélé returns, Mayulu rises, and the No. 1 question is settled

A crowded treatment room is finally emptying, just as two long-term absences arrive.

Four players came back for Nice: Ousmane Dembélé (thigh), Marquinhos, Nuno Mendes and Maghnes Akliouche, per VIPSG's squad report. Dembélé and Marquinhos had torn thigh muscles against Barcelona. For Roma, Lucas Digne and David Boly return to the 23-man squad, VIPSG reports, and L'Équipe expects Dembélé to start, per Footeo.

At Nice, PSG lined up in a 4-3-3 with Fabián Ruiz at the base of midfield, flanked by João Neves and Zaïre-Emery. Vitinha, Marquinhos, Pacho and Dembélé started on the bench, per the Fox Sports box score, a sign of how freely Luis Enrique rotates between league and Europe.

Form guide

Senny Mayulu, 20, is the in-form forward: four goals and two assists in his last five and a first senior France cap this month. Akliouche has settled quickly. Godts, signed from Ajax, is still adapting, Goal assesses. Fabián Ruiz has four goals and an assist in the Champions League, per Khelnow.

Goalkeeper and captaincy

Matvey Safonov has started every competitive match this season, Sports Mole reports, and is heading for a new contract. Marquinhos remains club captain, as psg.fr described him when presenting the Super Cup in September; Hakimi has worn the armband in his absence.

Off the pitch: stadium, FIFA politics and a sponsor exit

PSG have still not chosen a stadium future. Poissy residents are petitioning against a new ground, Massy would hold a binding referendum, and talks with the City of Paris over expanding the Parc to around 70,000 seats have resumed, Kick Football reports. The club aims to name a priority site by the end of autumn.

Nasser Al-Khelaïfi was a driving force behind French federation president Philippe Diallo's challenge to Gianni Infantino for the FIFA presidency, Le Figaro reports, via Yahoo. Diallo declared on November 17; the vote is in Rabat on March 18, 2027. Separately, crypto platform Bitpanda's partnership, worth about 5m euros a year, ends early by mutual agreement, per Sportune via Foot-sur7.

The Academy

Youth League night against Roma, and a 16-year-old keeper's first call

PSG's U19s mirror the senior fixture today at the Campus.

PSG's U19s host Roma in the UEFA Youth League today at the Campus PSG. They have seven points from four games (two wins, a draw and a defeat, the last a loss at Villarreal), which puts them 17th of 36, per FotMob. They travel to Aston Villa on December 8.

In the Championnat National U19, PSG were second in Group A on 18 points after beating Chartres 2-0 on October 31, per psg.fr. Goalkeeper Arthur Bar, 16, was called into the Ligue 1 squad against Le Mans in October as injury cover, Foot Mercato reported. Luis Enrique said in late October that academy players are finding first-team minutes harder to come by, per Maxifoot.

PSG Féminine

PSG Féminine: late heartbreak at Chelsea, a 16-year-old's breakthrough

Champions League qualification will go to the final matchday; the league chase with OL Lyonnes continues.

Paris Saint-Germain Féminine lost 1-0 at Chelsea in the Women's Champions League on November 18, Veerle Buurman scoring from a Lucy Bronze corner in the 87th minute, per psg.fr. Goalkeeper Katarzyna Kiedrzynek made several saves. Isabela went off early with what Flashscore called an adductor problem; the club has given no details, Flashscore reports.

PSG have five points from five league-phase games and must beat Juventus at the Parc des Princes on December 16, per psg.fr. They have won only one of their last 13 Champions League games, Gulf News notes.

Charlier steps up

Three days later, PSG beat Saint-Malo 1-0 at the Campus in the Arkema Première Ligue. Marie-Estella Lafontaine scored in first-half stoppage time, assisted by Alyssa Charlier, 16, who was named player of the match, per psg.fr. Charlier had become the youngest PSG player to appear in the Women's Champions League at Chelsea.

PSG are second on 25 points, five behind unbeaten OL Lyonnes, with Paris FC third on 22 with a game in hand, per Eurofootnews. Head coach Paulo César, the former U19 coach who replaced Fabrice Abriel, is under contract to June 2027. The next league game is at Toulouse on December 12.

Players Away

Players Away: Mayulu's France debut and Hakimi's double

Senny Mayulu won his first senior France cap as a late call-up by Zinedine Zidane with Kylian Mbappé ruled out, per PSG Post, and came on for Doué in the 77th minute of a 3-1 defeat to Italy at San Siro on November 12, per Ahram and psg.fr. Zaïre-Emery played eight minutes against Türkiye. Doué picked up a dental injury against Italy.

Achraf Hakimi scored twice from outside the box and assisted another as Morocco played Niger in AFCON qualifying, per PSG Post. For Portugal, João Neves set up Bruno Fernandes against Wales after a cameo in a 4-1 loss to Denmark; Vitinha started both games. Fabián Ruiz hit the post as Spain beat Czechia 2-0.

Elsewhere, Zabarnyi played right-back as Ukraine beat Georgia 1-0; Safonov kept a clean sheet in Russia's 2-0 win over Venezuela; Pacho helped Ecuador to two 2-0 friendly wins; Godts managed 12 minutes for Belgium. Ndjantou and Dro Fernández played youth internationals, all per PSG Post, a single source.

For the women, defender Griedge Mbock and Thiniba Samoura are in France's squad for friendlies against Venezuela on Friday and Morocco on December 5, per ParisFans; Sakina Karchaoui is injured.
