The Lead

Nine straight wins, a late-goal habit and PSV at the Bernabéu

Madrid needed a Bellingham equaliser and an 85th-minute Espí winner to beat Celta. Tuesday's visitors lead the Eredivisie but sit 24th in Europe, and Vinícius will start.

Nine wins in a row in all competitions, and Saturday night at the Bernabéu showed how thin the margin can be. Real Madrid trailed from the sixth minute, Kylian Mbappé put a penalty over the bar, and the winner only came on 85 minutes, from Carlos Espí, the striker who joined from Levante in the summer.

The final score was 2-1 against Celta Vigo, per the club report and LaLiga. It keeps Madrid second, three points behind Barcelona, with the Champions League back on Tuesday: PSV Eindhoven visit at 12:00 PM Pacific.

How Celta nearly did it

Pablo Durán scored on six minutes from Williot Swedberg's low cross, per VAVEL. Madrid had nine first-half shots but only one on target. On 36 minutes Javi Rodríguez was penalised for a pull on Mbappé; ESPN called the contact minimal, and Mbappé sent the kick over the bar, per ESPN.

Eduardo Camavinga then hit the post, and Thibaut Courtois kept the gap at one with saves from Swedberg and Durán. Celta goalkeeper Ionuț Radu was busy all night. The visitors went in 1-0 up.

Three changes, two goals

José Mourinho made a triple change on 57 minutes, sending on Vinícius Júnior, Marc Cucurella and Federico Valverde for Trent Alexander-Arnold, Álvaro Carreras and Bernardo Silva, per EFE. Vinícius was back in a league squad after his four-match domestic ban.

On 66 minutes Vinícius found Mbappé, who set up Jude Bellingham to equalise. On 85, Vinícius crossed from the left, Radu saved Espí's first header and Espí scored the rebound, his fourth goal of the season, per the club. Endrick, on for Yan Diomande at 71, forced another Radu save late on.

FotMob's numbers, via Sports Illustrated, show the pressure: 64 per cent possession, 24 shots, 7 on target and 3.76 expected goals (single-sourced). The crowd was 74,241. GOAL counts this as Madrid's third comeback of the season and 14 points won by goals after the 78th minute, per GOAL.

“Today's points are golden points.”
José Mourinho after Celta (translated from realmadrid.com)

Mourinho: golden points, frustrated coach

"Los puntos de hoy son puntos de oro," Mourinho said afterwards, per the club. He called Espí "un jugador de área," a penalty-box player, and gave the credit to the striker's former coach at Levante. He also admitted he was "un poco frustrado" because he always wants to play "más y mejor."

One remark explains Tuesday. In the club's English version, Mourinho said he would have made a sixth change, taking off Bellingham or Arda Güler for Aurélien Tchouaméni, "but that option doesn't exist." Both midfielders were spent, and Tchouaméni sat unused on his return from an adductor problem.

Elsewhere, Barcelona came from 2-0 down to beat Villarreal 3-2, per Claro Sports, and Atlético Madrid won 3-0 at Getafe, per LaGrada (single-sourced). Barcelona have 36 points, Madrid 33 and Atlético 31, per the LaLiga table.

PSV: top at home, 24th in Europe

Peter Bosz's side arrive top of the Eredivisie with 29 points from 13 games, one ahead of Ajax, after a 4-1 win at NEC on Saturday, per Flashscore and Sportschau. In the Champions League they have four points from four games and sit 24th, the last play-off place, per NBC Sports.

Madrid are second on 12 points, level with leaders PSG but behind on goal difference. Mourinho wants to stay in the top eight and avoid the play-off round. "Los 12 puntos nos dan tranquilidad," he said on Monday, adding that PSV are "worth more than their points," per the club.

Vinícius in, Endrick out

After Celta, Mourinho said Vinícius would be "titular el martes," a starter on Tuesday. Endrick will not be there: the club reported a muscle injury to the adductor in his left leg, and AS expects two to three weeks out, per GOAL and EFE.

Denzel Dumfries trained indoors on Monday with a right-ankle knock but is in the 23-man squad. Mourinho promised "un par de piernas frescas," a couple of fresh legs. FútbolFantasy projects Valverde at right-back, Tchouaméni back in midfield and Güler on the right, with Vinícius and Mbappé up front.

The battles that will decide it

PSV's danger sits wide. Mourinho singled out Filip Kostić and Ivan Perišić, per Voetbalzone, and Kostić scored at NEC. That puts Madrid's right side under the most pressure, whoever plays right-back. In midfield, Guus Til and Sven Mijnans, PSV's two leading scorers, arrive late in the box.

The opening for Madrid is PSV's defending. Against Club Brugge they conceded to Brugge's first real attack and again five minutes after equalising, and their captain admitted they "weren't well organised," per DutchNews. Mauro Júnior, that captain, is now injured for the rest of the year.

Mbappé has scored in all four Champions League games this season and has 17 goals in 17 matches overall, per the club. According to 365Scores, one more would be his 75th Champions League goal.

The clubs last met in 1989, when Madrid won a European Cup quarter-final 3-2 on aggregate after extra time in Madrid, a year after PSV knocked them out on their way to the trophy, per RSSSF. UEFA records Madrid unbeaten in four competitive meetings: one win, three draws, per UEFA.

The Boardroom

99 per cent for the budget, and a new round of Pérez v Tebas

Members approved every motion and welcomed Rafael Nadal to the board. Florentino Pérez used the stage to go after Negreira, the referees and LaLiga's president.

Real Madrid's members' representatives approved every item at Saturday's assemblies, per the club. The 2025/26 accounts passed 1,710 to 5 with 8 abstentions. The 2026/27 budget passed 1,705 to 5, and the 2027/28 membership fees 1,659 to 36. Rafael Nadal was ratified as a director 1,704 to 4, per the club.

The budget projects record revenue of 1.405 billion euros, including 154.8 million from player sales, and a record 707.1 million euros in wages plus amortisation, per Grada3. Net debt is projected at about 1.367 billion euros. Nadal called the role "una responsabilidad infinita," an enormous responsibility.

The speech

Florentino Pérez began with ownership: "el Real Madrid no se vende," Real Madrid is not for sale, and "no se venderá jamás," it never will be, per the full text. He also rejected opening membership to members' relatives and announced a fan loyalty scheme he said "no cambia en nada" what it means to be a member.

On Negreira he called the payments "extraordinariamente grave" and said he would not stop "hasta que se depuren todas las responsabilidades." He said referees have treated Madrid "como un enemigo" since the case broke, and called for a real overhaul of how refereeing is governed, per the club.

He said it was "indigno" that LaLiga president Javier Tebas had not resigned, per EFE. He also confirmed Madrid will return to the EFC (formerly the ECA) after February's agreement with UEFA, and announced the best UEFA club games will be free to watch in India from next season, per Infobae.

Tebas fires back

Tebas replied on X the same day that LaLiga would keep demanding the Negreira case be investigated "hasta el final," and asked why the club's concern had grown once Barcelona stopped being its Super League partner, per EFE. At the referees' gala he said he was studying legal action against Pérez, per Ahora.

Mourinho joined in on Monday, saying Villarreal's Santi Comesaña "tiene amnesia" for claiming referees favour Madrid and Barcelona, and asking him to leave Madrid out of the Negreira "saco," per Libertad Digital. Tebas answered that pressure on referees comes "clearly from Real Madrid TV," per GOAL.

In court this week

Barcelona and Pérez are due at a conciliation hearing in Madrid on Wednesday, November 25, over his May remarks on Negreira, per Mundo Deportivo via GOAL. Barcelona have threatened a criminal complaint if he does not retract. Earlier, on November 17, the Supreme Court refused LaLiga's appeal in the CVC case, a win for Madrid, per Crónica Global.

José Ángel Sánchez told the assembly that acoustic works at the Bernabéu are finished and there is "ningún impedimento legal" to concerts, which the club plans to resume soon, per Infobae. No date was given.

Scouting the Next Opponent

PSV Eindhoven: Eredivisie leaders who leak at the back

Peter Bosz's side score freely at home but have one win in four in Europe, and their captain is out.

Peter Bosz's PSV have won their last three games in all competitions, scoring 16 goals, including 7-0 against Willem II and 4-1 at NEC on Saturday, per Flashscore. Before that came a 1-1 draw with Feyenoord and a 2-1 home defeat by Club Brugge. They lead the Eredivisie by a point.

Europe has been harder: a 1-1 draw with Shakhtar, a 1-0 defeat at RB Leipzig, a 1-0 win at Porto settled by Guus Til, and the Brugge loss. That is four points and three goals scored, per NL Times and NBC Sports. Preview sites disagree on whether Bosz uses a 4-2-3-1 or a 4-3-3.

Who to stop

Sven Mijnans leads their scoring with nine, ahead of Til on eight and Ruben van Bommel on five, per statz.ai (single-sourced). Mourinho's warning was about the veterans out wide: Kostić, who scored at NEC, and Perišić, who took and scored a penalty there.

Mauro Júnior, the captain, was hurt at NEC and will not play again this year. Bosz confirmed: "No, he won't be playing," per PSV. Jerdy Schouten, Alassane Pléa, Sami Ouaissa and Adamo Nagalo are long-term absentees, per Omroep Brabant. Ricardo Pepi is just back and likely on the bench.

Bosz kept his plan to himself. "There are certainly chances here, but I won't tell you where," he said in Dutch to Omroep Brabant. Right-back Sergiño Dest called it "a very special match," per the club. Norway's Espen Eskås referees, per the club.

Transfer News

Three renewals in three days, and a FIFA stamp away from JJ Gabriel

Güler and Tchouaméni are tied to 2032 and Courtois to 2028. Valverde, Brahim and Bellingham are next in line.

Real Madrid announced a new contract for Arda Güler to 2032 on November 18, per the club, and one for Aurélien Tchouaméni, also to 2032, the next day, per the club. On November 20 Thibaut Courtois extended to June 30, 2028, per ESPN.

More are coming. The Athletic and AS report Federico Valverde has agreed a deal to 2031, per FootballTransfers. The Athletic also reports an agreement with Brahim Díaz to 2031, per El Nacional (single-sourced). Neither is official.

Jude Bellingham, under contract to 2029, is in advanced talks on a new deal with a significant pay rise, The Athletic reported on November 23, per Sports Mole. Certainty: advanced talks.

JJ Gabriel: agreed, waiting on FIFA

The 16-year-old forward said goodbye to Manchester United on November 17 and visited Valdebebas the next day, per GOAL. Fabrizio Romano said on November 18 that he "will sign for Real Madrid in the coming days" and that the deal is ready, per FootballTransfers.

AS reports full agreement on a three-year contract, with only FIFA's approval for the transfer of a minor still needed, per GOAL. He can join Castilla when the Primera Federación window opens in January. Certainty: personal terms agreed; not official.

Endrick: a 70 million euro offer, and a veto

Cadena SER's Antón Meana reported on Monday that AC Milan have made a formal offer of up to 70 million euros for Endrick, per El Desmarque and GOAL. Mourinho does not want him to leave, Meana said, because no forward of that level would arrive in January, per Defensa Central.

Meana added that Endrick's representatives are due in Madrid this week. The new adductor injury, which will keep him out for two to three weeks, complicates any January move. Certainty: formal offer reported; Madrid have not accepted.

The Squad

Rotation, a stretched midfield and Vinícius's second chance

Endrick joins the injury list, Tchouaméni is back, and Mourinho is managing his left wing in public.

Real Madrid play three times in eight days: PSV on Tuesday, Alavés on Saturday and Real Sociedad away on Wednesday, December 2, per the club calendar. Mourinho said three days between games is fine but "dos días es duro," two is hard, per the club.

Asked about players threatening to strike over the calendar, he said any such player could be offered 50 per cent less pay, per SB Nation.

The Vinícius question

Before Celta, Mourinho was pointed. He said it did not matter whether Vinícius Júnior started, sat on the bench or watched from the stands: "Nadie tiene que jugar por decreto," nobody plays by decree, per El Debate. He added that Vinícius perhaps should not have said what he did about his form while on Brazil duty.

Then Vinícius changed the Celta game in half an hour. On Monday Mourinho said he will return to "su potencial máximo" and that there is "no hay presión" from him, per the club. Yan Diomande, who started all four games of the ban, had a quieter night and came off at 71.

Fitness and form

Tchouaméni has recovered from the adductor problem he picked up with France and is in the PSV squad. Endrick is the new absentee. Rodrygo (knee) and Raúl Asencio (stress fracture of the tibia) remain long-term cases, and Ferland Mendy, back in training, is not registered for the Champions League, per EFE.

Mbappé's run of scoring in seven straight club games ended against Celta, but he has 17 goals in 17 games (13 in the league, 4 in Europe), per the club.

La Fábrica

A bad night for Castilla, a perfect run for the Juvenil A

Real Murcia won at the Di Stéfano, the under-19s are already through in the Youth League, and Espí keeps scoring for the first team.

Real Madrid Castilla lost 2-0 at home to Real Murcia on Friday night. David Flakus scored both goals, on 26 and 37 minutes, per the club report and Murcia Plaza. Julián López de Lerma's side had been unbeaten in three.

The defeat drops Castilla to eighth in Primera Federación Group 2 with 19 points from 13 games, per FotMob (single-sourced). They visit Rayo Majadahonda on Sunday, November 29, at 16:00 in Madrid, per ADSLZone (single-sourced).

Juvenil A

Álvaro López's under-19s lead División de Honor Group 5 with 28 points from 10 games, per the club. In the UEFA Youth League they have won all four games, scoring 13, and top the league phase. They host PSV on Tuesday at 16:00 in Madrid (7:00 AM Pacific), live on Realmadrid TV, per the club.

The first-team door

Castilla goalkeeper Mestre and midfielder Thiago Pitarch are in Mourinho's squad for PSV, alongside Espí, per the club. Pitarch played the last 20 minutes and Espí 70 of Spain under-21s' 0-0 friendly in Albania on November 17, per the club.

On loan

Franco Mastantuono came on at half-time for Argentina against Costa Rica on November 17 and scored twice in a 7-0 win, per ESPN. Back at Fiorentina it went badly: he was replaced on 67 minutes of a 3-0 defeat at Monza and rated jointly the side's worst at 4.5 by Calciomercato.it.

 
Femenino

Bruun's late double, then five at Granada

Real Madrid Femenino are unbeaten in Europe and four points off Barcelona in Liga F, with the international break next.

Real Madrid Femenino beat Inter 3-1 in the Women's Champions League on November 18, per the club report. Lineth Beerensteyn scored on 28 minutes, Inter equalised before half-time, and Inter's Vilhjálmsdóttir was sent off after a VAR review. Substitute Signe Bruun won it with goals on 85 and 90+4, the second a penalty.

Pau Quesada's side are sixth in the league phase with nine points (two wins, three draws), per the club. Barcelona and Lyon lead on 13. Madrid's last league-phase game is at Roma on December 16.

Granada 1-5 Real Madrid

On Sunday Madrid fell behind at Granada to a Raquel Gil goal on 34 minutes, then scored five. Keukelaar equalised on 45, Bruun put Madrid ahead in first-half stoppage time, Keukelaar scored again on 46, then Caruso and Athenea del Castillo added more, per the club and OKDiario.

Madrid are second in Liga F with 27 points from 12 games, four behind Barcelona and two ahead of Atlético Madrid, per Liga F. There are no club games until Madrid CFF visit the Di Stéfano on Tuesday, December 8, per the club.

International duty

Silvia Cristóbal, María Méndez and Eva Navarro are in Sonia Bermúdez's Spain squad for friendlies against Chile in León on November 28 and Venezuela in A Coruña on December 4, per EFE and DAZN. Cristóbal could make her debut. Other national squads with Madrid players had not been confirmed at press time.

Valverde and Mastantuono score, Brahim sets up a winner

The last games of the November window, from Montevideo to Tirana.
