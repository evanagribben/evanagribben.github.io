The Lead

La Liga, Matchday 12

Fermín's double at the Metropolitano sends Barça into the break three points clear

Atlético were the better side for half an hour. Then Pedri found Fermín, Lamine Yamal found him again, and Hansi Flick's leaders passed the hardest test of their season.

For half an hour on Sunday night, Barcelona were second best at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano. Atlético Madrid led inside ten minutes, hit the crossbar and made Barça's goalkeeper work twice. Then the game turned on one deflected shot, and by the final whistle Barça were going into the international break three points clear at the top of La Liga.

The opener came on ten minutes: Giuliano Simeone scored from Julián Álvarez's cross, and Pubill headed against the bar soon after. ESPN's Sam Marsden called Atlético "the better side in the first half", and Joan García needed a one-on-one save from Álvarez as early as the sixth minute.

Hansi Flick admitted afterwards that his side had been too far from their opponents in the first half to press. It showed. Barça used four different back lines over the evening, according to ESPN, and the right side, where academy graduate Brian Fariñas was playing out of position at full-back, was where Atlético kept coming.

“We have the quality to win these matches.”

Hansi Flick, after the 2-1 win at Atlético (ESPN)

The turn

The equaliser arrived on the stroke of half-time. Pedri found Fermín López, whose shot took a deflection off Pubill and went in. Flick's verdict was blunt: "The 1-1 was very important before the break." Barça did not trail again.

At half-time Flick took Fariñas off and sent on Frenkie de Jong at right-back. Vavel rated his 45 minutes a 7.5, the best mark of any substitute, and Barça took hold of the ball. Andreas Christensen, back from injury, replaced João Cancelo after an hour, with Gerard Martín moving to left-back.

The winner, after 66 minutes, was a Lamine Yamal special: a through ball that sent Fermín clear, and a first-time finish. "That pass was half a goal," Fermín said afterwards. It was his second of the night, his eighth of the season in all competitions and it came in his 100th La Liga appearance.

What it means

The table tells the story. Barça have 33 points from 12 games, three more than Real Madrid and five more than Atlético. They have scored 48 and conceded 13, and the only league defeat is still the 2-1 Clásico loss at Spotify Camp Nou on October 25. Asked to rate the run into the break, Flick said "a 10" out of 10.

There were warnings, too. Álvarez lobbed over the bar when clean through early in the second half, and Yamal could not connect with Anthony Gordon's cross for a third late on. Cancelo, so good against Aston Villa five days earlier, lost Giuliano Simeone for the opener, per ESPN. The club's search for a January defender did not come from nowhere.

Diego Simeone said his side were "not as clinical" as usual. Fermín went further: "Last year they beat us in the Cup and the Champions League," he told reporters. This time Barça won the duel that mattered, without the injured Rodri, Jules Koundé and Dani Olmo, and with four academy players called up to train with the first team beforehand.

Secondary feature

Cubarsí becomes Barça's fifth Golden Boy

A World Cup winner at 19, the centre-back took 475 of a possible 500 points.

Pau Cubarsí has won the 2026 Golden Boy, the Tuttosport award for Europe's best young player. The Italian newspaper announced it on Monday, and Football España reports the 19-year-old centre-back took 475 of a possible 500 points in a landslide.

He is the fifth Barcelona winner, after Lionel Messi, Pedri (2021), Gavi (2022) and Lamine Yamal (2024), and he succeeds Paris Saint-Germain's Désiré Doué. Corriere dello Sport, cited by Football España, says he is only the second defender to win it.

The award caps an extraordinary year. Cubarsí started all eight of Spain's games as they won the World Cup, beating Argentina 1-0 after extra time in the final, and was named the tournament's best young player. "Vamos a por más," he said in his thank-you message: let's go for more.

The ceremony is in Turin on December 14, where Joan Laporta will also collect the organisers' Best President award.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Next: Sunday, November 22

Villarreal: dangerous on the ball, fragile when it matters

Iñigo Pérez's side dominate games and still lose them. Barça should expect possession to be contested.

Villarreal arrive at Spotify Camp Nou eighth in La Liga with 15 points from 12 games (four wins, three draws, five defeats, goal difference +2). Iñigo Pérez, appointed in June after his success at Rayo, has them playing a front-footed 4-2-3-1, but the results have been uneven.

Their last outing was a 2-0 win over Getafe on November 8 in which they had 73 per cent of the ball, 22 shots and 2.38 xG, yet needed until the 80th minute to score (Football España, FotMob). Before that they lost 6-0 at home to Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League, where they sit 28th on three points.

Who to stop

Pape Gueye was FotMob's player of the match against Getafe and anchors the midfield two. Attacking left-back Carlos Romero scored the second goal, and Tajon Buchanan, who scored the first, had four shots. Ayoze Pérez and Nicolas Pépé have been used as impact substitutes.

Defensively, the PSG result and a 4-3 defeat at Liverpool show a side that can be opened up. Barça won 2-0 at La Cerámica in the last meeting, on December 21, 2025, with goals from Raphinha and Yamal (report). One caveat: Football España reported an interim, Sergio García, on the bench for the October defeat to Espanyol, and the reason was not clear in reports.

Transfer News

The big thread

The hunt for a left-footed centre-back

Flick wants one in January. The salary limit decides whether he gets one.

Barcelona left the summer window without a left-footed centre-back, and a run of injuries at the back has made the gap obvious. Before the Alavés game, Hansi Flick was direct: "If it is possible, we will do it" (AfricaSoccer, citing the press conference).

The name that keeps coming up as realistic is Lisandro Martínez. The Manchester United defender, 28, has a contract that ends in June 2027, and Mundo Deportivo and TEAMtalk report concrete interest (SoccerNews; TEAMtalk). Status: concrete interest. No bid has been reported.

Sport reported that Deco had sounded out Piero Hincapié's camp at Arsenal, with a six-month loan floated (Vistazo, citing Sport). Fabrizio Romano poured cold water on it on November 6: Arsenal have "no intention" of changing their back four, and "I don't see this one happening" (TEAMtalk). Status: link, cooling.

The longer game

RB Leipzig's Castello Lukeba, 23 and left-footed, is a summer 2027 idea. His agency, which also represents Jules Koundé, met Deco in late October, according to Sport citing Sky Germany (AfricaSoccer). His release clause is reported at €80m, falling to €65m. No bid has been reported. Status: concrete interest, summer.

Mundo Deportivo's longer list also includes Betis's Natan, plus Josko Gvardiol and Alessandro Bastoni, whom The Athletic also mentions (SI). The last two are seen as unrealistic for January. Romano also ruled out Chelsea's Levi Colwill: "absolutely nothing is happening".

The money

A €3m to €4m margin and a €60m choice

La Liga has set Barça's salary limit at €582.7m, against a budget of €636m for squad wages and amortisation, and the club sits under the 1:1 rule, so it can spend one euro for every euro it saves or earns. Crónica Global puts the spare margin at about €3m to €4m and reports that Deco is slowing some renewals to protect it (Crónica Global, Nov 10).

That leaves three routes for a January defender: a free agent, a loan with wages covered, or a sale. Sport has also reported, via Barça Universal in September, that the club holds about €60m of allowance it can either use in January or carry into 2027-28, when Camp Nou roof works are expected to cost matchday income (Barça Universal). The request to carry it over must be made before the window opens.

Outgoing

Balde accepts he must leave

Alejandro Balde, 23, has accepted that he must move on, according to The Athletic (via Sports Mole). He had five appearances and no starts when that was reported, and was unused at the Metropolitano. Manchester United have been interested since the summer. Romano says a January exit is "not something that we have to rule out 100 percent". Status: club open to a sale; no fee reported.

Ronald Araújo, on a season loan at Liverpool with a reported €55m purchase option (Barça Universal), could also become money for January: TEAMtalk reports early talks about making the deal permanent sooner, with Liverpool thinking around £40m to £45m (The Hard Tackle). This is single-sourced. Status: early talks.

Renewals

Raphinha commits to 2030

The captain Raphinha signed a new contract to June 30, 2030, at a ceremony with Joan Laporta on Thursday, November 5 (club announcement; SoccerNews). Status: official. He has 19 goals this season, 15 of them in La Liga.

Mundo Deportivo reports that Marc Bernal and Brian Fariñas have agreed extensions to 2031, to be signed after the international break, and that Gerard Martín will get a new offer (as relayed by Fichajes.net). Status: agreed, not official. Crónica Global says Gerard Martín's renewal has been pushed back for salary-limit reasons.

The Squad

Rodri's hamstring and the right-back puzzle

Flick's 4-2-3-1 is scoring for fun. The questions are at the back and in the holding role.

Rodri asked to come off at half-time against Aston Villa, and the club confirmed on November 4 that tests showed "a minor injury to his right hamstring", adding that "his exact recovery time will depend on how the injury develops" (beIN Sports). Reports suggest up to a month; that is not the club's figure. He also misses Spain's Nations League games.

It matters because Barça's summer signing had become the side's metronome. Marc Bernal started in his place at Atlético and played 74 minutes before Eric García replaced him. The two-week break gives Rodri time, but Villarreal on November 22 looks tight.

The right side

Right-back is the live selection question. With Jules Koundé (ankle) out, Flick has trusted La Masia's Brian Fariñas, a midfielder by trade, for three straight starts. He was hooked at half-time on Sunday, and Frenkie de Jong finished the game there. Cancelo's defending on the left was also questioned by ESPN after the Atlético opener.

Further forward, the numbers are outstanding. Raphinha leads La Liga with 15 goals, two clear of Kylian Mbappé, and Lamine Yamal leads the league in assists (8) and in goals plus assists (18), per SoccerNews. Fermín has seven league goals and five assists. Raphinha wears the armband, with Pedri, Eric García, De Jong and Yamal the other captains.

Set pieces

One defensive detail to watch is the dead ball: Pubill headed against the bar from a set play at the Metropolitano, one of the chances Atlético created in their strong first half-hour.

La Masia

Fariñas's fast lane, and Barça Atlètic stay top

A debutant at right-back, four youngsters on the bench at the Metropolitano and a 3-0 win in Manresa.

Brian Fariñas, 20, made his La Liga debut against Alavés on October 31 (club), then started against Aston Villa (his Champions League debut) and at Atlético. Ara reports that Flick blocked a loan to Girona (Ara). Sunday's half-time withdrawal was a reminder that he is learning a new position at the top level.

Goalkeeper Max Bonfill, Álex Campos, Ebrima Tunkara and Hamza Abdelkarim were called up to work with the first team before the Atlético game (Crónica Global). None made a debut.

Barça Atlètic

Juliano Belletti's side won 3-0 at CE Manresa on Sunday to stay top of their Segunda Federación group (club report). Orian Goren scored his first goal for the team after three minutes and Pedro Rodríguez converted a penalty on eight. The third, in stoppage time, is credited by the club as an own goal; Betevé gives it to Álex González.

Campos and Tunkara missed the game because they were with the first team. The club singled out Orian Goren, Pedro Rodríguez, goalkeeper Iker Rodríguez and Aziz. Next, per FotMob's fixture list: Girona B at home on Saturday, November 14.

Juvenil A

Pol Planas's Under-19s drew 3-3 with Aston Villa in the UEFA Youth League on November 3, rescued by Iu Martínez's 89th-minute equaliser after Tunkara and Joni Hernández had scored (club report). They have five points from 12 with Sabah and Manchester City to come. In the División de Honor they won 3-1 at Sant Andreu and sit second, five points behind Espanyol.

Out on loan

Ronald Araújo started in Liverpool's 3-1 win at Crystal Palace on Sunday (ESPN). He was Liverpool's player of the month for October (Barça Universal).

Marc Casadó returned from a broken collarbone as a 72nd-minute substitute in Deportivo's 0-0 draw at Espanyol, where home fans booed him (La Grada). Marc-André ter Stegen was in goal as Ajax drew 2-2 at Twente (Ajax). Diego Kochen (Lyngby) has been called up by the USMNT; Aron Yaakobishvili remains Almería's backup.

Barça Femení

Five past Madrid CFF, and now Arsenal at Camp Nou

The Liga F leaders are four points clear and top of their Champions League table, but Kerolin's injury bites.

Barça Femení beat Madrid CFF 5-1 at the Estadi Johan Cruyff on Sunday (Liga F; Reinas del Balón). Esmee Brugts opened the scoring, Ewa Pajor added a second in first-half stoppage time, and substitutes Carla Julià and Patri Guijarro scored before Clàudia Pina made it five. Inès Konan replied late for the visitors.

Pina has now scored in back-to-back games, and Julià had a goal and an assist. Pere Romeu's side have 27 points from ten games, four clear of Real Madrid (table).

Arsenal, a final rematch

Thursday's Champions League game against Arsenal is at Spotify Camp Nou, kickoff 9:45 AM Pacific. It was moved from Wednesday because of a general strike in Spain (Arseblog). Barça top their table with nine points from three games; Arsenal have seven (CBS Sports). It is a rematch of the 2025 final and Mariona Caldentey's return.

Injuries

Kerolin, who scored twice against Badalona and had an assist on Sunday, has a calf injury and is out for about four weeks, missing Arsenal (Crónica Global). Kika Nazareth has a thigh injury, while Irene Paredes and Vicky López are nearing returns (Crónica Global).

The summer turnover still frames everything: Alexia Putellas and Mapi León left for London City, Ona Batlle for Arsenal and Salma Paralluelo for Lyon, while Kerolin arrived from Manchester City. After Arsenal, Barça visit Deportivo Abanca at Riazor on Sunday, kickoff 9:00 AM Pacific.
