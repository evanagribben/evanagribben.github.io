The Lead

Atlético's November gauntlet starts at Getafe, with history in reach and bodies short

Two home defeats sent the team into the break on a sour note. The run that follows, four games in eleven days, will decide whether that was a wobble or a warning.

TTwo home defeats in five days sent Atlético into the November break: 0-2 to Bayern Munich in the Champions League and 1-2 to Barcelona in La Liga. They come out of it facing the most compressed stretch of their autumn. Getafe away on Sunday, Viking at the Metropolitano on Wednesday, Elche away on Saturday, then Barcelona at Spotify Camp Nou on December 2.

The table is not alarming, but it is unforgiving. Diego Simeone's side are third on 28 points, five behind Barcelona and two behind Real Madrid, with a record of nine wins, one draw and two defeats. In Europe the picture is murkier: 23rd of 36 on four points after four matches, inside the play-off band but level on points with the clubs either side of the cut.

What went wrong against Barcelona

The Barcelona defeat stung because Atlético were the better side for half an hour. Giuliano Simeone scored inside ten minutes from a low Julián Álvarez cross, and by the break Atlético had 1.38 expected goals from seven shots, according to All Football. Then Fermín López equalised on 45 minutes and won it on 66. In the second half Atlético managed two shots, neither on target.

That fade is the tactical thread to pull. With 37 percent of the ball (101greatgoals), a front two of Álvarez and Lee Kang-in had to press and then run the length of the pitch. Pubill headed against the bar, Álvarez lobbed against it and Hancko hit it from a corner. Simeone said he was "completely satisfied" with the performance (as translated), adding that the team "lacked the decisive final touch".

"I am completely satisfied with the players' performance... we lacked the decisive final touch."

Diego Simeone after the Barcelona defeat (translated)

The break's bill

The break has thinned his options further. Ademola Lookman went off in first-half stoppage time of Nigeria's 3-1 win over Tanzania on November 13 with what Nigeria's media officer called a knee ligament sprain. First estimates range from about two weeks (FútbolFantasy) to three, and he was due for club tests in Madrid on Tuesday.

Marc Pubill lasted 20 minutes of Spain's 3-2 defeat to England at the Metropolitano before Unai Simón landed on him at a corner. Tests found a mild left ankle sprain, and he did not train on grass on Tuesday. Cuti Romero, out since mid-October with an adductor problem, is still working apart. Pubill has been the stand-in centre-back, so the two absences overlap badly.

A record and a ranking

There is a record waiting, too. The 2-0 win at Levante on October 31 was Simeone's 344th La Liga victory, level with Luis Aragonés for the most by any coach in the competition. He has needed 565 matches; Aragonés took about 757. Barcelona denied him the 345th. Getafe, who have lost all six of their league away games but are unbeaten in six at home, are next in line.

The European arithmetic sharpens the week. Viking arrive on November 25 also on four points, in 22nd. Win that and Atlético climb past a direct rival with PSV, Bodø/Glimt and Fenerbahçe still to play. Drop points across the four games and the conversation turns from a title chase to damage limitation before Christmas.

Also this week

Simeone renewal: talks toward 2029 reported, nothing signed

His contract ends in June 2027. Spanish and Italian outlets say talks have started; nothing is signed.

Diego Simeone's current deal runs to June 30, 2027. Radio Estadio (Onda Cero) reported after the Barcelona game that he is close to renewing until 2029, with an announcement expected within a few months.

TuttoMercatoWeb added that contacts have begun and a meeting is planned for December, with 2029 gaining ground. Neither Marca, AS nor Relevo has confirmed it.

Certainty: talks reported, not agreed. He spent part of the break in Argentina on a personal matter with the club's permission, while assistant Gabi Fernández ran sessions with six first-team players. He was back on the grass on Tuesday.

 

Scouting the Next Opponent

Getafe: fortress Coliseum, no points on the road

José Bordalás's side are 15th and two points above the drop, but nobody has won at their ground in La Liga this season.

Getafe sit 15th on 12 points from three wins, three draws and six defeats, with eight goals scored and 18 conceded, per the official La Liga results. They have lost all six league away games without scoring, conceding 14. At the Coliseum they are unbeaten in six: three wins, three draws, every one of their eight goals, and only four conceded.

José Bordalás is still in charge, and the profile is familiar: a compact block, duels and set pieces. A preseason guide had him favouring a back five; recent line-ups were not confirmed. After the 2-0 loss at Villarreal on November 8 he said the team "lost our shape" late on and that players short of a pre-season were paying for it.

Who to stop

The men to stop: Martín Satriano leads them with three league goals, Enes Ünal has two and takes penalties, and Ramón Terrats delivers their dead balls. Goalkeeper David Soria has been their best-rated player on FotMob. Christantus Uche is out until May with knee ligament and meniscus damage. Juanmi Jiménez (knee) is listed out; Zaid Romero is cleared to return.

Their schedule has been crowded by Conference League football: a 0-1 home loss to Brighton on November 5 came between a Copa del Rey win and the Villarreal defeat. Atlético have won four of the last five meetings, including 1-0 at the Metropolitano in March and 1-0 at the Coliseum in November 2025.

Transfer News

The winter priority: a left-back to cover Grimaldo

Marca says Mateu Alemany's main January job is a natural deputy on the left. Villarreal's Sergi Cardona is the name in the frame.

With Ruggeri reportedly sold to Aston Villa and a Tagliafico deal collapsed, Atlético were left without a specialist behind Álex Grimaldo. Dani Martínez has covered. Marca, as relayed by El Desmarque, reports that a left-back is the winter priority.

Sergi Cardona of Villarreal is named as an option. Certainty: a reported priority with a named target, no approach confirmed. Single-sourced to Marca, which we could not read directly.

Sørloth and Juventus: interest, no offer yet

Gazzetta dello Sport says Luciano Spalletti wants the Norwegian in January. Atlético would want a convincing bid.

Alexander Sørloth has yet to score this season after a complicated August muscle injury, notes Esto es Atleti. Juventus see him as their No. 1 option for January, according to Gazzetta dello Sport, relayed by ESPN.

No offer has been reported, and Spanish outlets say Atlético would want a convincing proposal before selling. Certainty: concrete interest from the buyer. He returned to Norway's squad this window as an unused substitute against Wales.

Zubimendi: a story the player himself shut down

Links in Sport and on Spanish radio met denials from Zubimendi and from Fabrizio Romano.

Diario Sport linked Arsenal's Martín Zubimendi with Atlético, and Spanish radio floated a swap involving Julián Álvarez. Álex Baena added fuel by saying on Cadena SER he would love Zubimendi as a team-mate.

Zubimendi told Marca he had not "wasted any energy on it", per ESPN. Fabrizio Romano said there were no talks, no swap and no €65m to €80m January spend. Certainty: link, denied.

The 2027 contract list: Koke, Llorente and a loan clause

Three senior deals expire next June, and a key renewal is still on the table.

Esto es Atleti's run-down lists Koke, 34, whose extension of at least a year the outlet expects but which is not agreed. Marcos Llorente, 31, has no talks reported. Jonathan David is on loan from Juventus with a reported €25m option that looks unlikely to be used.

Marc Pubill's renewal is the one to watch. Matteo Moretto reported in October that a first offer had been made and a second was coming. Nothing has moved publicly since.

The Squad

A back line rebuilt on the fly, and a front line still waiting

Simeone has the internationals back, but not all of them in one piece.

How Simeone set up

Against Barcelona Atlético lined up in a 4-4-2: Oblak; Llorente at right-back, Pubill and Hancko in the middle, Grimaldo on the left; Giuliano, Barrios, Koke and Baena across midfield; Lee Kang-in alongside Julián Álvarez. Sørloth, Hjulmand, Cardoso and David came off the bench.

Marcos Llorente keeps toggling between midfield (against Bayern) and right-back. With Romero out, Pubill has been the centre-back next to Hancko. That is the partnership the injuries now threaten.

The forwards' problem

Álex Baena leads the club with five goals and four assists in all competitions, per Infoatleti. The four senior forwards, Álvarez, David, Sørloth and Lookman, have seven between them. Álvarez has two, both from the Stuttgart and Deportivo games. David scored in three straight matches, then faded. Sørloth has none.

Simeone's public line on Álvarez after Barcelona was patient: "he has our confidence... we will wait for him." He did score for Argentina against Uruguay on November 13.

The Academy

Torres's Madrileño edge Teruel; the Juvenil A keep rolling in Europe

A late winner for the reserves, ten Youth League points for the under-19s and a new affiliate in Ecuador.

Atlético Madrileño

Fernando Torres's reserve side beat Teruel 1-0 on Sunday, David García scoring in the 81st minute, per FotMob. It followed a 3-2 defeat at CE Europa and a 1-1 draw with Alcorcón.

That leaves the Madrileño on 14 points from 12 games in Primera Federación Group 2 (three wins, five draws, four defeats), around 14th in one outlet's table. Zaragoza lead on 27. Next: away at UE Sant Andreu on Saturday.

Juvenil A

The under-19s are second in División de Honor Group 5 on 19 points, six behind Real Madrid. In the UEFA Youth League they have ten points from four games after a 4-1 win over Bayern Munich on November 3, with Gabriel Obama scoring twice, per Futbol Total. They host Viking on November 25.

Loans and the new Ecuador link

On loan at Hajduk Split, Carlos Martín has three goals and an assist in seven Croatian league games, including two goals against Gorica on October 31. Javier Boñar, sold to Girona this summer with Atlético keeping half his rights, has eight league appearances. Miguel Cubo, 18, who scored for the first team in May, has renewed to 2029.

On November 13 Atlético completed the purchase of 50% of Atlético FC of Guayaquil, newly promoted to Ecuador's top flight for 2027. The club is coached by Carlos Santamarina, a product of Atlético's academy. It is Atlético's first affiliate in South America, alongside Atlético San Luis and Atlético Ottawa.

 

Atlético Femenino

Atlético Femenino win at Sevilla and hold third

Seven wins from eleven league games, with Synne Jensen on eight goals.

Atlético de Madrid Femenino won 2-1 at Sevilla on Sunday. Celia Segura, on loan from Barcelona, scored early, and Rosa Otermín curled in a direct free kick against her former club before Alice Marques pulled one back, per Las Futbolistas. A later Sevilla goal was ruled out for offside.

The previous Monday they beat Badalona 3-0 in Alcalá de Henares, with Jensen, Viola Calligaris and Kathrine Kühl scoring. Their three league defeats all came away from home, at Real Madrid, Barcelona and Athletic Club.

The table and the scorers

David González, 41, in his first women's job, has them third on 22 points (seven wins, one draw, three defeats; 24 scored, 13 conceded), behind Barcelona (28) and Real Madrid (24), per La Liga's table. Synne Jensen has eight league goals; Fiamma Benítez has three goals and four assists.

Tere Abelleira, signed on a free from Real Madrid, is still recovering from the knee injury that has kept her out since February 2025 and needs an arthroscopy. Next: Eibar away on Saturday. There is no European football this season after a fifth-place finish last term.

 

Players Away

Sixteen to eighteen Atlético players were on international duty, depending on how youth call-ups are counted.

Spain. Baena scored a penalty and Pubill a stoppage-time goal off the bench in a 2-0 win in Czechia (Nov 12). In the 3-2 loss to England at the Metropolitano (Nov 15) Baena played 90 minutes and Pubill went off injured after 20. Barrios was unused in both; Grimaldo left camp ill.
