Three clubs, three points, and Atlético host Barcelona on Sunday

La Liga's top three are separated by three points. Atlético have won seven league games in a row and go top if they win. Barcelona travel without Rodri. Real Madrid go to Mestalla on Saturday chasing an eighth straight victory.

The table says Barcelona lead La Liga. The form says something less settled. Atlético Madrid have won seven consecutive league matches, sit two points back, and host the leaders on Sunday evening in Madrid. Win it and Diego Simeone's side go top for the first time this season.

Barcelona's numbers are still the division's most arresting. Eleven games, ten wins, 46 goals scored, a goal difference of plus 34 that is sixteen better than anybody else's. Their only league defeat came at home to Real Madrid on October 25.

Atlético's case is built differently: 22 scored, eight conceded. The 2-0 win at Levante last Saturday, through Alejandro Grimaldo and Giuliano Simeone, was a controlled away performance of the kind Simeone has spent two seasons trying to restore.

And then there is the third party. Real Madrid have won all three league games since Vinícius Júnior's red card against Villarreal, including that win at Camp Nou, and go to Valencia on Saturday looking for an eighth straight victory in all competitions. They are three points off the top.

Our objective is not to beat Barcelona in one match. Our objective is to win a championship. José Mourinho, Real Madrid manager, October 31

The absences that shape it

Barcelona arrive short. Rodri, signed from Manchester City in the summer, was withdrawn at half time at Aston Villa on Tuesday, and tests on Wednesday confirmed a hamstring injury. Fabrizio Romano reported he will be out for roughly a month, which rules him out of Sunday and of Spain duty.

A workload argument has followed. Rodri had played 14 of Barcelona's first 15 matches and around 945 minutes, close to three quarters of everything available, including the full 90 against Alavés with the game long won. Broadcaster Josep Pedrerol was among those questioning the distribution. No medical link to Hansi Flick's management has been established.

Jules Koundé is also doubtful, having sprained his left ankle at Real Betis on October 17 and missed four games since. Dani Olmo, Andreas Christensen, Éric García and Roony Bardghji are all out, and Flick has said he will not force the recovery of his injured defenders.

Atlético are not whole either. Cristian Romero has still not trained with the group and was released by Argentina. Johnny Cardoso returned to training on Thursday after a thigh problem but is expected to begin on the bench. Morten Hjulmand is available again after a European suspension.

The tactical question

Simeone's answer to this Barcelona has been to play through the first press quickly rather than try to out-pass it, which is what the Levante win rehearsed. Expect Marcos Llorente restored at right back with the specific job of containing Anthony Gordon, and Julián Álvarez facing the club that pursued him in the last window.

The counterweight is Raphinha. Fifteen league goals in ten appearances, three clear in the Pichichi race, Player of the Month in both August and September, and a contract extension to 2030 signed on Thursday. Lamine Yamal, 19, has ten league goals and seven assists and became the Champions League's highest-scoring teenager this week.

Both carry European bruises into it. Atlético lost 2-0 at home to Bayern Munich on Tuesday, which ended a nine-match unbeaten run and left them 23rd in the Champions League league phase. Barcelona won 3-0 at Aston Villa. Then the league stops: a two-week international break starts on Monday.

Key facts: the three-way race

  • Barcelona 30 points, 11 played, plus 34. Ten wins. One league defeat, 1-2 at home to Real Madrid.
  • Atlético Madrid 28 points, 11 played, plus 14. Seven straight league wins. A win on Sunday puts them top.
  • Real Madrid 27 points, 11 played, plus 18. Vinícius Júnior serves the last game of a four-match ban at Mestalla.
  • Kickoffs, Pacific: Valencia v Real Madrid Saturday 12:00 pm. Atlético v Barcelona Sunday 12:00 pm.

Napoli go into a training camp, and Lazio arrive top

Aurelio De Laurentiis drove to the training ground on Friday. Massimiliano Allegri sent the squad to a hotel in Pozzuoli. Gennaro Gattuso's Lazio, on a club-record 28 points, are the visitors on Sunday lunchtime.

Serie A has an unfamiliar leader. Lazio have 28 points from ten games, nine wins and a draw, and are one of only two unbeaten sides, along with Roma. It is the most points the club has taken after ten matchdays in its history. Gennaro Gattuso was named the league's coach of the month for October this week, with league chief executive Luigi De Siervo noting that twelve different Lazio players have scored.

Their hosts on Sunday are in a different condition entirely. Napoli lost 2-0 at Juventus on November 1, then 4-1 at Porto in the Champions League on Wednesday, finishing with ten men after Rafa Marin was sent off. They are eighth, on 14 points, and 27th of 36 in the European league phase.

What followed was the week's most visible piece of man management. Italian reporting, led by Il Mattino and relayed by Tuttomercatoweb, describes a divided dressing room and tension between players and staff after the trip to Portugal. Massimiliano Allegri ordered a training camp in Pozzuoli from Friday evening, with Aurelio De Laurentiis behind him.

De Laurentiis brought his return forward and went to the Castel Volturno training ground on Friday to meet Allegri, sporting director Giovanni Manna and then the squad, urging players to drop disagreements that had become public. Each player was asked to state his position in front of his teammates.

The notable part is who is being blamed. Allegri is reported as safe, with the president's anger directed at the squad rather than the bench, and Gianluca Di Marzio reported no change coming at either Napoli or Juventus. Tuttomercatoweb's Friday framing was blunter: Allegri is not at risk for now, and the Lazio game is the first watershed.

The injury list explains some of it. Nineteen of Napoli's 24 available players have been sidelined at some point since August, and Giovanni Di Lorenzo's thigh injury was confirmed by tests on Friday. Marianucci is suspended. Lazio, who are not in Europe this season, have had a clear week to prepare.

Ronaldo ends his international career at 146 goals

He announced it on Thursday in a video on Instagram, a month after walking out of the national squad and serving a 30-day suspension. Jorge Jesus says he asked him to stay and does not feel guilty.

Cristiano Ronaldo has retired from international football. The announcement came on Thursday, and it closes a Portugal career of 146 goals in 234 caps, both national records, and both figures unlikely to be approached soon.

The ending was not clean. Ronaldo left the national team during the previous international window after a dispute with coach Jorge Jesus over playing time, accusing him of breaking promises. The Portuguese federation handed him a 30-day suspension, which he has now served. He apologised to his teammates for walking out. He did not apologise to Jesus.

His last cap was a 1-0 win over Wales in Lisbon on September 24. On October 4, in a 2-1 win in Norway, he was an unused substitute for the first time in 18 years. "It was not an easy decision," he said, describing handing back the number seven shirt.

The record is extraordinary even by the standards of his own career: most goals in World Cup qualifying with 41, most European Championship appearances with 30 and most Euro goals with 14, the only player to score at five different Euros and six different World Cups. He captained Portugal to Euro 2016 and to the Nations League in 2019 and 2025, and never won a World Cup. Portugal went out in the round of 16 this summer.

Jesus said he had asked Ronaldo to stay and now accepts the decision, and told reporters on Friday that he does not feel guilty about it. Ronaldo continues at Al Nassr, on 985 career goals, and is widely expected to leave at the end of this season.

Real Madrid are close to a 16-year-old, and Manchester United are weighing the law

Status: verbal agreement and personal terms, not official. Reported by Sky Sports, ESPN, The Athletic and Fabrizio Romano inside three days.

The best-sourced transfer story of the week concerns a player who has not made a senior appearance. JJ Gabriel, 16, Manchester United's academy player of the year last season, is expected to join Real Madrid.

Danyal Khan and Dharmesh Sheth reported for Sky Sports on November 4 that Madrid have reached a verbal agreement and intend to sign him in January, with no club-to-club contact yet. Rob Dawson of ESPN reported the same day that Madrid plan to place him with Castilla and in the UEFA Youth League. David Ornstein of The Athletic reported he is expected to sign.

Romano went furthest on Friday: the verbal agreement is "almost there", nothing will be signed this week, and "one of the first here we go for 2027 is coming". No fee or training-compensation figure has been published by anybody, so treat any number you see elsewhere as unsourced.

The mechanism matters. Sky cites a FIFA provision allowing European clubs to tie young players to professional contracts where the player holds a European passport, which Gabriel has through both parents. He is registered with United's academy only until the end of the season, and his representatives have asked the club to de-register him so he can leave as a free agent.

United are unhappy. Sky reported in October that the club would consider legal action, and ESPN that United believe his representatives met an unnamed club last summer without their knowledge. Gabriel asked to leave in September and has not trained with the first team since. Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain and Bayern Munich all explored the move.

Jorge Mendes is shopping Alejandro Balde around Europe

Status: concrete interest moving toward talks, with the agent actively canvassing. Barcelona want a permanent sale. The agent prefers a loan.

Alejandro Balde is barely playing. Romano put it plainly on November 2: "Now it's November, Balde is almost never playing, really limited minutes." Barcelona refused to let him go cheaply in the summer, and the situation has since become, in Romano's word, more complicated.

SPORT reported on Friday that Barcelona would prefer a permanent sale at around 40 million euros, while Jorge Mendes sees a loan with an option or obligation to buy as more realistic, which would at least give the club immediate salary relief. TEAMtalk put the asking price higher, at a minimum of 45 million euros.

Mendes has been busy with it. Per SPORT, approaches have been made to Liverpool, Aston Villa and Manchester United, none of whom has decided; Tottenham and Manchester City are options only if they have winter departures; talks have been held with Roma and Juventus, where his salary is the obstacle; Monaco are keeping an option open; Benfica have cooled.

United's interest has an obvious shape to it. They approached Balde in the summer, signed no cover at left back, and Luke Shaw has missed eight games and is out of contract at the end of the season. Claims that Balde's relationship with Flick is broken come from a single Spanish reporter on social media and should be treated as such.

Bayern tie Kane down, then look at a 60 million euro clause in Porto

Status: the Kane renewal is official. The Porto interest is scouting for next summer, not January.

Harry Kane signed a new Bayern Munich contract to 2029 on Thursday, ten days after becoming the first English Ballon d'Or winner in 25 years. The old deal ran to 2027. "I feel right at home at FC Bayern," he said. Sporting director Max Eberl called it a contract that "gives us certainty in our planning".

Florian Plettenberg and Nico Ditter of Sky Sport Germany reported the terms: more than 30 million euros gross a year including bonuses, tied to domestic titles, Champions League progress, another Ballon d'Or and goal and assist targets. Notably, there is no release clause and no option for a further extension.

Kane's record at the club is 156 goals in 161 competitive games since 2023, with two Bundesliga titles, a German Cup, two Supercups, the Bundesliga golden boot in all three of his seasons and two European Golden Shoes. Chief executive Jan-Christian Dreesen has indicated Michael Olise, whose deal runs to 2029, is next on the list.

The forward-looking item is Oskar Pietuszewski, an 18-year-old Poland international at Porto whose contract runs to 2031 with a 60 million euro release clause that Plettenberg reports is triggerable. Bayern want cover for Luis Díaz and Eberl is said to rate him highly, but the target window is next summer.

Inter's thorniest file has a February deadline attached

Status: no offer tabled. Hakan Calhanoglu's contract expires in June 2027 with no option to extend, and he can sign a pre-contract elsewhere from February.

Gianluca Di Marzio laid out Inter's renewal slate on November 3, and one name stands out. Hakan Calhanoglu's contract runs out at the end of this season with no extension option, which means that from February he is free to agree terms elsewhere.

He wants to stay and has told his agents so. Sporting director Baccin will take it to the ownership, and Inter plan to offer a two-year deal. Di Marzio calls the negotiation thorny and notes that Como and other clubs are circling. Nothing has been tabled.

The rest of the slate is calmer. Carlos Augusto's renewal is progressing well and could be formalised shortly. Federico Dimarco is tied to 2028 after Inter exercised an option, with talks about going beyond that not yet open. Marcus Thuram wants to concentrate on his football first.

At Napoli, the picture is worse. Romano reported that Scott McTominay's renewal, close in September and October, is now on hold because the player and his representatives want clarity on the club's direction, and that Frank Anguissa, also out of contract in June 2027, leaving for nothing is a concrete possibility.

Milan are hiring a head of football, and the clause figure is disputed

Status: advanced talks, close to agreed, official from neither club. Two outlets report two different clause figures.

Markus Krösche has not been Eintracht Frankfurt's sporting director since October 18, released from the role although his contract ran to 2028. Plettenberg and Luca Sixtus reported on November 2 that a move to AC Milan as head of football is becoming concrete, with a start discussed for January 1 ahead of the winter window.

Tuttomercatoweb went further on Friday, calling it a near certainty and reporting that Milan will pay the Frankfurt clause with Gerry Cardinale's approval. The figures do not match: Sky put the clause at five to seven million euros, Tuttomercatoweb at around 3.5 million. Both are reported here because neither has been corroborated.

His January brief, per Tuttomercatoweb, is a defender and a winger, depending on the fitness of Alexis Saelemaekers and Samuel Chukwueze. Frankfurt have meanwhile secured Joti Chatzialexiou from Nürnberg as sporting director, officially starting February 1 on a deal to 2030, for a fee of about 500,000 euros.

How to read a transfer item

Every deal in this edition is placed on the same ladder, from coldest to hottest:

  1. Rumour or link
  2. Concrete interest
  3. Advanced talks
  4. Fee agreed
  5. Personal terms agreed
  6. Medical booked
  7. Official

Only the last rung means it has happened. Anything below it can still collapse.

Chelsea's hundred-million-pound question at Bournemouth

Status: personal terms reportedly agreed, no new formal bid submitted. The asking price is the obstacle, and it is the same obstacle for two London clubs.

Bournemouth have become the Premier League's most expensive shop. Chelsea made multiple offers for Alex Scott in the summer, the highest 64 million pounds, all rejected, and The Sun reports the club want 100 million. CaughtOffside says Chelsea have already agreed personal terms with the 23-year-old England international, but there is no confirmation of a new bid.

Scott reportedly turned down a new Bournemouth contract that would have made him their highest-paid player. Liverpool have cooled over the price. He joined from Bristol City in 2023 for about 25 million.

The same club holds Tottenham's main target. Football Insider reports Tottenham are interested in Eli Junior Kroupi, 20, but he is contracted to 2030 and Bournemouth have made clear they want to keep him until at least the end of the season, with a January price again likely above 80 million pounds. He is only just back from a fractured metatarsal that needed surgery in July.

United's striker search is the more urgent one. With Benjamín Šeško out injured, Romano reported interest in Ivan Toney of Al Ahli, and Football Insider reported on Thursday that a January loan is the likelier structure. Newcastle have since emerged as rivals, with former scout Mick Brown noting Toney's existing relationship with Matthias Jaissle from Saudi Arabia.

Liverpool begin planning for life after Van Dijk

Status: concrete interest and scouting, no formal offer for either primary target.

With uncertainty over Virgil van Dijk and Joe Gomez, Romano's Daily Briefing reported on November 1 that Liverpool have identified two main centre-back targets. Neither will be straightforward.

Nico Schlotterbeck, 26, is Borussia Dortmund's captain and has just signed to 2031, but the new deal reportedly contains a release mechanism of about 50 to 60 million euros for selected clubs from the summer of 2027. Outside those conditions Dortmund have been linked with valuations above 70 million.

Murillo, 24, is contracted to Nottingham Forest until 2030, and Forest will not sell in January at any sensible price. Alessandro Bastoni and Koundé are also admired. Separately, Romano reported on Friday that Liverpool, United and Chelsea are all among the suitors watching Milan's Koni De Winter.

One live contradiction is worth flagging because it will resolve soon. Ronald Araújo is on loan at Liverpool from Barcelona. Sky Sports, ESPN and Marca have all reported a purchase option of around 55 million euros; Romano corrected that in September, saying it is a straight loan with no option. Araújo has just won Liverpool's player of the month award.

Premier League

Matchweek 9 produced one of the stranger statistical rounds in years, and the clearest story in it was Liverpool's. They lost 0-1 at home to Arsenal, beaten by a Kai Havertz header, and failed to register a single shot on target at Anfield for the first time on record, from 17 attempts.

Andoni Iraola was furious about a penalty not given for a hold on Alexander Isak, calling it "a clear, clear pull", and said he would now tell his players to go down in such situations. Jamie Carragher told VAR to do its job.

Manchester City lost 1-0 at home to Brighton, beaten by an 11th-minute Yasin Ayari goal despite 20 shots and 62 per cent possession, which leaves Brighton third on 19 points with the division's best goal difference.

Tottenham had 34 shots against Crystal Palace, their most in a Premier League game since 2016, and held 79.4 per cent possession, the lowest figure Palace have ever recorded. Everton won their first away league game of the season at Newcastle, where Matthias Jaissle was jeered and Jacob Ramsey collected two yellow cards in 86 seconds.

The pressure, though, is all at Old Trafford. Manchester United lost 2-0 at Chelsea with Lisandro Martínez sent off, are 17th on seven points, and had gone nine games in all competitions without a win before Tuesday night, their longest such run since September 1984. They have no clean sheet in nine league games and no league win since August.

Tuesday's 3-2 Champions League comeback against Roma stopped the bleeding; Sky reported on Thursday that United would be unlikely to appoint an interim should they sack Michael Carrick, and the Daily Mail reported that Didier Deschamps turned down an approach. Opta's model has United at home to Aston Villa on Sunday as the game that decides it. For what it is worth, Unai Emery confirmed on Friday that he is in talks over a new Villa contract.

Elsewhere: Cole Palmer scored his 50th Chelsea league goal; Coventry City climbed out of the relegation zone with a 3-1 win over Sunderland; Nottingham Forest are the division's lowest scorers on six goals and had 14 shots before registering one on target against Brentford, after which Oliver Glasner took responsibility, saying he had "decided everything", and warned that Forest could be on to their sixth manager since the start of last season if his players do not buy in.

Jean-Philippe Mateta's appeal against his red card failed and he serves three games. Jack Grealish remains unavailable to Everton after a breach of club discipline. Carlos Baleba is out for United for several weeks with a hamstring injury picked up against Roma.

And the Manchester City financial case still sits in limbo: the club was found guilty on the large majority of the charges brought in 2023, no sanction has been issued, and nothing has been confirmed as formally appealed.

La Liga

Beyond the title race covered above, the most arresting figure in Spain belongs to Málaga, who are bottom with five points and are the only club in the division yet to win a game after eleven. Racing Santander, promoted alongside them, have conceded 29 goals, the worst defence in the league, but have 11 points and a leading scorer in Yassir Zabiri on eight.

The outstanding promoted side is Deportivo A Coruña, eighth on 14 points with five draws, Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang on five goals in eight and Marc Casadó on loan from Barcelona.

Valencia, who host Real Madrid on Saturday, finally found a second win of the season, 1-0 at Elche through Umar Sadiq, but remain 19th on nine points under Javier Aguirre, who replaced the sacked Carlos Corberán in September. Athletic Club won the Basque derby 2-1 under Edin Terzić and have the best points-per-game record outside the top six with a game in hand. Getafe beat Sevilla 3-1 but have scored only eight goals in eleven games.

Two off-pitch threads. The abandoned Rayo Vallecano v Athletic fixture from October 10, stopped when fans protesting against president Raúl Martín Presa threw objects onto the pitch, will be replayed behind closed doors on December 9; Rayo were fined 6,000 euros and ordered to close one end of the stadium for a match.

And Vinícius Júnior missed Friday training because he is in Dubai, fulfilling what The Athletic describes as an annual commercial obligation scheduled deliberately during his suspension. Mourinho has declined to guarantee him his place back: "the team is above the player." Vinícius has one goal and three assists in twelve appearances; Yan Diomande, who has taken the shirt, has four goals and three assists in his last six.

Bundesliga

Eight matchdays in, Bayern Munich and Borussia Dortmund are the only unbeaten sides and are level on 18 points, Bayern ahead on goal difference. Their 2-2 draw at a sold-out Allianz Arena was the round's headline: Konrad Laimer and Dayot Upamecano scored for Bayern, Serhou Guirassy and an 89th-minute Fábio Silva equaliser for Dortmund, and Kane missed a penalty off the crossbar at 1-0.

Bayern's week was defined by news off the pitch. Vincent Kompany collapsed at the training ground on October 23 and was hospitalised; assistant Aaron Danks has taken charge since, winning three and drawing one in four. Danks said on Friday that Kompany appears to be on the road to a full recovery, with the international break the assessment point.

The injury list is long: Serge Gnabry is out for the rest of the calendar year, Ismael Saibari for three to four months after ankle surgery, and Jamal Musiala and Luís Díaz are due back after the break. Guirassy will miss Dortmund's Saturday game with a thigh strain.

The form story is in Bremen. Niclas Füllkrug scored in a seventh consecutive Bundesliga match in Werder Bremen's 2-0 win over Hoffenheim, equalling Rudi Völler's 1982-83 club record, and his nine goals after eight matchdays is a Werder best for that span. He leads the league. Oliver Baumann made his 400th Bundesliga appearance in the same game.

At the other end, promoted SV Elversberg are seventh after a 4-1 win over Mainz at the league's smallest ground, and they visit Dortmund on Saturday.

Borussia Mönchengladbach are bottom on four points, having sacked Eugen Polanski in September after the worst start in the club's Bundesliga history. Schalke are 17th with four goals in their last eight games, the weakest attack in Europe's five major leagues, and sporting director Frank Baumann used Sport Bild to give coach Miron Muslić an explicit public guarantee. Six clubs sit on nine points between 10th and 15th.

Serie A

Lazio's lead is covered above, but the chasing pack is unusually crowded: Inter on 25, Roma on 24 and unbeaten, promoted Frosinone sixth on 20. Milan won the Derby della Madonnina 3-1 on October 31, through Christian Pulisic, Adrien Rabiot and Diego Moreira, which was Rubén Amorim's first derby win over Cristian Chivu. Five days later Milan lost 2-0 at home to Ferencváros in the Europa League and Amorim said there was "no explanation" for it.

Juventus provided the week's biggest financial story. At the shareholders' meeting at the Allianz Stadium on Tuesday, the club approved accounts showing a consolidated net loss of about 66 million euros for the year to June 2026, a ninth consecutive annual loss, with no profit since 2016-17, and delegated a 250 million euro capital increase to the board.

New chief executive Giovanni Carnevali stressed that Juventus must work within their means because of a settlement agreement with UEFA, and backed Luciano Spalletti from the floor. John Elkann attended, was criticised by shareholders, and did not speak.

Atalanta are the crisis club. A 0-3 home defeat by Parma brought a second consecutive home booing, Maurizio Sarri spoke of "mental fragility" and apologised to supporters, and sporting director Cristiano Giuntoli called the second half unacceptable. They are 13th, twelve points off the Champions League places, with two fewer points than Ivan Jurić had at a comparable stage last season before being sacked. A dismissal is not ruled out, possibly during the international break.

Elsewhere: Parma's win was Alberto Gilardino's first; Udinese have changed manager again, with Paolo Zanetti making his debut at Venezia on Friday with the club 19th and winless since August 29; Alberto Aquilani is under pressure at Sassuolo, who have conceded 16 goals in four straight defeats.

On the new Milan stadium, the clubs' naming agreement with Generali is worth about 650 million euros over 20 years, the Lombardy administrative court rejected five appeals against the San Siro sale on October 6, and the feasibility and implementation plans are targeted for March 2027.

Ligue 1

France's week belonged to two things, and neither was a title race. Paris Saint-Germain lead on 20 points after winning 5-1 at Le Havre, where Achraf Hakimi scored a hat-trick from right back, including a direct free kick. Three days later PSG won 6-0 at Villarreal with four goals inside 17 minutes and a Champions League debut goal for 17-year-old David Boly. They have won all four European games, 20 goals to five. Hakimi has now been given a rest period until the international break and misses Saturday.

The other thing was Lens. Their 1-9 home defeat by Como on Wednesday, a third straight Champions League loss, produced a joint communiqué from five supporters' groups calling it an indelible stain and a red line crossed, announcing actions before Sunday's home game against Marseille and demanding sanctions on players and executives. Sporting director Jean-Louis Leca publicly kept faith with coach Yannick Cahuzac in L'Équipe: "nobody is thinking about leaving, not him, not us."

Lyon lost their manager mid-season from third place. Paulo Fonseca was presented at Sporting CP on November 4, the club of his childhood, for a compensation fee of about five million euros, with his Lyon contract due to expire at the end of the season. Carlos Vicens, 43, a former Guardiola assistant at Manchester City most recently sacked by Braga, was confirmed as his successor by Lyon executives to Canal+ on Thursday. Captain Corentin Tolisso: "I was disappointed." Jean-Michel Aulas publicly criticised the exit.

Also: Marseille won 1-0 at Bayer Leverkusen through Amine Gouiri for their first European points, ending Leverkusen's eleven-game unbeaten run in European home games, and Bruno Genesio equalled Guy Roux's record of 41 European wins by a coach with French clubs.

Monaco are three from three in the Conference League without conceding. Lille president Olivier Létang was banned for six months, two suspended, by the French federation's appeal commission over his conduct toward referees after a draw with PSG in August, with a 20,000 euro fine upheld; Lille will appeal. Le Havre have two points from nine games.

Major League Soccer

The regular season ends on Saturday. All 30 clubs play on Decision Day, the Eastern Conference at 1:00 pm Pacific and the West at 4:00 pm, and the Western Conference is still genuinely unresolved: six clubs separated by three points are fighting for three places, with LA Galaxy on 44, Portland Timbers, Colorado Rapids and Seattle Sounders on 43, Real Salt Lake on 42 and Minnesota United on 41. The first tiebreaker is wins, which puts Portland and Colorado ahead of Seattle at level points.

Nashville SC have already won the Supporters' Shield, their first, and sit on 74 points from 33 games, equalling Inter Miami's single-season record set in 2024. A point at FC Cincinnati breaks it. They have the league's best record at 21 wins, the fewest goals conceded at 23, and are the only club unbeaten at home this season. B.J. Callaghan is the first coach since 2016 to win the U.S. Open Cup and the Shield within two years.

Lionel Messi leads the Golden Boot race with 26 goals and 16 assists in 30 games, ahead of Petar Musa of FC Dallas on 22 and Brian White of Vancouver Whitecaps on 20, and would become the first player in league history to win consecutive Golden Boots. He scored the 83rd-minute winner at New England on November 1. All three ESPN writers project him for a third straight MVP. Year-end award voting closes Monday, with finalists announced November 12.

The playoffs then run on an unusually late calendar, pushed back by the World Cup break: the Wild Card round on November 19, best-of-three first-round series from November 20, conference semifinals December 5 and 6, finals December 11 and 12, and MLS Cup on Friday December 18, hosted by whichever finalist finished higher.

Six MLS clubs have 2027 Concacaf Champions Cup places, five of them settled: Nashville, Vancouver, Inter Miami, New England and St. Louis CITY, with the sixth going to the MLS Cup winner. 2026 is the last full spring-to-autumn season before the 14-game 2027 Sprint Season, which begins February 19.

Europe

After four matchdays of the Champions League league phase, PSG and Real Madrid are the only sides with perfect records, both on twelve points, PSG with a goal difference of plus 15. Madrid came from 2-1 down at AEK Athens to win 3-2 through a Federico Valverde stoppage-time goal, their second consecutive away comeback, and Jude Bellingham became the youngest player to reach 60 Champions League appearances. Como are fourth on nine points after the night at Lens, and Slovan Bratislava are the only club without a point.

Liverpool lost 3-2 at Fenerbahçe, who were led by interim coach Dirk Kuyt, conceding three times from set pieces, which Iraola called "cheap" and "messy".

Manchester City won 3-2 at RB Leipzig through an Erling Haaland goal on 78 minutes, though Gianluigi Donnarumma's form is now its own story: Stats Perform has him at minus 1.2 goals prevented this season after plus 6.8 last, and Enzo Maresca conceded that "Donnarumma knows he's made mistakes." Matchday 5 is November 24 and 25, with Manchester City v Napoli and Club Brugge v Liverpool the headline fixtures.

In the Europa League, Benfica, Bournemouth and Ferencváros are the three remaining perfect sides on twelve points. Bournemouth's 3-1 win at Sparta Praha, with three substitutes scoring, keeps them unbeaten in the club's first European campaign and extended a seven-win run in all competitions. Milan are 28th.

Sunderland lost 2-0 at home to Dinamo Zagreb and have one league point since their European campaign began in September. In the Conference League, which is a matchday behind, Lugano, Panathinaikos, Twente and Monaco are all on nine points, and Brighton are yet to concede a goal in the competition.
