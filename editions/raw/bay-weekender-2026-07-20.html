AT A GLANCE

It's race week: Sunday the SF Marathon sends 33,000 runners over the Golden Gate Bridge at dawn while the Giants host the Angels all weekend at Oracle Park — and Saturday July 25 is the busiest single day of the fortnight, with the Italian Family Festa in San Jose, Sausalito Art & Harbor Days, Berkeley's North Shattuck Block Party, and ChoreoFest plus the Bhangra & Beats night market back-to-back at Yerba Buena Gardens. Then the weekend of August 6–9 goes fully feral: Outside Lands, San Jose Jazz Summer Fest, Pistahan, the Gravenstein Apple Fair, two county fairs (Sonoma and Napa, newly confirmed for Aug 6–9), and a Giants–Tigers homestand, all at once. Tonight, if you're free: the Valkyries host the Mystics at Chase Center, 7pm.

Weekend weather: Textbook late July — gray mornings, afternoon clearing, and no rain anywhere in the 10-day. Highs near 65°F Saturday and 66°F Sunday in the city (lows ~57°F), partly sunny both afternoons; marathon morning will be cool and foggy — perfect running weather, chilly spectating, so bring layers to the course. Weekdays hold at 64–70°F. No rain contingencies needed for any outdoor pick this week.

⏰ Act-now clock: SF Marathon (Sun Jul 26) — the full and 1st Half are sold out (waitlists open); if you want in, the 5K/10K are your last realistic doors — check thesfmarathon.com today. Stern Grove's finale weekend (Public Enemy Aug 15, Al Green Aug 16): per the festival's published rules (lotteries open six weeks out for one week), the GA entry windows closed around July 11–12 — reserved tables were still on sale at press time and released tickets do surface, so watch sterngrove.org/ticket-info. Outside Lands is sold out at every GA/VIP tier — waitlist at sfoutsidelands.com. And Napa's Town & Country Fair just posted its concert slate (Lonestar, Sat Aug 8) — tickets on sale now at napafair.org.

★ PICK OF THE MONTH
Stern Grove's "Big Picnic" Finale: Public Enemy & Al Green
Sat Aug 15 (Public Enemy w/ LaRussell) & Sun Aug 16 (Al Green w/ Goapele, the Glide Ensemble & DJ Wonway Posibul), doors 12pm · Sigmund Stern Grove, 19th Ave & Sloat Blvd, SF · Free (GA by lottery; reserved tables sold separately)
Why this one: a soul legend who rarely tours, singing "Let's Stay Together" for free in a eucalyptus grove, is about as only-in-SF as it gets — and this is the 89th season's send-off, billed as the Big Picnic Weekend, with Chuck D one day and the Reverend Al the next. Honesty department: the GA lottery windows have now closed under the festival's six-weeks-out rules, so your paths in are reserved tables (on sale at press time), released/returned tickets, or a friend with a plus-one — worth the effort, and the listings below have plenty of backups if the grove doesn't break your way. Take the L Taraval or the 23/28 Muni lines; parking is scarce. Fog is part of the experience — bring a blanket. Lineup & ticket paths: sterngrove.org/lineup2026 · ticket info · Al Green event page

MUSIC · ARTS · NIGHTLIFE
MUSIC
Stern Grove Festival — the closing month — SF's storied free summer concert series in a natural amphitheater, now down to its last five shows: Sun Jul 26 Suki Waterhouse, Sun Aug 2 Violent Femmes w/ Tune-Yards, Sun Aug 9 Patti LaBelle, Sat Aug 15 Public Enemy, Sun Aug 16 Al Green (doors 12pm). Sigmund Stern Grove, 19th Ave & Sloat, SF. Free via GA lottery — Jul 26–Aug 9 are marked sold out, and the finale weekend's lottery entry windows have now closed per the festival's six-weeks-out rules; reserved tables and released tickets are the remaining paths. All ages. Muni over driving, always. Lineup & lottery good for: lawn picnics, legends on a budget
Outside Lands 2026 — the year's marquee splurge: Charli XCX, Rufus Du Sol and The Strokes headline 90+ acts, plus the Wine Lands/Outside Clams food universe. Fri–Sun Aug 7–9 · Golden Gate Park (Polo Field entrances on Fulton/MLK). GA, GA+ and VIP are sold out (single-day included) — waitlists are open, and resale/premium Golden Gate Club passes are the only tickets left. All ages. No festival parking: Muni 5/N or festival shuttles. Lineup & waitlist good for: the one big blowout
San Jose Jazz Summer Fest — a dozen stages of jazz, Latin, R&B and salsa dancing spill across downtown San Jose the same weekend as Outside Lands, at a fraction of the price — 2026 headliners include Patti LaBelle (Sat), Trombone Shorty & Orleans Avenue (Sun), Zapp, The Soul Rebels and the Afro-Cuban All Stars. Fri–Sun Aug 7–9 · Plaza de César Chávez & downtown San Jose. Day passes on sale. All ages. Caltrain/VTA to Diridon beats parking garages. Lineup & passes good for: the anti-Outside-Lands weekend
Lionel Richie & Earth, Wind & Fire — "Sing a Song All Night Long" — two catalogs of pure joy in one arena night. Thu Aug 6, 7:30pm · Chase Center, Mission Bay, SF. Tickets via Ticketmaster from the venue page. Chase Center events good for: multigenerational group outings
Meghan Trainor — The Get In Girl Tour — Sat Aug 8, 6:30pm · Chase Center, SF. An indoor pop counterweight to Outside Lands weekend. Tickets
★ Jerry Day 2026 — the Excelsior's homegrown Grateful Dead tribute in the amphitheater named for its own Jerry Garcia; an SF tradition tourists never find. Sat Aug 1 (daytime) · Jerry Garcia Amphitheater, McLaren Park, SF. Free, all ages. Limited parking — 29 Sunset or 54 Felton. Details good for: tie-dye, community vibes
Playing the Dead & Friends on Healdsburg Plaza — wine country's free Tuesday concert series goes full Deadhead for a night; bring a picnic and a dance partner. Tue Jul 28, 6pm · Healdsburg Plaza, Healdsburg (Sonoma County). Free, all ages. Details good for: a weeknight wine-country run
ARTS
Free Shakespeare in the Park: "Antony & Cleopatra" — SF Shakes' 2026 touring production, directed by Katja Rivera; a tight 90 minutes, no intermission. Previews this Sat–Sun Jul 25–26, then Aug 1–2 & Aug 8–9 (opening Aug 8), all 6pm at Memorial Park Amphitheater, Cupertino; Aug 15–16 it moves to Red Morton Park, Redwood City (SF parks in Sept). Free, all ages; bring low chairs. Schedule good for: golden-hour picnic theater
ChoreoFest — free outdoor dance festival — Bay Area companies perform on the Yerba Buena Gardens esplanade; day one pairs neatly with the Bhangra & Beats night market that evening. Sat Jul 25, 2–4pm & Sat Aug 1 · Yerba Buena Gardens, SoMa, SF. Free. Details
SF Arts & Crafts Show at the Presidio — final Saturday — the open-air juried show of local artisans on the Main Post lawn wraps its July run. Sat Jul 25, 10am–6pm · 210 Lincoln Blvd, Presidio, SF. Free. PresidiGo shuttle from downtown. Details
"Hairspray" at SF Playhouse — the big-hearted Broadway musical in an intimate 199-seat house; matinees and evenings most days through the summer. Ongoing; e.g. Sat 3pm shows · SF Playhouse, 450 Post St, Union Square · from ~$47. Tickets good for: fog-proof evenings
Handmade Pacifica Artisan Fair — the 6th annual coastside makers' fair at the Sanchez Art Center; pair it with a fog-line walk on Pacifica Pier. Sun Jul 26, 1–5pm · Sanchez Art Center, Pacifica (Peninsula coast). Free. Details
NIGHTLIFE
"SOMA Nights" block party + night market — Folsom Street closes from 6th to 12th for food stalls, DJs and neighborhood bar spillover; the city's newest evening market picks up where the workday ends. Thu Jul 23, 5–9pm · Folsom St (6th–12th), SoMa, SF. Free entry. Details good for: a Thursday that feels like a Friday
Up Your Alley Fair (Dore Alley) — Folsom Street's rowdier, more local mid-summer leather & kink street fair; SoMa's longest-running warm-up to September's big one. Sun Jul 26, 11am–6pm · Dore St & Folsom St, SoMa, SF. Free (donations at gates) · strictly adults-only. Details
Noe Valley Night Market — the Town Square turns into a small-batch evening market with food, drink and live music; village-scale and stroller-tolerant. Tue Jul 28, 5–8pm · Noe Valley Town Square, 24th St, SF. Free entry. Details
Chinatown Night Market — lanterns, night-market snacks and lion dances down Grant Avenue; one of the city's best free evening scenes. Fri–Sat Aug 14–15 · Grant Ave, Chinatown, SF. Free. Details good for: pre-Stern-Grove-finale evenings
★ SF Neo-Futurists: "The Infinite Wrench" — 30 plays in 60 minutes, order set by audience shouting, price set partly by a die roll; the city's best-kept late-night theater secret. Saturdays 9pm · 447 Minna St, SoMa, SF · $13–16 + die roll. Details good for: unpredictable second acts to a night out
Oakland First Fridays — Telegraph Avenue's monthly street party of art, food trucks and live performance returns for its August edition — a solid free alternative if Outside Lands priced you out. Fri Aug 7, evening · Telegraph Ave (West Grand to 27th), Oakland (East Bay). Free (donations welcome). Details
FOOD · DRINK · FESTIVALS
FOOD
Italian Family Festa — the South Bay's 44th annual celebration of all things Italian: pasta, bocce, music, and nonna-level cannoli. Sat Jul 25, from 10am · History Park, San Jose. Free admission. Details good for: families, carb loading before marathon Sunday
★ Gravenstein Apple Fair — Sonoma County's "sweetest little fair" under the oaks of Ragle Ranch: heritage Gravenstein apples, cider tastings, farm demos, live bands. A west-county institution worth the drive. Sat–Sun Aug 8–9, 10am–6pm · Ragle Ranch Regional Park, Sebastopol. Ticketed (buy ahead online; kids discounted). Tickets good for: apple pie pilgrims, an OL-weekend escape hatch
Tokyo Central Food Fair — a daylong Japanese food fair at the East Bay's Tokyo Central market — think street snacks, bento specials and imported-grocery deep cuts. Sat Jul 25, 9am–9pm · Bay Street, Emeryville (East Bay). Free entry. Details
Head West Marketplace at the Ferry Building — first-Sunday makers-and-food market along the Embarcadero waterfront. Sun Aug 2 · Ferry Building, SF. Free to browse. Details
Off the Grid: Friday night market at Fort Mason — 20+ food trucks, bars and bay views; the classic SF Friday warm-up. Fridays, 5–10pm · Fort Mason Center, Marina, SF. Free entry (food/drink for purchase). Details good for: zero-planning Fridays
DRINK
Sonoma County Fair — ten days of wine-country fair season: local wine and cider pours, fair food, free live music, the Wine Country Rodeo and Cirque Ma'Ceo, plus the famous Hall of Flowers show. Fri Aug 7 – Sun Aug 16 · Sonoma County Fairgrounds, Santa Rosa. Admission ticketed; check site for hours/prices. Details good for: a proper day trip north
Napa Town & Country Fair — Napa's hometown fair returns at the Expo: carnival rides, livestock, local wine & beer, and free-with-admission concerts topped by country hitmakers Lonestar on Saturday night. Thu–Sun Aug 6–9; gates 3pm Thu–Fri, 1pm Sat–Sun; Lonestar Sat 7:30pm · Napa Valley Expo, 575 Third St, Napa. Ticketed — on sale now. Tickets & lineup good for: fair food with a wine-country accent
FESTIVALS
Bon Odori Street Dance Festival — Japantown's 95th annual obon street dance: everyone's invited into the circle, no experience needed. Sun Jul 26, 11am–4pm · Buddhist Church of San Francisco, Japantown, SF. Free. Details good for: joining in, not just watching
52nd Nihonmachi Street Fair — Japantown's flagship summer fair: taiko, food booths, small-business vendors, and one of the city's oldest community-run festivals. Sat–Sun Aug 1–2, 11am–6pm · Post & Buchanan, Japantown, SF. Free. Details
33rd Pistahan Parade & Festival — the West Coast's largest celebration of Filipino food, dance and music; the lumpia and lechon alone justify the trip. Sat–Sun Aug 8–9; parade Sat 11am down Market St · Yerba Buena Gardens, SoMa, SF. Free. Details
Sausalito Art & Harbor Days — waterfront art showcase from the town's working artist community. Sat Jul 25, 10am–3pm · Ferry Landing, Sausalito (Marin). Free. Take the Golden Gate Ferry and make it a car-free day. Details
North Shattuck Block Party — Berkeley's Gourmet Ghetto closes to cars for a day of food stalls, local makers and live music in the neighborhood that invented California cuisine. Sat Jul 25, 11am–6pm · North Shattuck district, Berkeley (East Bay). Free. Downtown Berkeley BART + a short walk. Details
"Bhangra & Beats" Night Market — Yerba Buena Gardens goes big on South Asian food, DJs and bhangra dancing into the evening. Sat Jul 25, 4–9pm · Yerba Buena Gardens, SoMa, SF. Free. Details good for: making Jul 25 a full YBG day (ChoreoFest 2pm → this at 4)
Laurel Street Fair — Oakland's easygoing MacArthur Blvd neighborhood fair: food trucks, kid zones and live soul/funk all afternoon, the same day the big festivals rage elsewhere. Sat Aug 8, 11am–7pm · MacArthur Blvd (35th–Maybelle), Laurel District, Oakland (East Bay). Free. Details
