The Lead

Nine at Bollaert: Como rewrite the Champions League record book

A 9-1 win at Lens equalled the competition's biggest winning margin, made Tasos Douvikas Europe's top scorer and lifted Como to fourth. The harder question arrives on Sunday at San Siro.

The biggest night in Como's 119-year history came on a Wednesday night in northern France. Cesc Fabregas's side won 9-1 at Lens in the Champions League, the fourth European Cup game the club has ever played, and turned a promising debut campaign into a story told across the continent. It was "a wonderful night for Como", Fabregas said. The record books agreed.

The numbers came fast and kept coming. Tasos Douvikas scored at 7, 32 and 57 minutes. Nico Paz, Lucas Da Cunha, Marc-Oliver Kempf, Mattia Liberali and Martin Baturina joined in, and the 18-year-old academy midfielder Matteo Papaccioli rounded it off in stoppage time on his first-team debut. Lens managed one reply, a Ruben Aguilar volley from a corner at 65 minutes.

The eight-goal margin equals the biggest win in Champions League history, alongside Liverpool's 8-0 over Besiktas in 2007 and Real Madrid's 8-0 over Malmo in 2015, beIN Sports noted. Como are only the second side to score nine in a Champions League game, after Bayern's 9-2 against Dinamo Zagreb in 2024. No Italian club had ever scored more than seven in the competition.

How it happened

This was not a smash-and-grab. Como had 57 per cent of the ball, 18 shots to 11 and, per ESPN's numbers, 12 on target to Lens's three. FotMob's expected-goals model gave Como about 4.2 and Lens about one, so the finishing ran hot. But Fabregas's side created enough to win comfortably, and the clinical part followed from that.

The shape was Fabregas's familiar 4-2-3-1: Jean Butez in goal; Yan Couto, Jacobo Ramon, Kempf and Alex Valle across the back; Maximo Perrone and Luis Milla in midfield; Da Cunha, Paz and Maxence Caqueret behind Douvikas. The opener came from a Perrone corner. Kempf headed in a Milla cross for 5-0. Paz's rifled shot made it 3-0 just before half-time, and he then set up Da Cunha for the fourth.

There were two early warnings. Butez denied Florian Thauvin, and a Lens penalty for a Caqueret handball was overturned by VAR, according to VAVEL's live report. Had either gone the other way, this might have been a much tighter night.

The bench made it a record

Fabregas changed three players at 58 minutes, sending on Samuele Ricci, Liberali and Baturina, then Papaccioli for Douvikas three minutes later. Liberali scored within four minutes of arriving, and ESPN credited him with two assists as well. Baturina added the eighth. Papaccioli's goal, ruled onside after a review, completed the nine.

That detail matters. Fabregas told Sky Sport on Tuesday, as reported by ANSA, that his Champions League list leaves him only 15 outfield players. Moise Kean, Assane Diao and Jesus Rodriguez are all injured. The players who came on this week were the young and the recently recovered, and they scored three times in half an hour.

We must learn this now so we don't buy into our own hype or think that the job is already done.

Cesc Fabregas, after the win at Lens (via ANI)

Where it leaves Como, and what comes next

Como are fourth in the 36-team league phase on nine points, three wins from four, with a goal difference of plus 13 (19 scored, six conceded). Only PSG, on 20, have scored more. The top eight go straight to the round of 16, and Como's next two games are at home to AEK Athens on November 24 and away to Real Betis.

Douvikas has now scored in all four of Como's Champions League games, six goals in total, the most in the competition. Sky Sport Italia found only one precedent for an Italian side's player scoring in each of the first four, Alessandro Del Piero in 1995-96. He has 11 goals in all competitions and signed a new contract to 2031 in late October.

Fabregas spent the post-match period trying to lower the temperature. He said he was "angry" about the goal conceded, because his substitutions had changed the defensive structure, and that he had been telling the players to slow the tempo with another game coming. He also called PSG and Barcelona "two teams who are of a superior level to the rest."

The real test is Sunday. Como have won six in a row in all competitions, but they visit Inter, the one club Fabregas has never beaten as Como coach. In four meetings since December 2025 Inter have won three and drawn one, including the Coppa Italia semi-final in April, when Como led 2-0 at San Siro and lost 3-2. Beating Lens 9-1 proves this side can be ruthless. Winning at San Siro would show it can compete for the title.

 
Also this week

Suwarso: the selling will come, and the stadium plan is ready

In a wide-ranging interview, the club president prepared fans for future sales and said the revised Sinigaglia project is ready to file.

Speaking to La Provincia di Como on Tuesday, president Mirwan Suwarso was unusually direct about the club's model. "Sooner or later, more sooner than later, we will be forced to sell some pieces of our collection," he said, as reported by Gianluca Di Marzio. He named Atalanta as the model.

The books explain why. An independent analysis of UK filings published on The Esk this week put the 2024-25 net loss at 105.1 million euros and owner funding at 297.4 million euros by November 2025.

On the stadium, Suwarso said the revised Sinigaglia project, rewritten to answer earlier objections, is ready to be filed with the Comune; he gave no date, according to La Provincia di Como. He also clarified his old "Disneyland" remark about the city. The ground already got a new home end this summer, taking capacity to 12,370 for Serie A and 10,875 for UEFA games, per Calcio e Finanza.

On Fabregas, the message was calm: "An excellent relationship, very clear, very transparent... the day he wants something else, he'll tell us in time."

Scouting the Next Opponent

Inter: wounded, rotated and still second

Cristian Chivu's side lost the derby last weekend but answered with a 3-0 win in Rotterdam. Without Lautaro Martinez, they are beatable. History says Como have not managed it yet.

Inter sit second on 25 points from 10 games (eight wins, one draw, one loss), two clear of Como. Their only league defeat came last Saturday, a 3-1 derby loss to Milan in which they conceded twice in the first 10 minutes, once from a corner, and a third from a direct free kick. They responded with a 3-0 win at Feyenoord on Tuesday, through Bisseck, Pio Esposito and Zielinski, per beIN Sports.

Chivu has used a 3-5-2. Marcus Thuram leads the scoring with five league goals, and Hakan Calhanoglu runs the set pieces; he made five key passes in the derby. Piotr Zielinski scored against Fiorentina and Feyenoord, and Carlos Augusto has chipped in from wing back. In Europe, Inter are only 15th on six points after defeats to Real Madrid and Club Brugge.

Team news and weak spots

Lautaro Martinez is out with a muscle strain and is aiming for the Atalanta game after the break, per L'Interista. Luis Henrique (adductor) is also missing. John Stones and Curtis Jones are back in partial training and could make the bench, Benjamin Pavard is fit again, and Alessandro Bastoni, who scored in the derby, is available after serving a Champions League suspension.

The vulnerabilities are recent and specific: slow starts (the derby), a habit of leaving it late (they needed stoppage-time goals to beat Fiorentina 2-1), and set pieces against. Como's quick wide combinations and Douvikas's movement are built to test exactly that.

The head-to-head is the worry. Inter won 4-0 at San Siro in December 2025, won 4-3 at the Sinigaglia in April and came from 2-0 down to beat Como 3-2 in the Coppa Italia semi-final second leg. The only result Como took was a 0-0 in the first leg. Soccerway also recalls that Inter wanted Fabregas as their coach in June 2025, and Como said no.

Transfer News

Nico Paz: Como's line is firm, but the clause hangs over everything

Premier League clubs are circling Como's playmaker. Real Madrid hold the real power, and nothing can happen until July.

The structure was set in June. Real Madrid triggered their buy-back on Nico Paz, Como then bought him back for 60 million euros, and Madrid kept a new 80 million euro buy-back clause, as Fabrizio Romano reported. ESPN's Alex Kirkland described Madrid's option as one-sided and for the 2027-28 season.

Spanish outlet Fichajes, citing AS, says the clause can be triggered from July 1 to 15, 2027. That is single-sourced and worth treating as a report, not a fact. TEAMtalk, via The Hard Tackle, says Chelsea, Manchester United and Arsenal are monitoring him and that Como will not entertain January bids. Certainty: rumour. Paz has already scored twice against United this season.

Jacobo Ramon draws the same suitors

CaughtOffside, citing the Daily Mail, links Arsenal, Manchester United and Chelsea with the young centre back Jacobo Ramon, and says Real Madrid retain a buy-back and sell-on interest; no figures were given. Certainty: rumour. Marca reports he is in contention for a first senior Spain call-up when the squad is named on Friday.

Camello the name for January

The only named incoming target this week is Rayo Vallecano striker Sergio Camello. Marco Conterio of TuttoMercatoWeb wrote on October 30 that Como admire him, that his contract ends in June 2027, that a January move would cost about 10 million euros, and that Atletico Madrid hold a buy-back "until the winter". Certainty: concrete interest. With Kean, Diao and Rodriguez all injured, a forward makes sense. Sky Italia reported in September that the winter window opens early, in December.

Douvikas is going nowhere

The one piece of official business: Tasos Douvikas extended his contract to 2031 in late October, per Calciomercato.com. TMW's Simone Bernabei argued on Thursday that he is now worth more than 50 million euros and that Como would refuse even huge offers. That is opinion, but the contract makes it a strong position.

The Squad

Fabregas's emergency up front, and how he is managing it

Three forwards are out, one centre back is a doubt, and the Champions League list is short. It has not stopped the winning.

Cesc Fabregas has stuck with a 4-2-3-1 through the injuries. Against Venezia the XI was Butez; Couto, Kempf, Ramon, Kaiki; Ricci, Milla; Da Cunha, Paz, Baturina; Douvikas. Against Lens he rotated: Valle for Kaiki at left back, Perrone for Ricci and Caqueret for Baturina. After Lens he said he is "only thinking about getting back the many players we're missing, especially up front", per Corriere dello Sport.

Douvikas has carried the attack, with 11 goals in all competitions (five in Serie A, six in Europe). Paz has six goals and leads Como's Serie A assists with three. Butez has started the big games, though Fabregas called the Butez and Robert Sanchez question "still open" in October.

Squad-list squeeze

UEFA allows one injury replacement before the knockout phase, goalkeepers apart, and Como have used it on Caqueret. "We're penalised by the Champions list: we have 15 outfield players," Fabregas told ANSA on Tuesday, citing his comments to Sky Sport. That is why academy players such as Papaccioli are getting minutes in Europe.

The Academy

Papaccioli's night, and a Primavera finding its feet

An 18-year-old from the academy scored on his debut in the Champions League. The Primavera, new to the top youth division, sits mid-table.

Matteo Papaccioli was born on May 9, 2008. He is a left-footed Italian-Brazilian midfielder who grew up in Brazil, playing for Taubate and futsal until he was 10, joined Como in 2022 and signed his first professional contract in October 2024, per TuttoMercatoWeb. He has 11 caps and three goals for Italy's under-16s and under-17s. At Lens he came on for Douvikas at 61 minutes and scored in stoppage time.

Primavera

Como's Primavera are in Primavera 1 for the first time after promotion, coached by Gianluca Falsini, formerly of Roma's academy. They are 12th on 12 points after nine rounds (three wins, three draws, three defeats), six behind leaders Parma and Inter, per MondoPrimavera. Their latest league game was a 2-1 home win over Atalanta. In the UEFA Youth League they lost 2-1 at Lens, with Riccardo Cassano scoring. Sportitalia reports Cassano has been named in Italy's senior squad this week (see Players Away). Next: Fiorentina away on Sunday.

Women's Team

Como 1907 Women, unbeaten newcomers, host leaders Roma

In their first Serie A season, Selena Mazzantini's side are third and unbeaten. Saturday brings the leaders to town, and the city derby follows a week later.

A note first: two clubs from the city play in Serie A Women this season. Como 1907's own team, coached by Selena Mazzantini with Patrizia Panico as her assistant, won Serie B last season. Como Women, in black and white, is a separate club owned by Mercury13, as Il Post explains. This section follows Como 1907's team.

They are third on 11 points after five games (three wins, two draws, goals 13 to seven), four behind leaders Roma, per DAZN. Last Saturday they drew 1-1 at Ternana: Bonfantini scored after 25 minutes from Moraca's pass, and Strode equalised at 76.

A statement start

The results so far: 4-1 against Milan, 2-1 at Napoli with Xhemaili scoring in the 95th minute, a 4-2 win at Juventus with two from Agnese Bonfantini, and a 2-2 draw with Sassuolo (Valentina Giacinti scored, and Tessa Wullaert rescued a point with a 93rd-minute penalty). Wullaert, signed from Inter, leads the scoring with four; Bonfantini has three and Emilie Haavi two.

Next up is Roma, top on 15 points, at home on Saturday (3:30 a.m. Pacific, DAZN). The derby against Como Women, fourth on eight points after beating Milan 1-0 last weekend, follows on Sunday, November 15, according to the FIGC schedule.
