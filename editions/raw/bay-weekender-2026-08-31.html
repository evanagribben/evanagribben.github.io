At a Glance

Five weeks, and the best free weekend of the year at the end of them

This is a five-week edition, and it is front-loaded with the kind of weekend San Francisco is actually good at. Labor Day lands on Monday September 7. Before it, the Dons play twice at Negoesco and Bay FC host Kansas City under lights in San Jose. After it, the calendar barely pauses.

September is the month the city stops pretending it is summer and gets on with it. Twelve pianos go into the Botanical Garden for eleven days from September 10. The San Francisco Opera plays a free afternoon on the grass on September 13. Grant Avenue closes for the Autumn Moon Festival, Haight Street closes for its street fair, and Folsom Street closes for a fair that needs no introduction.

The sport is unusually good right now. The Valkyries have clinched a playoff spot at 28-11 and close the regular season with two home nights at Chase Center. The Giants have not, and their final homestand against the Dodgers is the last baseball at Oracle until spring. The Earthquakes take their annual trip to Levi's Stadium on September 19.

Then the window closes on the best free weekend of the year. Hardly Strictly Bluegrass runs October 2 to 4 in Golden Gate Park, and the night before it, the Masonic hosts a one-off tribute to Steve Earle with Emmylou Harris, Jackson Browne and Gillian Welch. Plan the first weekend of October now.

The weekend read

The National Weather Service has San Francisco holding steady in the mid-60s all week: partly sunny and 65 on Monday, up to 67 on Tuesday, and mostly sunny and 66 by Friday. The only wet note in the forecast is a slight chance of showers on Friday night.

Saturday September 5 comes back mostly sunny and 67, which is close to ideal for the first Saturday of a long weekend. Sunday September 6 sits past the current forecast horizon, so treat it as unknown rather than assured.

For Candlestick Point, Mount Diablo or anything in Golden Gate Park, the risk this week is wind and afternoon fog rather than rain.

The act-now clock

  • NowPortola passes are on sale for September 26 and 27. Two-day general admission starts at $399.95 and single days at $269.95, both direct from the festival with no added fees.
  • GoneOrville Peck at the Castro Theatre on September 12 has sold out. The Sunday September 13 show was still available at the time of writing.
  • Arrive earlyThe Valkyries give a shoulder bag to the first 10,000 fans at the September 19 season finale. That is a real queue, not a token one.
  • Book the weekendHardly Strictly Bluegrass on October 2 to 4 is free and needs no ticket, but the Steve Earle tribute at the Masonic on October 1 is a single benefit night and will not repeat.
  • Last chanceSunday September 27 is the Giants' final home game of 2026. There is no baseball at Oracle Park after that until spring.

Pick of the Month

Hardly Strictly Bluegrass

Friday October 2 to Sunday October 4, 2026·Hellman Hollow, Golden Gate Park, San Francisco·Free. No tickets, no wristbands, no queue.

The defensible reason is the simplest one available: it is the largest free music festival in the country, it is in our park, and it is free because a San Francisco financier endowed it to stay that way in perpetuity. Warren Hellman died in 2011. The festival has not charged a cent since, and roughly 750,000 people turn up across the three days.

The 2026 bill has now had three announcement rounds. Gillian Welch and David Rawlings, My Morning Jacket, Alison Krauss, Sierra Hull, Old Crow Medicine Show, Hiss Golden Messenger, Kathleen Edwards, Yasmin Williams with William Tyler, AJ Lee and Blue Summit, El Khat and Tyler Ballgame are all confirmed, with more still to come.

What tips it from very good to unmissable this year is the night before. On Thursday October 1 the Masonic hosts An Evening Honoring Steve Earle, with Emmylou Harris, Jackson Browne, Hot Tuna, Gillian Welch and David Rawlings, Molly Tuttle, Buddy Miller, Larry Campbell and Teresa Williams and others, benefiting Sweetwater Spectrum. That lineup will not assemble again.

Practical advice: do not drive. The N Judah, the 5 Fulton and the 7 all serve the park and all of them will be full. Go in on foot from the east end, bring a layer for when the fog comes in around four, and wear boots if there has been rain, because Hellman Hollow turns to mud faster than you would think.

Trending and gone viral

The matcha queue

Kissaten Hifi, Nagomi, Shoji and Q Specialty Coffee, across San Francisco

Twenty-five-minute waits for a matcha latte are now normal at several San Francisco cafes, driven almost entirely by social video. Being honest about it: The Infatuation, which named the trend, also said plainly that the waits exceed what is in the cup. The queue is real. The verdict on the drink is mixed.

The baklava boom

Turquaz, Cafe Velora and Kapari's, across San Francisco

Turkish and Middle Eastern bakeries have moved baklava out of the special-occasion box and into everyday sales, with walnut, pistachio and chocolate cherry versions all over the city. This one has caught on because the product genuinely improved, not because a video did well.

Yemeni coffee after ten

Delah Coffee, Haraz Coffee House and Heyma, across San Francisco

Yemeni coffee houses are open past 10pm and in some cases until 1am at weekends, which has quietly solved a problem San Francisco has had for a decade: nowhere sober to go after dinner. That, rather than the coffee itself, is why the format is spreading.

Up and coming

Vinyl-first cafes and bars

Kissaten Hifi, San Francisco; Kissakeko, Nob Hill

A small cluster of rooms built around a record player and a real sound system rather than a playlist, following the Japanese kissaten and listening-bar model. Kissakeko in Nob Hill is the sake-focused version. Early, but the format has legs.

The no-reservation turn

La Cigale, Glen Park; Khao Tiew, San Francisco

La Cigale in Glen Park opened with no tips and no reservations, and Khao Tiew has since switched to walk-in only. It trades planning for queueing, which is a real cost, but it is also how a neighbourhood restaurant stays a neighbourhood restaurant.
