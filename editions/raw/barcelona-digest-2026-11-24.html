The Lead

Match of the week · La Liga, Matchday 13

Two down in seven minutes, three goals in ten: how Barça turned Villarreal around

Barça trailed 2-0 after seven minutes, scored three times in ten second-half minutes, and stayed three points clear. Now comes the bill: Yamal is out for Sabah and Fermín faces a ban.

Seven minutes in, Barcelona were 2-0 down at home to Villarreal. Tajon Buchanan had scored after five minutes from a loose pass by Fermín López, and Tani Oluwaseyi doubled the lead two minutes later (EFE via Infobae; ESPN). According to beIN, Barça had not conceded twice in the first seven minutes of a La Liga game since November 1981 (beIN Sports).

By the 67th minute it was 3-2. Gerard Martín headed in Pedri's corner after 57 minutes, Fermín finished a move started by Raphinha's ball-winning and Lamine Yamal's pass on 61, and Eric García's cross forced Villarreal centre-back Pau Navarro into an own goal on 67. Three goals in ten minutes, and Barça stayed three points clear at the top.

Control, then a cutting edge

The numbers say Villarreal were never in it after their second goal. Barça had 70 per cent of the ball, 30 shots to four and 10 corners to none, according to ESPN's match stats. Their expected goals came to 3.29 against Villarreal's 0.17. Villarreal goalkeeper Peter Gulácsi made seven saves. Joan García did not have to make one: both shots he faced went in.

The trouble was the hour before the comeback. Yamal won a penalty in the first half after a VAR review and put it against the post. ESPN counts that as his third missed penalty for Barça in 2026 (ESPN). Barça went in 2-0 down despite dominating the ball, which is why the second half needed a change of rhythm rather than a change of plan.

“The reaction was unbelievable.”

Hansi Flick after Barcelona 3-2 Villarreal, via ESPN

A back line built from spare parts

The bigger story is who did it. With Andreas Christensen injured and Pau Cubarsí not fit to start, Hansi Flick played Frenkie de Jong and Gerard Martín at centre-back, Eric García at right-back and João Cancelo at left-back (EFE via Infobae). Marc Bernal held the midfield in place of the injured Rodri.

Flick praised both centre-backs for playing out of position. Of Gerard Martín, he said: "His evolution and impact on the team is incredible" (Forbes). Of De Jong he admitted "that position isn't an easy one for Frenkie" (Barça Universal via Yahoo). It worked well enough on the day, but it is not a pairing Flick will want to rely on against Atlético on December 2.

What it cost

The afternoon still came with a bill. Yamal felt "mild discomfort" in his right ankle in the warm-up and played the full game, but has been left out of the trip to Baku as a precaution (FutbolFantasy, citing the club). Bernal finished with cramp, which is why Gavi came on in stoppage time (Vavel).

And Fermín, substituted on 68 minutes, was sent off from the bench six minutes later. That story is below.

Flick was also booked for protesting and was critical of referee Alejandro Hernández Hernández. "I am really happy with the referees, but with this referee I am not," he said (ESPN). He also opened a debate about penalties, saying they are "decided on the pitch" but that "maybe I have to think about changing it" (Pulse, citing Flick).

Why it matters

Real Madrid beat Celta 2-1 on Saturday and Atlético won 3-0 at Getafe on Sunday, so a draw would have cut Barça's lead to one point (Futboltotal). Instead the table after 13 games reads Barça 36, Madrid 33, Atlético 31. With eight more games before the end of the year, by the club's count, every point banked now matters (FC Barcelona).

Secondary feature

One game or two? Fermín waits on the referee's report

Barça are fighting to keep the ban to one match. If it is two, he misses Deportivo and Atlético.

Fermín's afternoon went from equaliser to red card in 13 minutes. Substituted for Dani Olmo on 68 minutes, he went to the fourth official, Alejandro Clemente Ortuño, to protest and was sent off after heading for the dressing room. The referee's report says he repeatedly shouted "¡No me jodas!" (SI; Goal).

The wording matters. Protest can carry two to three matches under the Spanish federation's code, according to SI, while Mundo Deportivo says a lighter classification as conduct contrary to good sporting order could mean one (SB Nation, citing Mundo Deportivo). Barça were preparing their arguments this week. Former referee Eduardo Iturralde, speaking on AS and SER, expects two games (Goal).

No decision had been published when we went to press. A domestic ban does not affect Wednesday's game: Fermín travelled to Baku with the squad (Infobae). He has eight La Liga goals, third in Barça's chart behind Raphinha (15) and Yamal (10), according to 365Scores.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Next: Wednesday, November 25 · Champions League, Matchday 5

Sabah: a leaky defence at the end of Barça's longest European trip

Three points from four games for the Baku side. Barça arrive without Yamal but with a chance to draw level with the leaders on points.

Barça's longest away trip in European competition, about 4,000 kilometres, ends at the Baku Olympic Stadium on Wednesday (SocBlaugrana via Yahoo). Sabah are 31st of 36 in the league phase with three points: a 4-0 defeat at Manchester United, a 2-0 home win over Slavia Prague, a 2-1 defeat by Borussia Dortmund and a 4-1 loss at Viking (Afrik-Foot). They have scored four and conceded ten.

Valdas Dambrauskas, the Lithuanian coach, sets them up in a 4-2-3-1 (Goal). Afrik-Foot's preview singles out Nwachukwu, Khaybulayev, Mickels and Mbina as the players who matter most (Afrik-Foot). Defender Aden McCarthy is suspended after his red card in Norway.

Where Barça can hurt them

Sabah have conceded 2.5 goals a game in the competition, and their defeats at Old Trafford and in Stavanger came by three goals each. Barça took 30 shots against Villarreal; a similar volume should tell. The risk is complacency after a long flight: the squad flew in on Monday and will fly home straight after the game, with no training on Thursday (SocBlaugrana via Yahoo).

For Barça, the stakes are clear. They are fifth on nine points from four games, three behind leaders PSG and Real Madrid (World Today Journal). A win would keep them in the race for the top eight and a bye past the play-offs, before Manchester City visit on December 8. Nicolás Walsh referees, his fourth Champions League game (SB Nation, citing Mundo Deportivo).

Transfer News

The big thread

Deco reopens the centre-back question

"Everything can be reconsidered," says the sporting director. The shortlist has changed, the rule that decides it has not.

A week ago the line was that Barça would only sign in January if someone left. Then Deco went on Catalunya Ràdio. "It's not normal to have Christensen, Eric and Koundé injured in November," he said, adding that "everything can be reconsidered" and that any decision would be taken with Flick (Barça Universal, citing Mundo Deportivo, November 18). Status: concrete interest in the position; no named target.

Mundo Deportivo's January shortlist, as relayed by Barça Universal, has three names: Inter's Alessandro Bastoni (around €40m, with about 18 months left on his deal), Borussia Dortmund's Nico Schlotterbeck (around €60m) and Manchester United's Lisandro Martínez, "the more economical alternative" (Barça Universal, citing MD, November 19). Status: links.

ElNacional adds that Bastoni is unlikely before the summer, that Twente's 18-year-old Ruud Nijstad is another January option, and that Josko Gvardiol remains Flick's preferred summer target but is seen as very hard to get (ElNacional, November 23). Sport reported earlier this month that Twente want about €20m for Nijstad and Barça will not pay it (Barça Universal, citing Sport). Status: links.

Cooling: Tah

Bayern's Jonathan Tah has drifted out of the picture. Florian Plettenberg of Sky Germany said on November 10 that a move was "incredibly complicated" because Bayern will not sell (Sports Mole), and a November 23 roundup says Barça have now ruled him out (LiveScore). Status: cooling; the ruling-out is single-sourced.

The constraint is the same as last week. Barça are still working under La Liga's spending rules, and Gerard Martín and De Jong at centre-back against Villarreal gave the "trust the squad" camp an argument (ElNacional).

Outgoing

Balde: Mendes in town, PSG and Juventus watching, Laporta saying stay

The left-back has played 123 minutes this season. The club's public and private lines do not quite match.

Alejandro Balde is the busiest outgoing story. Fabrizio Romano said on November 18 that a January exit is "serious and concrete" and that Balde "is genuinely ready to consider leaving" (Football365, citing Romano). Mundo Deportivo reported that his agent Jorge Mendes flew to Barcelona and visited the player, with Paris Saint-Germain and Juventus monitoring; Balde's contract runs to 2028 (Goal via LiveScore).

Goal, citing Foot Mercato, says PSG want him in January, Juventus are exploring a loan with an option that becomes compulsory if they qualify for the Champions League, and Barça want at least €40m (Goal). That report is single-sourced. Status: concrete interest from PSG and Juventus; no bid confirmed.

The club's public line is different. In New York on November 21, Joan Laporta told TUDN: "We must recover Balde," and left January decisions to Deco (Crónica Global).

Deco said on the radio that "there's been nothing on Balde's part" and that if he said he was unhappy, "we'd consider it" (Barça Universal). Flick, asked after Villarreal, said: "It's not easy for him to get playing time" (Barça Universal via Yahoo).

On loan

Araújo shines as Deco talks up Liverpool's option

Ronald Araújo started at right-back as Liverpool beat Manchester United 4-0 on Sunday and was rated 7 by ESPN, which called him "immense at the back again" (ESPN).

Deco said a sale "would not be a bad thing for us" if Liverpool trigger their purchase option (SI), and that "there is a date to execute the deal" but "the date is not now" (FootballTransfers). SI puts the option at about £47m; Spanish media cite €55m. Status: existing option; no decision from Liverpool.

The Squad

Yamal stays home, Rodri edges closer, and a penalty debate

Flick takes 22 to Baku, including a first call-up for Jesse Bisiwu, and could have his holding midfielder back on Sunday.

The headline absence for Wednesday is Lamine Yamal. The club's medical update on November 23 reported mild discomfort in his right ankle and said he would be held back as a precaution, with a return expected at Deportivo on Sunday (FutbolFantasy; SB Nation). He had 10 La Liga goals and leads the league in assists, with eight or nine depending on the count (365Scores).

The 22-man list for Baku gives Barça Atlètic forward Jesse Bisiwu his first Champions League call-up. Pau Cubarsí, who missed Villarreal with an overloaded iliopsoas after Spain duty, travels (Infobae; FutbolFantasy). Jules Koundé returned for his first minutes since October 17, coming on for the last six minutes plus stoppage time.

The Rodri question

Rodri, out since injuring his hamstring against Aston Villa, could be back sooner than planned. Mundo Deportivo reports he could be available at Deportivo, with Flick keen to give him minutes before Atlético on December 2; ElNacional expects medical clearance this week (ElNacional, citing MD).

Until then Bernal holds the role. Flick said: "Rodri is an incredible player, but the rest of the midfielders are also doing very well" (Barça Universal via Yahoo).

Who takes the penalties?

Yamal's miss reopened a debate. Raphinha leads La Liga with 15 goals and is the club captain. Flick said both can take penalties, but "maybe I have to think about changing it; I am not sure" (Pulse). Set pieces paid off elsewhere: Barça won 10 corners to none, and the first goal came from one.

La Masia

Out of the Copa Catalunya, still top of the league

A cup exit at Can Vidalet, a hard-fought derby draw and a first-against-second game on Saturday.

Juliano Belletti's Barça Atlètic went out of the Copa Catalunya on Wednesday, November 18, beaten 2-1 at Can Vidalet with a largely second-string side. Ruslan Mba equalised after 76 minutes before Roger Valenzuela won it for the hosts on 85 (FC Barcelona).

Saturday's derby at Espanyol B finished 0-0 at the Dani Jarque. Catovic had an early goal ruled out for offside, Barça hit the woodwork, and Espanyol B finished with nine men: Lucas Alves was sent off for stopping Orian Goren, and a late injury came after their substitutes were used (La Grada).

The draw keeps Barça Atlètic top of their Segunda Federación group on 26 points from 12 games, two ahead of Terrassa (FotMob).

Calls from upstairs

Jesse Bisiwu has his first Champions League call-up, for Sabah (Infobae). Barça Universal reports that 16-year-old centre-back Mikael Baza, signed from Hamburg, trained with the first team on November 21 when Christensen was injured and the Atlètic defenders were busy with the derby (Barça Universal via Yahoo). That report is single-sourced.

Out on loan

Barça Femení

Twelve goals, two clean sheets, and a quarter-final place already won

Clàudia Pina's hat-trick against Servette sealed the Champions League knockouts; Graham Hansen ran the show against Athletic.

Pere Romeu's side answered two draws with two 6-0 wins. On Thursday, November 19, they beat Servette at the Johan Cruyff to secure a place in the Women's Champions League quarter-finals with a game to spare (FC Barcelona). Aitana Bonmatí opened the scoring, Esmee Brugts and Caroline Graham Hansen (penalty) added goals, and Clàudia Pina scored three.

The win took Barça top of the league phase on goal difference over OL Lyonnes. By our count they have 13 points from five games. The last group game is at Bayern Munich on Wednesday, December 16, at 12:00 PM Pacific (UEFA).

Athletic swept aside

Sunday's Liga F game against Athletic Club followed the same script, 6-0 (FC Barcelona). Aïcha Camara scored first from a rebound after a Graham Hansen corner, another Graham Hansen corner produced an own goal, and the Norwegian then scored twice. Ewa Pajor and Mayssa Baha completed it. Ara called Camara the unexpected protagonist (Ara).

Barça go into the break on 31 points from 12 games, four clear of Real Madrid (27) and six ahead of Atlético (25), according to Liga F. There is no club game until Espanyol visit on Tuesday, December 8, at 8:00 AM Pacific, a date that could move because of a clash with the Juvenil A (LaLiga).

International duty and injuries

Spain play Chile in León on Saturday, November 28 (9:00 AM Pacific, provisional), then Venezuela in A Coruña on December 4. Aitana, Patri Guijarro, Clara Serrajordi, Camara and Pina are in Sonia Bermúdez's squad; Irene Paredes and Cata Coll were rested (EFE via Infobae; Prensafútbol). Kerolin is out of Brazil's games against Japan with a foot injury (O Povo), and Laia Aleixandri is out until early 2027 (Ara).

Players Away

The window closes: Cancelo starts, Raphinha watches

In Brief

Pérez in court. Barça and Florentino Pérez, the Real Madrid president, are due at a conciliation hearing in Madrid on Wednesday at 9:40 AM local time, the step before a possible complaint over his comments on the Negreira case. Barça want a retraction (Qué!, citing Mundo Deportivo).

The revenue gap narrows. A study by the Association of Economists of Catalonia puts Barça's 2024-25 revenue at €987m against Real Madrid's €1.161bn, a gap of €174m, down from €303m (Goal, citing Sport).

Camp Nou crowds. Crónica Global puts the current capacity at 62,652 and the average league crowd at 55,527, or 88.6 per cent full (Crónica Global). The club has carried out more than 3,000 structural reinforcements on the second tier, against about 800 planned (Crónica Global).

Espart signs. Academy right-back Xavi Espart, currently injured, has signed a new contract to 2030 (FC Barcelona).

Nine in a month. Barça have eight games left in 2026 (nine counting Villarreal), by the club's count, including Sabah, Deportivo, Atlético and Manchester City (FC Barcelona).
