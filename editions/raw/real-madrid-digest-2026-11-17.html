The Lead

Vinícius returns with five goals, and Mourinho has a choice to make

Madrid come out of the break three points off the top, with three home games in eight days, a bruised midfield and a left wing that suddenly has two strong claims.

The November break was supposed to be a lull for Real Madrid. Instead it has handed José Mourinho the most delicate selection call of his season. Vinícius Júnior, whose four-match domestic ban ended at Mestalla, flies back from Singapore with five goals in two games for Brazil. The man who replaced him, Yan Diomande, has not given the shirt back.

The table explains the stakes. Madrid are second in La Liga on 30 points, three behind Barcelona and two clear of Atlético Madrid after 12 games, per ESPN. They have won eight straight in all competitions since losing 2-1 at Atlético on September 20, and they sit second in the Champions League league phase on 12 points.

Five goals in four days

Vinícius scored both Brazil goals in a 3-2 defeat by Japan in Singapore on November 14, a game in which Carlo Ancelotti and Raphinha were sent off, per Al Jazeera. On Tuesday he added his first hat-trick for Brazil inside the opening 21 minutes of a 7-0 win over Singapore, then went off around the hour, per El Español.

Endrick started that game, scored, and set up Vinícius's second goal, per Goal. The timing is pointed. Vinícius has one goal for Madrid this season. In Singapore he admitted he had "ended up training less than in recent years" and that the summer contract saga overwhelmed him, according to Hipócrita Lector. He renewed until 2032 in August.

“I ended up training less than in recent years.”
Vinícius Júnior, speaking with Brazil in Singapore (translated)

The case for Diomande

Diomande started all four league games of the ban and Madrid won all four. He scored against Sevilla and Racing and his cross led to the first goal of the Clásico win at Barcelona, per Las Voces del Pueblo. He then played 90 minutes and made an assist in Ivory Coast's 2-1 win over Gambia on November 12, per the club.

Mourinho has given no public signal, per Mundiario. He has previously described his handling of Vinícius as "80 per cent soft hand, 20 per cent firm." Rotation is the obvious tool: Celta on Saturday, PSV Eindhoven on Tuesday and Alavés four days later are all at the Bernabéu, and all kick off at 12:00 PM Pacific.

The midfield problem

The bigger worry sits behind the forwards. Aurélien Tchouaméni left France's camp on November 10 with a right adductor injury, in the same area that troubled him at the World Cup, and missed both Nations League games, per Sports Illustrated.

Cadena COPE's early read, relayed by Goal, was a minor problem that should not rule him out. Marca's view, cited by SI en Español, is that the staff will not rush him and his place is not guaranteed. Treat him as a genuine doubt for Celta.

Bernardo Silva also came home early. Portugal released him after he scored a stoppage-time consolation in a 4-1 home defeat by Denmark. Tests in Madrid ruled out an injury and he is expected to be available on Saturday, per Libertad Digital.

Who comes back in what shape

Kylian Mbappé missed France's 3-1 defeat in Italy with hamstring discomfort as a precaution, per 24matins, then played 90 minutes in a 2-1 win over Türkiye. Arda Güler scored a free kick against Belgium and assisted Türkiye's late goal in Bordeaux, per the club.

Jude Bellingham set up goals in both of England's wins. The second was a 3-2 victory over Spain at Atlético's stadium that ended Spain's 43-game unbeaten run, per TSN. Full training resumed at Valdebebas on Tuesday afternoon, with the internationals returning in stages.

The Boardroom

Saturday's assembly: a record budget, a record wage bill and Rafael Nadal

Member delegates meet hours before Celta kicks off. The numbers are big, the agenda is short, and the club won a racism case on Tuesday.

Real Madrid hold their ordinary and extraordinary general assemblies on Saturday, November 21, at the Ciudad Real Madrid pavilion, with first call at 9:00 Madrid time, per the official notice. The agenda covers the 2025/26 accounts, the 2026/27 budget, next season's membership fees and the ratification of Rafael Nadal as a director.

The figures going to the vote

The budget, as reported by 2Playbook and Grada3, projects record total revenue of 1.405 billion euros, of which 1.250 billion is ordinary income and a record 154.8 million euros comes from player sales. Stadium revenue is put at 420.1 million euros.

The sporting cost, wages plus amortisation, rises to a club-record 707.1 million euros. The target net profit is 14.2 million euros, down from 26.3 million in 2025/26, and net debt is projected to fall about 3 per cent to 1.367 billion euros. The adult membership fee for 2027/28 is set at 156.79 euros plus VAT.

Nadal's appointment was proposed unanimously by the board. Zinedine Zidane, asked about it at a press conference on November 14, said Nadal "loves football and, above all, Madrid" and asked "why not see him in another role?", per Fútbol Centroamérica.

A conviction for the abuse of Mbappé

On Tuesday the club announced that the Provincial Court of Asturias had convicted the person who racially abused Mbappé at Real Oviedo's Carlos Tartiere on August 24, 2025, per the official statement. The sentence for a hate crime is eleven months in prison, suspended on condition of a two-year ban from stadiums, plus a fine.

The club acted as private prosecutor. It notes earlier convictions for racist abuse of its players at Mestalla, Son Moix, Vallecas and the José Zorrilla, and says it continues to pursue other cases.

Negreira and Tebas

In the Negreira case, Barcelona have not yet handed over the 2010 to 2021 financial statements and audits that Madrid requested and a Barcelona court partly ordered, per El Debate's Tomás González-Martín via Yahoo Sports. LaLiga president Javier Tebas reopened his feud with the club on X on November 15, saying its history grants "no immunity from criticism," per Iusport.

 
Scouting the Next Opponent

Celta Vigo: seven draws, a long injury list and a 5-0 cup tune-up

Claudio Giráldez's side arrive at the Bernabéu without their main striker and their best creator.

Celta Vigo are 11th in La Liga with 13 points from 12 games: two wins, seven draws and three defeats, with a goal difference of zero, per FotMob. They have scored only 10 league goals. Their last league outing was a 0-0 at home to Levante on November 8.

The break brought one confidence boost: a 5-0 Copa del Rey win at ten-man Salamanca on November 13, with two goals each from Ferran Jutglà and Williot Swedberg, per LiveScore. In Europe they are struggling, with three points from four Europa League games after a 2-1 home defeat by Union Saint-Gilloise, per Plano Informativo.

System and absentees

Giráldez builds patiently from the back with a three-man defence and wing-backs, per Comuniate. Two of the men who make that work will miss the trip: striker Borja Iglesias has a torn left calf, and midfielder Miguel Román, their set-piece taker, has had knee surgery, per FutbolFantasy.

The list of doubts is long. Carl Starfelt is still on the medical report, Álvaro Núñez and El-Abdellaoui have a chance of returning this week, goalkeeper Ionuț Radu has a shoulder problem, and wing-back Javi Rueda and centre-back Javi Rodríguez have carried knocks since Levante, per FutbolFantasy. Nobody is suspended.

Where Madrid can hurt them

Celta do not score enough to punish a side that controls the ball, and a patched-up back three is vulnerable to quick switches. Jutglà and Pablo Durán, with two league goals each, are the men to watch. The history is mixed: Madrid won 2-1 at Balaídos in March, but Celta won 2-0 at the Bernabéu in December 2025.

Transfer News

JJ Gabriel: final stages, and a January start

Fabrizio Romano says the decision is made. Manchester United's grievance and FIFA's rules on minors are what is left.

Real Madrid's pursuit of JJ Gabriel, the 16-year-old Manchester United academy forward, is at "the final stages," Fabrizio Romano said on Tuesday, per Football365. "The decision has been made," he added, with formal steps to follow before a "here we go." Romano says the player's new agents are in Madrid and the target is January.

El Chiringuito's Marcos Benito reports a three-year contract and about 360,000 euros in training compensation to Manchester United, per Madrid Universal via Yahoo. That is single-sourced. El Desmarque adds that he would start with the Juvenil A, because federation rules keep him out of Castilla until January, per El Desmarque.

United have weighed legal action and any move needs FIFA approval for an international transfer of a minor, which Gabriel can clear with an EU passport, per Sports Illustrated. Certainty: advanced talks, agreement reported in principle; nothing signed or official.

January: the door stays shut, the phones keep ringing

No signings planned, no sales wanted. Endrick, Trent and the midfield are where the noise is.

Madrid will not make a January signing and will decide on loanees and buyback options in June, per Defensa Central, echoing AS. That covers Nico Paz and Jacobo Ramón at Como and a possible move for Rafa Marín at Napoli next summer. Tchouaméni's adductor reopens the old question of midfield cover, but there is no reported search.

Corriere dello Sport reported on November 16 that AC Milan are preparing 60 million euros plus 10 million in bonuses for Endrick, with an 80 million buyback clause, per beIN Sports. Mourinho reportedly opposes a sale or a loan. Certainty: rumour; no talks between the clubs reported.

Trent Alexander-Arnold, now third choice at right-back behind Valverde and Dumfries, has "no plans to leave Real Madrid in January," Romano said on November 14, adding there is no contact with Liverpool (Romano on X).

The Squad

A skeleton break at Valdebebas, and who is fit for Celta

Eighteen players away, no regular midfielder at home, and two long-term absentees still in rehab.

With 18 first-team players on international duty, José Mourinho worked through the break with goalkeepers Thibaut Courtois and Andriy Lunin, Álvaro Carreras, Antonio Rüdiger and a group of academy players, per Futbolete and the club's training report. Full sessions resumed on Tuesday afternoon.

Rodrygo (knee) and Raúl Asencio (stress fracture of the right tibia) continued their individual recovery, per the club. Earlier estimates put Rodrygo's return no sooner than late December and Asencio's in January. Ferland Mendy is training normally but has not yet been back in a matchday squad, per FutbolFantasy.

Form and numbers

Mbappé has 17 goals in 16 games in all competitions and has scored in seven straight matches, per Las Voces del Pueblo. The same outlet credits Diomande with four goals and three assists. Güler and Bernardo Silva have become the creative pair in midfield, with Federico Valverde at right-back and wearing the armband.

One disciplinary flag from the break: in Brazil's defeat by Japan, Endrick appeared to push the referee, per AfricaSoccer. He was not booked and no governing body has acted. Single-sourced.

La Fábrica

Castilla win in Tarragona as the cantera waits for minutes

Pol Fortuny's early goal was enough, and Thiago Pitarch made his Spain under-21 debut.

Real Madrid Castilla won 1-0 at Gimnàstic de Tarragona on Friday, November 13, through Pol Fortuny's 11th-minute goal, per the club report. Gimnàstic's Van Rijn was sent off after a VAR review on 56 minutes, and goalkeeper Mestre made the key save on 67.

It was Julián López de Lerma's fifth league win. Castilla are sixth in Primera Federación Group 2 with 19 points from 12 games, eight behind leaders Zaragoza, per Fútbol Total (single-sourced). Real Murcia visit the Alfredo Di Stéfano on Friday, per Real Murcia.

Juvenil A and the first-team door

Álvaro López's Juvenil A had the weekend off, per the club. They lead División de Honor Group 5 with 25 points from nine games, six clear, per BeSoccer. Their next UEFA Youth League game is at home to PSV on November 24, per the club.

EFE, via Infobae, called the academy Mourinho's "great forgotten." Thiago Pitarch's stoppage-time appearance at Valencia is the only debut so far, while Jorge Cestero and Mario Rivas have each sat on the bench five times without playing.

Pitarch did make his Spain under-21 debut, coming on at half-time in a 2-0 win over Japan on November 13, with Carlos Espí introduced on 61 minutes, per the club. Spain's under-21s play in Albania on Tuesday.

On loan

Franco Mastantuono, Madrid's only loanee per SI en Español, sat on Argentina's bench for the full 90 minutes of the 2-1 defeat by Uruguay. He is in contention to start against Costa Rica on Tuesday night before returning to Fiorentina for Sunday's trip to Monza, per Fiorentina News.

 
Real Madrid Femenino

A first Champions League win, then a 3-3 thriller

Pau Quesada's side beat Paris FC, drew with Badalona and host Inter on Wednesday with the top four in sight.

Real Madrid Femenino finally won in Europe on November 10, beating Paris FC 3-0 at the Alfredo Di Stéfano, per the club report. Caruso followed up Felicia Schröder's saved shot on 55 minutes, Lineth Beerensteyn ran onto a Schröder pass on 65, and substitute Linda Caicedo finished another Schröder assist on 81.

The win lifted Madrid to seventh in the league phase with six points from four games (one win, three draws), per Inter. The top four go straight to the quarter-finals.

Badalona: six goals and two crossbars

Saturday's Liga F game against FC Badalona finished 3-3, per the club report. Badalona led twice; goals from Angeldahl, Caicedo and Keukelaar put Madrid 3-2 up on 76 minutes, before Uribe equalised late. Keukelaar had a hand in all three Madrid goals, and Däbritz and Bruun both hit the bar.

Madrid are second in Liga F with 24 points from 11 games, four behind Barcelona and two ahead of Atlético Madrid, per LaLiga. Back-to-back league draws have taken the edge off a run that is still seven games unbeaten. Caicedo, who limped off at Athletic Club, has scored in both games since, per El País.

Inter on Wednesday

Madrid host Inter, 13th on four points, on Wednesday at 9:45 AM Pacific (18:45 in Madrid), per EFE via Infobae. Hungarian referee Katalin Kulcsár takes charge. The league then resumes at Granada on Sunday, per LaLiga.
