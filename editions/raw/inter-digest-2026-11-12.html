The Lead

Inter give Como the ball and take the points

Chivu's side had 43 percent of the ball and still controlled a top-four clash. The break arrives with Inter a point off Lazio, and Atalanta next.

For a team that came into the weekend with the highest average possession in Serie A, Sunday's win over Como was a change of character. Inter let Cesc Fàbregas's side have 57 percent of the ball, struck twice in the first 19 minutes and then protected a 2-0 lead at San Siro with little fuss, according to SempreInter's match data.

Francesco Pio Esposito opened the scoring after 12 minutes, meeting Djed Spence's cross. Seven minutes later Marcus Thuram finished at the near post past Robert Sánchez after Álex Valle failed to deal with Piotr Zielinski's ball across goal (Football Italia, Flashscore). It was Thuram's sixth Serie A goal of the season.

The numbers show how one-sided the contest was despite the possession split. Inter had 14 shots to Como's five and an expected-goals tally of 1.86 against 0.23, with 10 of their attempts from inside the box. Josep Martínez made his first real save after 63 minutes, from a bouncing shot by Nico Paz, who later struck the woodwork on 84 minutes.

Cristian Chivu called it complete. “Today we played well in every aspect,” he said, adding that his players had shown “a performance of maturity and quality” (AFP via theScore). A week earlier, after the derby defeat, he had admitted to “the wrong approach.” Two clean, controlled wins later, that sounds like a long time ago.

“We counter-attacked with ferocity and were clinical in front of goal.”

Cristian Chivu, after Inter 2-0 Como

Why giving up the ball worked

Como are built to keep the ball and to press high, and Inter's own pre-match analysis had flagged the goals they win from turnovers. Chivu's answer was to sit off, keep the middle closed and go direct when the ball was won. Both goals came from quick attacks, before Como had settled; after that, Inter could defend in shape and break when it suited them.

The back three of Manuel Akanji, Alessandro Bastoni and Carlos Augusto kept Anastasios Douvikas, who scored a hat-trick at Lens the previous week, on the fringes. Goal's ratings credited Akanji's positioning with starving him of service. Como arrived on a six-match winning run, per AFP, and left fifth, a point behind Milan.

Esposito, booked for what he called a foul he “had to make,” said he had been waiting for a goal at San Siro but that “the team's performance matters more.” Thuram spoke of “three important points, in a home game in front of our fans and against a very strong opponent” (Inter.it).

The race at the break

Four sides are now within five points. Lazio, still unbeaten under Gattuso, drew 1-1 at Napoli on Sunday, where on-loan Inter midfielder Davide Frattesi scored their goal; they lead on 29 (AGI). Roma are third on 27 after beating Sassuolo 3-0, and AC Milan fourth on 24 after winning 2-1 at Genoa.

Inter's next step is a trip to Bergamo on November 22, followed three days later by Stuttgart in the Champions League. Atalanta are 12th, but Maurizio Sarri's side ended a winless run of almost a month at Monza on Sunday and hope to recover several injured players over the break. Inter expect Lautaro Martínez back, but have lost Ange-Yoan Bonny.

Bonny out, Lautaro due: the front line after the break

A right hamstring strain removes Inter's fourth striker just as the captain nears a return.

Ange-Yoan Bonny came on for Pio Esposito after 73 minutes against Como and finished the match with muscular discomfort. Tests at the Humanitas Institute on November 10 found a strain of the flexors of his right thigh, according to the club bulletin reported by Pazzi di Fanta. Sky Sport's Matteo Barzaghi put the layoff at about three to four weeks.

Bonny withdrew from Ivory Coast's squad for two Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Gambia, and Sturm Graz forward Oumar Diakité was called up instead (YSScores). On Barzaghi's timetable, he is unlikely to face Atalanta or Stuttgart and would be back in early December at the soonest.

The captain should ease the problem. Lautaro Martínez has been out with a left hamstring strain since the 4-3 home defeat by Club Brugge in the Champions League on October 13, and Tuttomercatoweb reported his return would come no earlier than the Atalanta match. Luis Henrique, out with a problem in the long adductor of his right thigh, rejoined group training on Thursday, per Fantacalcio.

Bonny's injury also reopens a debate. Before the Feyenoord match, Beppe Marotta said Inter were not looking for a fifth striker. Tuttomercatoweb's Simone Bernabei wrote on November 2 that a forward with a different profile has been considered for some time (Tuttomercatoweb). With four forwards down to three fit ones, the question will return before January.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Atalanta: Sarri's patched-up side finally win again

Twelfth, injury-hit and only just out of a month-long slump, Atalanta still have the attacking talent to make Bergamo awkward.

Atalanta arrive in an unfamiliar place. Maurizio Sarri's side are 12th on 14 points after 11 games (four wins, two draws, five defeats), with 17 goals scored and 20 conceded, per GidStats. Before Sunday they had gone almost a month without a win in all competitions: three draws and three defeats since October 15 (Football Italia).

That run ended at Monza, where Nikola Krstovic scored after 49 minutes from a parried Lorenzo Bernasconi cross and later hit the woodwork, with goalkeeper Marco Carnesecchi credited for protecting the 1-0 win. A week earlier they had lost 3-0 at home to Parma. They are also in the Conference League, where they drew 1-1 with Kairat Almaty last Thursday.

System and threats

At Monza, Sarri used a 4-2-3-1, per Fantacalcio's line-ups: Carnesecchi; Zappacosta, Scalvini, Bernasconi, Kolasinac; Pasalic, Ederson; Nicola Zalewski, Eljif Elmas, Charles De Ketelaere; Krstovic. De Ketelaere, Zalewski and Elmas carry the creative load behind the striker, with Ederson the engine in midfield.

Injuries have shaped the season. Football Italia listed Gaetano, Bellanova, Kristensen, Hien, Kessié, Raspadori and Kossounou as missing at Monza, although Fantacalcio's log shows Kessié coming on late. Sarri hopes to have Bellanova, Kossounou, Hien and Giacomo Raspadori back for Inter, with Kristensen aiming for the end of the month (Pazzi di Fanta).

Where Inter can hurt them

Recent history favours Inter: a 1-0 win in Bergamo on December 28, 2025 and a 1-1 draw at San Siro on March 14, 2026. 365Scores shows no Atalanta win in the 15 meetings it lists (11 Inter wins, four draws). A defence that has conceded 20 times in 11 games should be vulnerable to the quick, direct attacks that undid Como.

Transfer News

Calhanoglu's future: contact resumed as Turkish interest grows

The midfielder's contract ends in June 2027, and the week brought reports pulling in both directions.

Hakan Calhanoglu is the senior player whose future matters most to Inter's planning. Before the Feyenoord match, Beppe Marotta said the club has “an excellent relationship” with him (Tuttomercatoweb). Last week Sky Sport Italia reported a first meeting with his agent but no offer, with the two sides apart on salary and length.

This week La Gazzetta dello Sport reported that contact has been re-established, while Tuttosport says he could leave next summer. In Turkey, Fanatik reports that Fenerbahçe have turned their attention to him, and Como have also been linked. These reports reached the Digest through the aggregator TransferFeed; the originals were not read in full.

Status: TALKS REOPENED, per Gazzetta. Fenerbahçe: INTEREST, single source (Fanatik). Calhanoglu is with Türkiye this week and plays Belgium in Izmir on Thursday.

 
 
 

A United scout for Pio Esposito, and a Carlos Augusto deal still unsigned

Interest in the young striker is unlikely to go anywhere soon; a renewal that was meant to be done by October is not yet official.

Manchester United sent a scout to San Siro to watch Francesco Pio Esposito score against Como, according to Calciomercato.it as relayed by Indonesia's Bola.com, which says Inter would ask for around 100 million euros. The 21-year-old signed a new contract to 2032 in September (Inter.it), so a January move looks remote. Status: SCOUTING, single source.

Carlos Augusto's renewal to 2031 has still not been announced. On October 23, La Gazzetta dello Sport (via Tuttomercatoweb) expected it by the end of the month, and Nicolò Schira said the player was confident of an agreement (YSScores). The player's own line, quoted by Tuttomercatoweb: “I still have two years of contract, stay calm!” Status: ADVANCED TALKS, announcement overdue.

Thuram, Dimarco and Bastoni

Tuttosport reports initial talks on extending Marcus Thuram's contract, which runs to 2028, and Tuttomercatoweb says Federico Dimarco's camp is negotiating a salary adjustment after Inter exercised their option to 2028. Both via TransferFeed. Status: EARLY TALKS.

On Alessandro Bastoni, Tuttomercatoweb says Inter rebuffed repeated Barcelona approaches in the summer, while a report credited to The Athletic says Barcelona coach Hansi Flick halted a potential move. The Digest could not read The Athletic's original. His contract runs to 2028. Status: RUMOUR, no live talks.

The Squad

A near-full house at the break, with two gaps up front

Stones and Jones are fit again, Luis Henrique is back in the group, and Chivu's 3-5-2 is settling.

Cristian Chivu stayed with the shape that won in Rotterdam: Josep Martínez; Akanji, Bastoni, Carlos Augusto; Spence, Barella, Calhanoglu, Zielinski, Dimarco; Pio Esposito and Thuram. It was the same front pair and the same wing-backs as the 3-0 at Feyenoord, with Bastoni back in place of Bisseck after serving his European ban, and Inter kept a second straight clean sheet.

John Stones and Curtis Jones, back in group training since last week, were on the bench against Como but did not play (Pazzi di Fanta). The two-week break gives Chivu time to build their fitness. Benjamin Pavard came on late for a second successive match.

Barella's armband, Thuram's omission

With Lautaro out, Nicolò Barella captained the side, and Goal rated his ball-winning among the best on the pitch. Marcus Thuram was not named in France's squad for this window either; asked after the match about missing out on the previous one, he said he never gets angry, per Inter.it. Andy Diouf is with France instead.

Inter's goals are spreading. Thuram has six in Serie A, Lautaro four, and Calhanoglu and Pio Esposito three each, per the season statistics compiled on Wikipedia. Carlos Augusto has four in all competitions, a remarkable return for a defender. Inter's 28 league goals are second only to Roma's 31 among the top six.

The Academy

Charlys keeps the Under-23s rolling; Frattesi scores at Napoli

A second straight home win for Vecchi's side, a point for the youth team in Bergamo, and a goal from Inter's most prominent loanee.

Stefano Vecchi's Inter U23 beat Salernitana 1-0 at the Stadio Breda on November 7 in Serie C. Matheus Charlys scored after 41 minutes, set up by Bovio after a pass from Mosconi; an earlier Charlys goal, on 27 minutes, was ruled out after a video review for a Fiordilino handball (Inter.it). It was a second straight home win after defeat at Cosenza.

The Under-20 youth team drew 1-1 at Atalanta in the 10th round of its league, with Putsen scoring after 57 minutes. The Under-18s drew 2-2 at Frosinone, the Under-16s won 5-1 at Vicenza and the Under-19 women beat Como 2-0 in the Coppa Italia Primavera (Inter.it).

Out on loan

Davide Frattesi scored for Lazio at Napoli on Sunday, finishing first time with his left foot from Noslin's pass after 28 minutes; Lazio drew 1-1 and stayed top. AGI rated him 6.5, and he was replaced after 78 minutes (AGI, Flashscore). Inter loaned him to Lazio in August with an option to buy.

Akinsanmiro started for Monza in their 1-0 home defeat by Atalanta, per Fantacalcio's line-ups. The Digest could not verify the other loanees' weekends.

Inter Women

Derby delight, then a European lesson from Manchester City

Bugeja's penalties settled the Milan derby; four days later City showed the gap Inter still have to close in Europe.

Inter Women won 3-0 at AC Milan on Saturday, November 7. Haley Bugeja converted two second-half penalties and Glionna added the other goal, with the minutes listed by Inter as 57, 67 and 83. Milan goalkeeper Estévez was sent off for handling outside her area just after the hour (Inter.it).

“The penalty moment changed everything and quickly after that the red card made it very difficult to create opportunities,” said Milan coach Suzanne Bakker (AC Milan). David Sassarini's side are second on 16 points, unbeaten and yet to concede in six league games, two behind a Roma side that has won all six.

City too strong in Monza

On Tuesday, Manchester City won 4-1 at the Stadio Brianteo in the Women's Champions League. Bartoli put Inter ahead after 24 minutes, but Fujino scored twice before half-time and Casparij and Clinton completed the scoring after 56 and 70 minutes (Inter.it). Inter stay on four points after four league-phase games.

Next up: Juventus at the Stadio Ernesto Breda on Sunday at 3 p.m. in Italy (6:00 a.m. Pacific), with free entry and a free-to-air broadcast on Rai 2 (Inter.it). Then comes Real Madrid away on November 18, and Arsenal at home on December 16 (Inter.it).
