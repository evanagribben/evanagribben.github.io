The Lead

Liverpool find an answer at Selhurst, but the set-piece leak still stings

Two defeats in four days, then a composed 3-1 win at Crystal Palace. Iraola's side go into the break fourth, nine points off Arsenal, with one flaw still glaring.

Ten days ago Liverpool were a side still searching for a settled identity under Andoni Iraola. They still are, but the shape of the problem is clearer now. A 1-0 home defeat to Arsenal on November 1, a 3-2 loss at Fenerbahce on November 4, then a calm 3-1 win at Crystal Palace on Sunday: two steps back and one firm stride forward before the international break.

Arsenal's win at Anfield, their first there since 2012, came from a short goal kick. Odegaard intercepted, Rice crossed and Havertz scored at the back post on 16 minutes. Liverpool did not manage a shot on target all afternoon, AP reported, and the defeat ended a five-match winning run.

What went wrong in Istanbul

Iraola made five changes at the Sukru Saracoglu, resting Isak, Wirtz and Gakpo and using Barcola as a makeshift striker in a front line with no No 9. Liverpool had 68 per cent of the ball, by FotMob's count, and led twice: through Trey Nyoni's deflected volley on 25 minutes and Isak's header with his first touch on 69. They still lost, as liverpoolfc.com reported.

All three Fenerbahce goals came from dead balls. Greenwood's free-kick deflected off Jacquet through the wall on 29 minutes. Archie Brown tapped in at the far post after a corner on 73. Vedat Muriqi, a substitute, rose above Jacquet to head in from a long throw on 79. Liverpool have now conceded 10 goals in four Champions League games, three in each of the last three.

Cheap goals. Messy goals.

Andoni Iraola after Fenerbahce 3-2 Liverpool

"We conceded from a free-kick, a corner, a long throw and that has hurt us," Iraola told reporters, per the Irish Examiner. He added that the noise made it "almost impossible to communicate." Isak was blunter: "We had the lead twice and for us to then lose the game is not good enough," he told the club's website.

The answer at Selhurst Park

The reply at Selhurst Park turned the set-piece theme around. Alexis Mac Allister met Szoboszlai's free-kick at the near post on 40 minutes, his fifth league goal of the season. Florian Wirtz finished Barcola's cutback first time on 54 minutes, ending a 12-game league drought, and Isak sealed it on the counter on 89 before Nketiah's stoppage-time penalty, Sky Sports reported.

It was not a dominant display. Palace had 19 shots to Liverpool's 20, ESPN's match stats showed. Alisson made six saves, including a double stop from Pino and Lerma on 77 minutes, and Pino hit the bar. "It was an important win for us before going into the international break," Iraola said.

Where this leaves Iraola

The win lifted Liverpool back to fourth on 18 points from ten games, nine behind Arsenal, four behind Brighton and three behind Manchester City. Their 13 away points are the most in the division. At home the story is weaker: five points from five games, with defeats to City and Arsenal.

The break arrives at a useful moment for the medical staff and an anxious one for the manager. Szoboszlai went off at Selhurst with a knee problem, Gravenberch (heel) missed the trip, and Frimpong and Bradley are targeting the visit of Manchester United on November 22. United arrive 18th and without a league win since August: an opportunity, and a trap.

Secondary feature

The class of 2027: Liverpool's spine is running out of contract

Van Dijk, Alisson and Gomez are all out of contract in June 2027, and Mac Allister has gone public about his own future.

Behind the week's results runs a quieter question that will shape Iraola's first full year at Liverpool: how much of this team is still under contract in 2027? Captain Virgil van Dijk, goalkeeper Alisson and Joe Gomez all have deals that end in June 2027, according to TEAMtalk's reporting.

On Van Dijk, TEAMtalk's Graeme Bailey reports that Iraola "really wants to keep him around", with talks expected in early 2027 (single-sourced). Alisson told the Liverpool Echo's Paul Gorst: "My agent is talking with the club... my will is to stay," as relayed by Anfield Index. Fabrizio Romano has called an exit next summer "a serious possibility" given interest from several countries, per TEAMtalk.

The most striking words came from Mac Allister, whose deal runs to June 2028. He told Argentine journalist Gastón Edul that "last season I was told that the club's idea was not to renew it, at least for now," calling it a "painful, unpleasant situation," Goal reported. Four days later he headed the opener at Selhurst Park.

Add the Araujo decision, which both sides are reported to be deferring to the end of the season, and the spine of this side is in flux. That in-tray belongs to Julian Ward, who returned as sporting director in September after Richard Hughes left for Al-Hilal, according to LiveScore's report.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Manchester United: dangerous on the break, brittle everywhere else

Michael Carrick's side arrive at Anfield 18th and under review, but they have shown they can come from behind.

Manchester United visit Anfield on Sunday November 22 (4:30pm UK, 8:30am Pacific) sitting 18th on 8 points from 10 games (W1 D5 L4), one point above bottom-placed Palace and without a league win since August, Sky Sports noted. Michael Carrick's position is to be reviewed during the break, and he stayed tight-lipped on his future after the Villa draw, TimesLIVE reported.

Their last five: a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa on November 8, rescued by Dorgu in the 96th minute; a 3-2 Champions League comeback against Roma on November 3; a 2-0 defeat at Chelsea; a 3-0 home loss to Bournemouth on October 25; and a 4-1 defeat at Como on October 21.

System and the players to stop

Without the ball United defend in a zonal 4-2-4 or 4-4-2, pressing in moments, with four players narrow and high waiting for turnovers, according to BBC Sport's analysis.

Bryan Mbeumo has four league goals. Bruno Fernandes scored a penalty against Villa, and Matheus Cunha scored twice against Roma; his red card that night is a UEFA ban, so he should be available at Anfield.

The weaknesses are plain. United conceded twice inside four minutes against Villa and Sky described theirs as the worst defence in Europe's top leagues across all competitions. Opta Analyst flagged errors by goalkeeper Senne Lammens and Leny Yoro.

Carlos Baleba is out for "a number of weeks" with a hamstring injury (ESPN), and Benjamin Šeško (shin) is out until at least January, The Athletic reported. That leaves no natural holding midfielder: exactly the gap Liverpool's counter-attacks exploited at Palace.

History is less comforting. Liverpool have lost the last two meetings, 1-2 at Anfield in October 2025 and 3-2 at Old Trafford on May 3, 2026, per Sports Mole's record.

Transfer News

Araujo's €55m option: both sides wait for the summer

The Barcelona centre-back is thriving on loan, and the decision on a permanent move is reportedly being parked until the end of the season.

Ronald Araujo joined Liverpool on a season-long loan from Barcelona with an option to buy reported at €55m (£47m). Spanish paper SPORT reports that club and player will both wait until the end of the season before deciding, as relayed by TEAMtalk. Status: option in the loan deal; decision deferred (reported).

His form makes the case. Araujo was voted the club's October Player of the Month (liverpoolfc.com) and has mostly started at right-back. TEAMtalk, citing SPORT, also claims Barcelona have floated a package with Alejandro Balde; treat that as an unconfirmed rumour.

Bayern want Ngumoha. Liverpool say no

The 18-year-old winger is wanted in Munich, and Liverpool's answer is a new contract.

Rio Ngumoha is the subject of real interest from Bayern Munich: German reporter Christian Falk says Bayern have held talks with his camp about next summer, via Football365. Fabrizio Romano says selling was "never even an option to negotiate" and that new contract talks are going "quite well", per SportBible. Status: concrete interest from Bayern; Liverpool not selling; renewal talks advanced (reported).

Ngumoha's deal runs to 2028, and TEAMtalk and SportBible report an extension could be signed during this break. He was named in the England squad for the Nations League games against Croatia and Spain.

Finding Van Dijk's successor

Two expensive names are being linked as the captain's contract runs down.

TEAMtalk's James Holland reports Liverpool would pay £85m to £90m for Brighton's 19-year-old Croatia defender Luka Vuskovic; the club's own Media Watch repeated the claim with its usual caveat. Status: rumour.

Chelsea's Levi Colwill is also linked. CaughtOffside says Barcelona have him top of their list, with Liverpool watching ahead of the summer, as reported by The Hard Tackle. Status: rumour.

Contracts to watch

Trey Nyoni's talks over a long-term deal are reported to be advanced by The Athletic's Gregg Evans, via TEAMtalk; nothing is announced. Mac Allister's revelation about renewal, and the 2027 expiries for Van Dijk, Alisson and Gomez, are covered in the secondary feature above.

The Squad

Szoboszlai's knee, Gravenberch's heel and a manager who wants more from referees

Iraola rotates heavily in midweek and is still settling his best attack; the break brings an injury scare and some respite.

Liverpool have mostly lined up as a back four with Alexander Isak through the middle and Wirtz, Barcola and Gakpo around him. Iraola makes "four or five" changes for midweek games, and after trying Barcola as a striker in Istanbul he said the Frenchman will "mainly" stay a winger. Van Dijk captains the side.

Wirtz's first league goal was a relief. "Happy for him because I think he's been very unlucky in the Premier League," Iraola said, pointing to "a couple of goals disallowed," per Hayters. Isak scored in both games this week, sealing the Palace win on the counter on 89 minutes.

The Szoboszlai scare

Dominik Szoboszlai went off on 69 minutes at Selhurst. "I think he extended a little bit the knee, it was fine, but not 100 per cent, so he has to be careful to see if he can play with the national team or not," Iraola said. He was due medical tests on Monday, theScore reported; no result had been published when this edition went to press.

Iraola is also unhappy with officiating. He called his side "sometimes too honest" and, after the late penalty at Palace, said: "I am very upset because the ones in our box they are calling penalties and in the other box they are not," FotMob reported. ESPN cited Opta data showing 58 per cent of fouls in Liverpool's games are given against them, the highest ratio in the league.

Kirkby

Nyoni's breakthrough and a Will Wright hat-trick in Istanbul

The academy had its best night of the week while the first team lost at Fenerbahce.

Hours before the senior game, Liverpool's Youth League side won 5-3 at Fenerbahce. Will Wright scored three, including a penalty, his second Youth League hat-trick of the season, with Josh Sonni-Lambie and Finn Inglethorpe also on target, liverpoolfc.com reported. Rob Page's side are unbeaten in the group (W2 D2) and visit Club Brugge on November 25.

Wright has since earned a first England U19 call-up. In the first team, Nyoni scored with a deflected volley in Istanbul at 19, making him Liverpool's second-youngest Champions League scorer behind Trent Alexander-Arnold, the Irish Examiner noted. He then came off the bench at Palace and was named in the England U21 squad.

The U21s, last seen drawing 2-2 at Chelsea on October 24 through Wright and Jayden Danns (liverpoolfc.com), visit Huddersfield on Tuesday at 7pm UK (11am Pacific).

Loan watch

Harvey Elliott was an unused substitute as Valencia lost 3-0 at home to Real Madrid at the weekend; he had yet to start a game this season as of November 1, RotoWire reported. Kieran Morrison has 13 appearances and three assists at Sheffield Wednesday and is in the Northern Ireland squad.

From The 4th Official's roundup covering late October and early November: Ármin Pécsi made five saves in Hartberg's 4-0 win over Grazer AK; Calum Scanlon was praised in Cardiff's 3-3 draw with West Ham; Keyrol Figueroa scored off the bench for Port Vale; Kyle Kelly scored for Forest Green; Ifeanyi Ndukwe impressed in Levante's 2-0 defeat to Atlético Madrid.

Liverpool FC Women

Liverpool push Arsenal close at Anfield but the wait for a win goes on

Gareth Taylor's side lost 2-1 to Arsenal and have not won in the WSL since late September.

Liverpool FC Women lost 2-1 to Arsenal at Anfield on Sunday. Alessia Russo scored on 9 minutes and again on the stroke of half-time, either side of Beata Olsson's 15th-minute equaliser from a Natalia Ramos through ball, the club reported. Grace Fisk and Jenna Clark made key blocks and Clark had a goal ruled out for offside.

It followed a 4-1 defeat at West Ham on November 1, when Vivien Endemann scored as a half-time substitute (liverpoolfc.com). Liverpool have not won in the league since beating Everton 2-0 on September 27 and sit 12th on 8 points from 9 games (W2 D2 L5), per the WSL table. Manchester City lead on 25.

Next up: Aston Villa in the Subway Players Cup at St Helens on Wednesday (7pm UK, 11am Pacific), then a trip to Brighton on Saturday at 5:15pm UK (9:15am Pacific), moved for Sky Sports in the UK (listings). Chelsea visit on November 21.
