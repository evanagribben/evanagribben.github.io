The Lead

Douvikas alone: Como's striker crisis meets four games in 12 days

Moise Kean's latest tibia setback leaves Tasos Douvikas as the only fit centre-forward, just as Cagliari, AEK Athens, Juventus and Cremonese all visit the Sinigaglia in twelve days.

The international break was supposed to give Como a chance to breathe. It ended with an ambulance. On Tuesday the club said Moise Kean was undergoing “further medical tests” after a problem in training, and ANSA reported that he had been taken to the emergency department at Sant'Anna hospital. Italian reports now expect him back only in 2027.

Kean has not played for Como since September 20. He hurt his tibia on Italy duty against Belgium in late September and had treatment at a clinic in Barcelona, with five weeks the stated target. That target has gone. Tuttosport says he will miss Juventus on November 29 at the very least.

That leaves Cesc Fàbregas with one fit, recognised centre-forward. Tasos Douvikas has 11 goals in 15 games in all competitions, and his six in the Champions League make him the competition’s top scorer. He signed a new contract to 2031 last month. He has rarely looked more indispensable.

Four home games, twelve days

The calendar is the real problem. Como host Cagliari on Saturday and AEK Athens in the Champions League on Tuesday.

Then come Juventus on Sunday, November 29, and Cremonese in the Coppa Italia round of 16 on Wednesday, December 2. All four games are at the Sinigaglia.

Douvikas also spent the break working. He came on for about the last 25 minutes of Greece's 7-0 defeat in the Netherlands and started Monday's 1-0 win over Serbia before being withdrawn after 61 minutes. That is a manageable load. Playing four full matches in twelve days, with no natural deputy, is not.

The cover is thin. Assane Diao is coming back from a grade-one hamstring strain and is expected on the bench at best. Jayden Addai has a thigh problem from the win at Torino, and Jesús Rodríguez is still weeks away. Trevoh Chalobah, a defender, is the one likely return, per Fantacalcio.

Where the goals come from

The good news is that this team has never relied on one finisher. Nico Paz has six goals and leads the Champions League with five assists, and Martin Baturina has five goals. The 9-1 win at Lens on November 4 had seven different scorers, including two teenagers.

One of them was Mattia Liberali, 19, who has now scored in Serie A and in Europe. Another was 18-year-old Matteo Papaccioli on his debut. Without a second No. 9, the obvious lever is more goals from midfield, with Paz and Baturina arriving from deeper and Liberali running beyond. That is the Digest's reading, not a stated plan.

Fàbregas has hinted at the flexibility. After Lens he admitted to “a weakness for left-footed players”, noting that eight are in his squad and five were on the pitch together. It is a squad built for fluid attacking shapes, which may be what saves it until January.

“I’m proud of what the players give me every day.”

The stakes, on both fronts

The break began badly: a 2-0 defeat at San Siro, with Esposito and Thuram scoring inside 19 minutes. Fàbregas called Inter “the strongest in Europe” in transition and conceded “we didn't start the game well.” Inter have now beaten Como in 12 straight Serie A meetings.

Even so, Como are fifth on 23 points, one behind Milan and six off leaders Lazio, per the Serie A table. In Europe they are fourth of 36 with nine points and a +13 goal difference. AEK sit 29th on three. A win on Tuesday would take Como to 12 points with three games left, in the race for a top-eight finish and a direct place in the last 16.

The fix, if there is one, comes in January. Italian reports, led by Gazzetta, say Como are weighing a striker, with Rayo Vallecano's Sergio Camello the leading name (see Transfer News). That is six weeks away. Until then, the season leans on one Greek forward.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Cagliari: one win in five, and leaky on the road

Fabio Pisacane's side arrive ninth, with Daniel Maldini carrying the goals and an injury list that is finally shrinking.

Cagliari have 16 points from 11 games: five wins, a draw and five defeats, with 10 scored and 15 conceded. They are seven points behind Como. Their last five league games bring one win, a 1-0 home win over Frosinone on November 7, after four straight defeats.

Those four defeats were 2-1 at Lazio, 4-2 at Roma, 2-0 at home to Bologna and 4-0 at Monza. Their last three away games have cost them ten goals, which is the vulnerability Como will look to exploit early.

Manager, system and the men to stop

Fabio Pisacane has moved between a back three with wing-backs and a back four, according to TuttoMercatoWeb’s preview, so the shape is hard to call. Up front, Daniel Maldini is the man to watch with five league goals. M'Bala Nzola has two.

The injury list is easing. Unione Sarda reported on November 16 that Yerry Mina and Obert were close to full fitness, and that Nzola, Aurelio and Trepy had rejoined the group. Harry Winks (back) and Alieu Fadera (hamstring), who is on loan from Como, were still training alone.

History and form

Como won 2-1 in Sardinia in March, drew 0-0 at home in November 2025 and won 3-1 in May 2025. Fàbregas is unbeaten in four meetings with Cagliari, two wins and two draws, and Como are unbeaten in six home games in all competitions, per the Playmakerstats preview. The referee is Calzavara.

Transfer News

Camello heads the list as Como plan for life without Kean

The January striker hunt is on. Rayo Vallecano's forward is the name most often linked, with Gianluca Scamacca the Serie A alternative.

Kean's injury has turned a quiet window into an urgent one. Gazzetta dello Sport, via GOAL Italia, reported on November 18 that Como are considering Sergio Camello, the 25-year-old Rayo Vallecano striker with seven goals and two assists in 11 Liga games. His contract runs to June 2027.

From Spain, Matteo Moretto told Radio Marca, as reported by Calciomercato.com on November 19, that Camello “will definitely leave” Rayo but that there is no agreement on the table. The asking price is about €10 million, and Marseille are also interested. Certainty: concrete interest. No bid is confirmed.

Sporting director Carlalberto Ludi said Como would “evaluate opportunities in attack”. The alternative named by Gazzetta and Corriere della Sera is Atalanta's Gianluca Scamacca, with Juventus a possible rival. Certainty: rumour/link.

Real Madrid and the €80m question over Nico Paz

Mundo Deportivo says Madrid will trigger the buy-back next summer. Como say Paz is theirs, for now.

Nico Paz chose a good week to be brilliant. Two days after his two goals for Argentina, Mundo Deportivo, via GOAL, reported that sources close to Real Madrid's management say the club will pay €80 million to bring him back next summer. Certainty: rumour/link, single-sourced. There is no official decision.

The structure matters. Como paid Madrid €60 million for its 50 per cent of Paz's rights last summer, having bought the other half for €6 million in 2024. Infobae reports that the buy-back can only be used from July 1 to 15, 2027, that Paz must agree terms with Madrid, and that he could choose to stay.

Ludi's line is unchanged: “Nico Paz is 100 per cent a Como player. But Real Madrid retain the right to buy him back until a certain date.” Infobae, citing The Sun, also lists Manchester United, Arsenal, Chelsea and PSG as admirers. Certainty: rumour.

Kean's Fiorentina deal: what the obligation says

The injury does not appear to change the price.

Calciomercato.com gives the terms of Kean's move from Fiorentina as a €5 million loan fee, a €30 million obligation to buy and up to €5 million in bonuses. TuttoMercatoWeb, via FirenzeViola, says the obligation is triggered by Como's first point after the start of February, a condition tied only to results. Certainty: reported terms, not official.

What Inter exposed

The 2-0 loss at San Siro was Como's first league defeat since September 20. They were 2-0 down after 19 minutes and never recovered their usual control. Fàbregas told DAZN the team “weren't confident” early on, a rare admission from a coach whose side usually dictates games.

Before that, Como had won six in a row in all competitions. The defence, with Jacobo Ramón scoring as well as defending, had conceded four goals in the six games before Inter. Ramón has three goals this season, two of them in Serie A.

Roles and the captaincy

Lucas Da Cunha wears the armband and scored the penalty that beat Venezia on November 1. Liberali's rise is the story of the autumn: his first Serie A goal at Fiorentina on October 18 was followed by another at Lens. With Kean out, expect Baturina and Paz to keep drifting into the box.

The Academy

Primavera surge, a Youth League fight and a debut to remember

Gianluca Falsini's under-20s have won two straight in Primavera 1, while Matteo Papaccioli's first-team debut produced a goal.

Como's Primavera play in Primavera 1 under Gianluca Falsini. They beat Atalanta 2-1 on October 31, with goals from Fustini and Albini, then came from 2-0 down to win 3-2 at Fiorentina on November 8. Ballone, Zamagni and Cassano scored.

The latest published table had them eighth on 15 points after ten rounds. That table may not include the Fiorentina win, so treat the position as approximate.

UEFA Youth League

The under-19s have drawn 1-1 with Leipzig, lost 1-0 at Feyenoord, beaten Manchester United 2-1 (Papaccioli and Albini) and lost 2-1 at Lens (Cassano). Four points leave them 24th of 36. They host AEK Athens on Tuesday at 6:00 a.m. Pacific, per Calciomercato.com (single source).

The debutant

Matteo Papaccioli, 18, is a Brazilian-born midfielder who tore a cruciate ligament in January and only returned on October 10, per Il Giorno. At Lens he came on after 61 minutes and scored in stoppage time, assisted by Liberali. Riccardo Cassano, born in 2009, trained with Italy's senior squad this month.

Women's Football

Como 1907 win the lake derby, Como Women brace for the leaders

Selena Mazzantini's newly promoted side are fourth in Serie A Women; the separately owned Como Women host Roma on Sunday.

A note on names. Como 1907 Women are the club's own team: promoted as Serie B champions in 2025-26 and coached by Selena Mazzantini, per their club record. Como Women are a separate club, owned since 2024 by the women's multi-club group Mercury/13. The Digest covers both.

On November 15 the derby went one way: Como 1907 3-0 Como Women, with Bonfantini and De Rita scoring before half-time and Wullaert adding the third. Como 1907 are fourth on 14 points from seven games in DAZN’s provisional table, ten behind leaders Roma; the derby report had them third, as not every side has played the same number of games.

Como Women

Paolo Tramezzani's side beat Milan 1-0 on November 1 through Veronica Bernardi, then lost 1-0 at Parma a week later before the derby defeat. They are sixth on eight points and host leaders Roma on Sunday at 3:30 a.m. Pacific, per Calciomercato.com.

Next up for Como 1907

Como 1907 travel to Fiorentina, third on 16 points, on Sunday, November 22, per BetExplorer. The kickoff time is not yet confirmed. They then host Parma on December 13.

Players Away

Twelve on duty, and Nico Paz lights up Argentina's first week after Messi

Thirteen Como players were called up, per Calcio e Finanza; Kambwala withdrew, leaving twelve. Here is how each fared.

Nico Paz (Argentina) started the 2-1 defeat to Uruguay at La Bombonera on November 13, Argentina’s first game since Messi’s retirement. Four days later he scored twice, in the 7th and 39th minutes, of a 7-0 win over Costa Rica, before making way at half-time. ANSA said he lit up the post-Messi Argentina.

Máximo Perrone started against Costa Rica and was not substituted, per La Nación.

Nations League

Lucas Da Cunha started both of France's games: a 3-1 defeat to Italy at San Siro and a 2-1 win over Turkey in Bordeaux.

For Italy, Samuele Ricci came on in stoppage time against France and was not in the squad for the 4-1 loss in Belgium. Mattia Liberali, on his first call-up, did not play.

Martin Baturina played 90 minutes as Croatia lost 2-1 to England and 74 in a 2-1 win over Czechia. Ivan Smolčić started at Wembley, conceded the foul for England's penalty, was booked and was replaced after 76 minutes.

Tasos Douvikas came off the bench in Greece's 7-0 defeat in the Netherlands and started the 1-0 win over Serbia, leaving after 61 minutes.

Spain, Peru and the rest

Jacobo Ramón earned a first senior Spain call-up but the federation's reports show no minutes, including the 2-0 win in Prague. Álex Valle started both Spain under-21 games, a 2-0 win over Japan and a 0-0 draw in Albania.

Francesco Andrealli, 19, was called up by Peru for the first time; Peru beat Bolivia 2-0, but we could not confirm whether he played. Willy Kambwala withdrew from the DR Congo squad. No injuries on international duty were reported.

Off the Pitch

Stadium plans, sustainability talk and a new brand

President Mirwan Suwarso says the revised Sinigaglia project is ready; the paperwork tells a slower story.

In a November 3 interview with La Provincia di Como, reported by Calcio e Finanza and Di Marzio, Mirwan Suwarso said the revised stadium project was ready to submit to the city and that he would start work “even tomorrow”. He aims for financial sustainability within two years.

He also said that “sooner or later, sooner rather than later” Como will have to sell players, naming Atalanta as the model, and that the club backs Adriano Galliani for president of the Italian federation.

The Sinigaglia

The history is less tidy. ComoZero reported in September that the club missed a July 2 deadline to file updated documents. The redesign cuts capacity to about 14,000. For now, a rebuilt home curva gives the ground 10,875 seats for the Champions League and 12,370 for Serie A.

Business

FashionNetwork reports that Como are preparing to launch the Curva project and a new brand, with 2026 revenue projected to rise 57.9 per cent. We could not read the full article to confirm the details. Revolut is the front-of-shirt sponsor this season, with Uber on the back.
