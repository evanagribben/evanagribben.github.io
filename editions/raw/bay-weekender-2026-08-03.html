At a Glance
This is the heaviest four weeks San Francisco will see all year, and it front-loads hard. Outside Lands takes Golden Gate Park Friday through Sunday — already sold out at every GA tier — while Pistahan fills Yerba Buena, the Sonoma County Fair opens a ten-day run in Santa Rosa and San Jose Jazz Summer Fest puts a hundred artists on eight downtown stages. Then the week everyone forgets to plan for: Stern Grove closes its season with Public Enemy on a Saturday and Al Green on a Sunday, both free, both in the best outdoor room in the region. The back half of the month belongs to the trails and the ballpark — the Double Dipsea, the Angel Island Half and a six-game Giants homestand — with the Pokémon World Championships parking itself at Moscone for the last weekend. Below: 24 events across the three pillars, San Francisco first and the rest of the Bay right behind it.
Weekend outlook · Fri Aug 7 – Sun Aug 9
No live National Weather Service pull was available at press time, so treat this as pattern guidance rather than a forecast — check weather.gov/mtr the morning of. Early August in San Francisco is the deep marine-layer stretch: fog over the western half of the city until late morning, a few hours of sun, and the whole thing sliding back in around 5–6pm with real wind behind it. Plan on upper 50s to mid 60s at the Polo Field, 70s to 80s inland at Santa Rosa and San Jose. Rain contingency: measurable rain in the first half of August is climatologically close to zero here — what ruins an outdoor day in SF in August is wind and cold, not water. For Outside Lands, Stern Grove, Pistahan and the Dahlia show: a windproof layer, not a poncho. For the Perseids on Aug 12–13 the fog is the forecast problem — go inland or high.

Pick of the Month
Stern Grove Festival’s closing weekend — Public Enemy, then Al Green
Sat Aug 15 & Sun Aug 16 · 2pm · Sigmund Stern Grove, 19th Ave & Sloat Blvd, Sunset · Free
Why this and not Outside Lands: Outside Lands is the bigger headline and it is gone — every general-admission tier sold out. The Grove is the one still gettable, and it is not a consolation prize. Two acts of this stature, on consecutive days, for free, in a 3,000-capacity amphitheater sunk into a eucalyptus grove, happens once a year and only here. Public Enemy landing on a Saturday in an otherwise Sunday-only series is itself unusual, and the supporting bill — Vallejo’s LaRussell and KPFA’s Davey D — is a Bay-specific lineup no tour would reproduce. The next afternoon Al Green closes the season with Goapele and the Glide Memorial choir. No dynamic pricing, no resale market: the festival hands out 1,500 tickets per concert in person through its Community Box Office, two per person, to whoever shows up.
How to actually get in: both lotteries have closed (July 4 and July 5). Watch sterngrove.org for the Community Box Office distribution locations and go early; reserved tables for up to ten are sold separately. Getting there: the K/M lines and the 23 and 28 buses stop within a few blocks and there is essentially no parking. Weather: the Grove sits in a hollow and holds cold air all afternoon — a blanket and a real jacket, even if it is sunny at 19th and Sloat.

Eight picks — trails, ballparks, meteors and a roller disco
Outdoors
Wed–Thu, Aug 12–13 · after midnight
Perseid meteor shower peak
The best meteor shower of the year peaks overnight. The catch is always the same here: you have to get above or east of the marine layer.
Best from Mt. Tamalpais, Point Reyes or Chabot Regional Park · Free (some parks charge for parking) · Peak 1–4am · If fog is in at the coast, drive inland — the East Bay ridges are the fallback. Dress for 45°F.

Sat, Aug 22 · morning start
★ Double Dipsea — Stinson Beach
The Dipsea Trail run backwards and then forwards: 13.7 miles and roughly 2,200 feet of stairs and singletrack, with handicap starts. One of the oldest trail races in America.
Start/finish Stinson Beach, Marin County · Entry fee; permit-capped and historically closes well before race day · Highway 1 over Panoramic; weekend transit does not run early enough · Coastal fog on one side, full sun on the other.

Sat–Sun, Aug 29–30
★ Angel Island Half Marathon & 10K
Ferry out of Tiburon, run the perimeter road and fire roads with the whole Bay below you, ferry back. A course you cannot run any other weekend.
Angel Island State Park; ferry from Tiburon · Entry fee includes the ferry on most tiers · The ferry is a hard cutoff and Tiburon parking is scarce — arrive 45 minutes early · Exposed and windy on the west side.
