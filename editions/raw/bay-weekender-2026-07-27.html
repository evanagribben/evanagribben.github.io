AT A GLANCE

A rare warm, blue-sky week sets up a stacked Saturday, August 1: Jerry Day in McLaren Park, the Nihonmachi Street Fair filling Japantown all weekend, the new-ish AAPI On The Bay festival on Treasure Island, and night two of the Dark Star Orchestra's Rex Foundation benefit at the Greek — with the Violent Femmes closing Sunday at Stern Grove (sold out; picnic outside the gates and you'll still hear it). The Giants host the Brewers Mon–Wed to start the week. Then August 6–9 remains the month's feral stretch — Outside Lands, San Jose Jazz Summer Fest, Pistahan, the Aerial Arts Festival, the Gravenstein Apple Fair and two county fairs at once — and the back half of the window brings a run of neighborhood night markets, the Stern Grove finale (Public Enemy Aug 15, Al Green Aug 16), and a new anchor for the last weekend: the 37th Oakland Chinatown StreetFest, Aug 22–23.

Weekend weather: Unusually kind. After a gray-edged start to the week (highs 65–67°F), Friday clears to ~69°F and Saturday Aug 1 should touch 72°F in the city — genuinely warm for an SF August — with Sunday near 69°F, lows ~57°F, and zero rain chances across the whole stretch. For once the outdoor slate needs sunscreen, not a rain plan; Jerry Day and Nihonmachi should be glorious. (Point forecast checked Mon Jul 27 — the fog line can still move; Treasure Island and the Sunset run cooler.)

⏰ Act-now clock: San Jose Jazz Summer Fest passes get more expensive Saturday — the festival's own site says prices rise August 1, so buying this week is real money saved (summerfest.sanjosejazz.org). Stern Grove's finale: Aug 2, 9 and 16 are all marked sold out on the lineup page, but Public Enemy (Sat Aug 15) still had reserved tables on sale at press time and released tickets do surface — watch sterngrove.org/ticket-info. Outside Lands is sold out at every tier; waitlist only (sfoutsidelands.com). Thee Sacred Souls at the Greek (Aug 15) is sold out — resale or nothing. And Daniel Caesar's two Chase Center nights (Aug 20–21) are on sale now — the Friday will go first.

★ PICK OF THE MONTH
San Jose Jazz Summer Fest — the big weekend you can still get into
Fri–Sun Aug 7–9 · Plaza de César Chávez & a dozen downtown San Jose stages · Day passes on sale — prices rise Aug 1 · All ages
Why this one: everything else that weekend is a closed door — Outside Lands is sold out, Stern Grove's lotteries have come and gone — while Summer Fest is the Bay's best-value marquee festival with tickets simply for sale. The 2026 bill: Zapp ft. Tuxedo, The Soul Rebels, Adi Oasis, Joe Bataan and Thee Sinseers on Friday; Patti LaBelle Saturday (her Stern Grove show the next day is sold out — this is the seeable set), plus Parlor Greens; Trombone Shorty & Orleans Avenue, Lakecia Benjamin and Cory Wong on Sunday — with Latin, blues and salsa-dancing stages running all three days. Skip the parking garages: Caltrain or VTA light rail to downtown San Jose and walk. Warm-weather bonus: San Jose runs 10–15 degrees warmer than the fog belt. Lineup & passes: summerfest.sanjosejazz.org/lineup · tickets (buy before Aug 1)

MUSIC · ARTS · NIGHTLIFE
MUSIC
Stern Grove Festival — the last four shows — the free summer institution in its eucalyptus amphitheater winds down its 89th season: Sun Aug 2 Violent Femmes w/ Tune-Yards, Sun Aug 9 Patti LaBelle w/ Destani Wolf, then the Big Picnic finale weekend — Sat Aug 15 Public Enemy w/ LaRussell and Sun Aug 16 Al Green w/ Goapele & the Glide Ensemble. Sigmund Stern Grove, 19th Ave & Sloat, SF. Free by GA lottery — Aug 2, 9 and 16 are marked sold out; Aug 15 reserved tables were still on sale at press time, and released tickets surface on the ticket-info page. All ages. L Taraval or 23/28 Muni; don't drive. Lineup · Ticket info good for: legends, lawn picnics, fog-blanket soul
Outside Lands 2026 — the year's marquee blowout: Charli XCX, Rufus Du Sol and The Strokes top 90+ acts plus the Wine Lands/Outside Clams food universe. Fri–Sun Aug 7–9 · Golden Gate Park (Polo Field entrances on Fulton/MLK). GA, GA+ and VIP sold out including single days — waitlist and resale only. All ages. No festival parking: Muni 5/N or the shuttles. Lineup & waitlist good for: the one big splurge — if the waitlist smiles on you
Dark Star Orchestra — two nights for the Rex Foundation — the Grateful Dead interpreters headline the Greek's annual benefit for the Dead's own charity: Fri Jul 31 with Melvin Seals & JGB, Sat Aug 1 with Peter Rowan & the Sam Grisman Project — and Saturday pairs perfectly with Jerry Day across the bay that afternoon. Greek Theatre, UC Berkeley (East Bay). Tickets on sale. Downtown Berkeley BART + uphill walk. Tickets good for: a full Dead-family weekend
★ Jerry Day 2026 — the Excelsior's homegrown Garcia tribute in the amphitheater named for its own Jerry; an SF tradition tourists never find, and this year it lands on a 72-degree Saturday. Sat Aug 1, 11:30am–5:45pm · Jerry Garcia Amphitheater, McLaren Park, SF. Free, all ages. Limited parking — 29 Sunset or 54 Felton. Details good for: tie-dye, community, then DSO at the Greek after
Lionel Richie & Earth, Wind & Fire — “Sing a Song All Night Long” — two catalogs of pure joy in one arena night. Thu Aug 6, 7:30pm · Chase Center, Mission Bay, SF. Tickets on sale. T Third to the door. Tickets good for: multigenerational group outings
Meghan Trainor — The Get In Girl Tour — the indoor pop counterweight to Outside Lands weekend. Sat Aug 8, 6:30pm · Chase Center, SF. Tickets
Tedeschi Trucks Band — Future Soul Tour — the 12-piece soul-rock juggernaut with Lukas Nelson opening, in the Bay's best outdoor room. Wed Aug 12 · Greek Theatre, Berkeley (East Bay). Tickets on sale. Tickets good for: a midweek show worth the school-night
Thee Sacred Souls — the sweet-soul revivalists with LA LOM and the Womack Sisters — Sat Aug 15 at the Greek Theatre, Berkeley. Sold out — verified resale only, and note it's the same night as Public Enemy at Stern Grove. Venue page
Daniel Caesar — Son of Spergy Tour — the R&B headliner's two-night Chase Center stand closes the window's concert slate. Thu–Fri Aug 20–21, 7:30pm · Chase Center, SF. On sale now — Friday will sell first. Tickets good for: date night, late-August plans made early
Helado Negro + Reyna Tropical, free on the Great Lawn — the Yerba Buena Gardens Festival's late-summer coup: dreamy Latin electronic pop, outdoors, gratis. Sat Aug 15, 2–4:30pm · Great Lawn, Yerba Buena Gardens, SoMa, SF. Free, all ages. Details good for: an afternoon warm-up before Public Enemy
ARTS

Sat–Sun Aug 1–2 & 8–9 (opening night Aug 8), 6pm at Memorial Park Amphitheater, Cupertino (South Bay); Aug 15–16 & 22–23, 6pm at Red Morton Park, Redwood City (Peninsula), running there through Aug 30 before SF parks in September. Free, all ages; bring low chairs and layers. Schedule good for: golden-hour picnic theater

Fri Aug 7 8pm; Sat Aug 8 1–9:30pm; Sun Aug 9 11am–8:15pm · Cowell Theater & Gateway Pavilion, Pier 2, Fort Mason, Marina, SF · $15–75. Program & tickets good for: the anti-festival festival — jaw-dropping, crowd-free

“Hairspray” at SF Playhouse — the big-hearted Broadway musical in an intimate 199-seat house; matinees and evenings most days all summer. Ongoing · SF Playhouse, 450 Post St, Union Square · from ~$47. Tickets good for: fog-proof evenings
Martinez Art in the Park Festival — a low-key East Bay art fair in a shaded downtown square, an easy pairing with the waterfront. Sun Aug 16, 10am–5pm · Susana Park, Martinez (East Bay). Free. Details

FOOD · DRINK · FESTIVALS
FOOD
Head West Marketplace at the Ferry Building — the first-Sunday makers-and-food market along the Embarcadero waterfront. Sun Aug 2, 11am–5pm · Ferry Building, SF. Free to browse. Details good for: a sunny-Sunday waterfront wander
Off the Grid: Friday night market at Fort Mason — 20+ food trucks, bars and bay views; the classic SF Friday warm-up (and the Aerial Arts Festival is next door on Aug 7). Fridays, 5–10pm · Fort Mason Center, Marina, SF. Free entry. Details good for: zero-planning Fridays
★ Gravenstein Apple Fair — Sonoma County's “sweetest little fair” under the oaks of Ragle Ranch: heritage Gravenstein apples, cider tastings, farm demos, live bands. A west-county institution worth the drive — and the escape hatch from Outside Lands weekend. Sat–Sun Aug 8–9, 10am–6pm · Ragle Ranch Regional Park, Sebastopol (Sonoma). Ticketed — buy ahead online; kids discounted. Tickets good for: apple-pie pilgrims, kids, cider people

Sonoma County Fair — ten days of wine-country fair season: local wine and cider pours, fair food, free live music, the Wine Country Rodeo and the famous Hall of Flowers. Fri Aug 7 – Sun Aug 16 · Sonoma County Fairgrounds, Santa Rosa (North Bay). Admission ticketed; check site for hours/prices. Details good for: a proper day trip north
Napa Town & Country Fair — Napa's hometown fair at the Expo: carnival rides, livestock, local wine & beer, and free-with-admission concerts topped by country hitmakers Lonestar Saturday night. Thu–Sun Aug 6–9; gates 3pm Thu–Fri, 1pm Sat–Sun; Lonestar Sat 7:30pm · Napa Valley Expo, 575 Third St, Napa. Ticketed — on sale now. Tickets & lineup good for: fair food with a wine-country accent
FESTIVALS
52nd Nihonmachi Street Fair — Japantown's flagship summer fair: taiko, food booths, small-business vendors, one of the city's oldest community-run festivals — on what should be the warmest weekend of the season. Sat–Sun Aug 1–2, 11am–6pm · Post & Buchanan, Japantown, SF. Free. Details good for: takoyaki in the sun
AAPI On The Bay: Islander Roots Festival — an afternoon-into-night AAPI food and music festival on Treasure Island, with the city skyline as the backdrop. Sat Aug 1, 2–9pm · Treasure Island, SF · from ~$22. Muni 25 from downtown; the island runs cooler than the city — bring a layer for sunset. Details good for: skyline golden hour
33rd Pistahan Parade & Festival — the West Coast's largest celebration of Filipino food, dance and music; the lumpia and lechon alone justify the trip. Sat–Sun Aug 8–9, 11am–5pm; parade Sat 11am down Market St · Yerba Buena Gardens, SoMa, SF. Free. Details good for: the city's best free festival weekend
Bay Area Aloha Festival — the Peninsula's big Pacific Islander gathering: hula and Tahitian dance stages, island food, craft vendors. Sat Aug 8 (has run as a full weekend in past years — check before a Sunday trip) · San Mateo County Event Center, San Mateo (Peninsula). Free ($5 suggested donation). Details
Live on Larkin — Little Saigon's free street fest of food stalls, music and community groups — a Tenderloin counter-programming gem on the busiest Saturday of the month. Sat Aug 8, 12–5pm · Larkin St (Ellis to Eddy), SF. Free. Details
37th Oakland Chinatown StreetFest — the East Bay's biggest late-summer street festival returns: two days of food stalls, lion dancing, cultural performances and vendors across Chinatown's core blocks. Sat–Sun Aug 22–23 (hours not yet posted) · 9th & Franklin area, Oakland Chinatown (East Bay). Free. Lake Merritt or 12th St BART. Details good for: the last weekend's anchor plan
OUTDOORS · SPORTS · ACTIVE
OUTDOORS
The warm-weekend window (Jul 31–Aug 2) — with the city forecast near 72°F and zero fog-drip in sight, this is the weekend to do the exposed classics that are usually misery in August: Lands End to the Sutro Baths, the Marin Headlands' Coastal Trail, or Angel Island by ferry. No event ticket required — just go before the fog remembers where it lives. good for: the hike you keep postponing
Free National Parks Day — entrance fees waived at every national park site: Muir Woods (still requires a parking/shuttle reservation), the Hyde Street Pier ships, Yosemite and beyond. It's a Tuesday — the crowd-dodger's day to go. Tue Aug 25 · all NPS sites. Free. Details
