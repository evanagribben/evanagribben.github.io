At a Glance

Five weeks from the Moon Festival to the pumpkin fields

This edition runs five weeks, from the middle of September to the middle of October, and it is the busiest stretch of the Bay Area year. The first weekend alone has the Greek Festival, Oktoberfest on Front, the Autumn Moon Festival in Chinatown, the Haight Street Fair and Comedy Day, every one of them free to walk into.

The last weekend of September is the loudest. Portola fills Pier 80 on September 26 and 27, Folsom Street closes on the 27th, and the Giants play their final home series against the Dodgers. If you would rather be somewhere quieter, the Presidio hosts a free Chuseok harvest festival and Pleasanton hosts Corgi Con.

October opens with the best free weekend of the year. An all-star tribute to Steve Earle at the Masonic on October 1 leads into three days of Hardly Strictly Bluegrass, and the Castro Street Fair follows on the Sunday. Fleet Week arrives on October 4, with the Blue Angels over the Marina from October 9 to 11.

Sport is everywhere. The Valkyries close the regular season with two home nights and head into the playoffs as one of the best teams in the league. The Warriors play three preseason games, and the Roots say goodbye to the Coliseum on October 10. 12 of the 40 listings are outside San Francisco, from Half Moon Bay to Lake Merritt.

The weekend read

The first weekend is a Mission and Chinatown weekend. Start at the Greek Festival on Valencia on Friday, walk to Front Street for Oktoberfest, and save Saturday for the Autumn Moon Festival on Grant Avenue.

Sunday September 20 is the day to be in or near Golden Gate Park: Flower Piano closes, Comedy Day is in Robin Williams Meadow, and the Haight Street Fair is a short walk away. Bay FC and the Earthquakes are both in the South Bay that weekend if you want sport instead.

Mid-September is usually the warmest part of the San Francisco year, but the fog still comes in by late afternoon in the west of the city. Carry a layer to anything in the park.

Pick of the Month

Hardly Strictly Bluegrass

Friday October 2 to Sunday October 4, 2026·Hellman Hollow, Golden Gate Park, San Francisco·Free. No tickets, no wristbands.

It is the second edition running that Hardly Strictly has been our pick, and the reason has not changed: it is the largest free music festival in the country, it is in our own park, and it is free forever because Warren Hellman endowed it that way. This is its 26th year.

The 2026 bill is as strong as any in recent memory. My Morning Jacket, Gillian Welch and David Rawlings, Mavis Staples, Emmylou Harris, Alison Krauss and Union Station, Los Lobos, Marty Stuart, Old Crow Medicine Show, Sierra Hull, Jesse Welles, Hiss Golden Messenger and Kathleen Edwards are all confirmed.

The night before, the Masonic hosts An Evening Honoring Steve Earle, with Earle himself, Emmylou Harris, Jackson Browne, Hot Tuna and Molly Tuttle, benefiting Sweetwater Spectrum. If you can get a ticket, do the pair.

Practical advice: do not drive. The N Judah, the 5 Fulton and the 7 all serve the park and all will be full. Walk in from the east, pick one stage and stay put for a few sets, and bring a layer for when the fog arrives around four.
