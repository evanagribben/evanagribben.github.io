The Lead

Simeone reaches 345 and Atlético find their edge again

A 3-0 win at Getafe made Diego Simeone LaLiga's winningest coach. Now a side sitting 23rd in the Champions League must turn domestic control into European points against Viking.

There was no ceremony at the Coliseum on Sunday night, just a 3-0 win that looked routine and was not. With it, Diego Simeone reached 345 LaLiga victories as a coach and moved past Luis Aragonés, whose mark of 344 he had matched at Levante on October 31, as the competition’s all-time leader.

It also settled a question that had hung over Atlético through the international break. Two home defeats in five days, 2-0 to Bayern Munich in the Champions League and 2-1 to Barcelona in the league, had cooled an autumn that brought six wins in seven matches. At Getafe, that earlier side reappeared.

Álex Baena set the tone after ten minutes with a low drive from around 30 yards, set up by Julián Álvarez. Álvarez supplied the second too: goalkeeper David Soria could not hold his delivery on the half-hour and Johnny Cardoso tapped in. Alejandro Grimaldo completed the scoring from the spot in stoppage time.

That penalty came after Djené brought down Alexander Sørloth; the Getafe defender was sent off after a VAR review. The numbers were lopsided. Atlético held 68.8% of the ball and, by LaLiga’s count, took 20 shots to Getafe’s 11. One match report recorded no Getafe attempt on target at all. It was Getafe’s first home league defeat of the season.

Run: last five wins all clean sheets

Simeone’s verdict was that his team “read well how to press and how to hurt Getafe,” and that the lead could have been bigger by half-time, per futbolcentroamerica. The win kept Atlético third on 31 points, two behind Real Madrid and five behind Barcelona.

A defence that has stopped conceding

The deeper trend is at the back. Atlético’s last five wins, against Manchester United, Espanyol, Deportivo, Levante and Getafe, all came with a clean sheet, a run Europa Press highlighted on the eve of the Viking game. Ten goals conceded in 13 league matches is the foundation of the push.

That has come without Cristian Romero, the summer signing from Tottenham, who has missed six games with a muscle problem, and with Clément Lenglet and José María Giménez out on loan. Robin Le Normand and David Hancko have carried the load, with Marcos Llorente at right-back while Marc Pubill recovers from an ankle sprain.

Baena in form, the forwards waiting

Baena’s goal was his sixth in the league, which makes him Atlético’s top LaLiga scorer ahead of Grimaldo (five) and Giuliano Simeone (four), per FotMob. He also scored for Spain against the Czech Republic during the break.

The other side of the ledger is up front. Simeone said he is waiting for Álvarez, Sørloth and Ademola Lookman “to show up again, because they have goals in them.” Sørloth has not scored this season. Lookman is out for about a month with a knee sprain picked up with Nigeria. Álvarez, at least, arrived back from Argentina with three goals in two games.

Twelve points to play for

The record will matter less by Wednesday night. Atlético are 23rd of 36 in the Champions League table with four points from four games, just inside the play-off places (9th to 24th). Viking, who visit the Metropolitano, are 22nd on the same points. “We know we only have four points,” Koke said on Tuesday. Simeone added: “There are still four games left, 12 points at stake.”

Last season offers a warning. Atlético needed a home win over Bodø/Glimt in the final league-phase match to reach the top eight; the Norwegians won 2-1 at the Metropolitano and Atlético were pushed into the knockout play-offs. Europa Press notes it is the only one of Atlético’s seven games against Norwegian sides that they failed to win.

“Tomorrow, Viking is my next record. I have no other deadline.”

Diego Simeone, Nov 24 press conference (translated)

There is a subplot. Simeone’s contract runs to June 2027, and the club spent the week insisting nothing has changed. President Enrique Cerezo said on November 19 there was “nothing, for or against” on a renewal. Sporting director Mateu Alemany said before Getafe that there was no need “to set deadlines”. Simeone’s answer was a line about Viking.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Viking bring goals, and leave the back door open

Norway’s champions have scored in 24 straight matches but have not kept a European clean sheet.

Viking FK are the reigning Norwegian champions. They won the 2025 Eliteserien on the final day, beating Vålerenga 5-1 to finish one point ahead of Bodø/Glimt for their first title in 34 years. They then beat Dinamo Zagreb 5-3 on aggregate in the play-off round to reach the league phase.

Their four European games have brought one win, one draw and two defeats: 3-1 losses at Stuttgart and Aston Villa, a 3-3 home draw with Bayern Munich and a 4-1 home win over Sabah. That is nine scored and ten conceded. They have kept no clean sheet in the competition (match preview).

At home they are second in the 2026 Eliteserien on 62 points from 27 games, three behind Bodø/Glimt with three rounds left, per FotMob. Their last outing was a 3-2 defeat at Tromsø on Saturday, and they host Vålerenga on Sunday, so this trip lands in the middle of a title race.

System and the men to stop

Joint head coaches Bjarte Lunde Aarsheim and Morten Jensen, in charge together since 2021, set up in a 4-3-3. Simeone called them hard and well organised, and dangerous on the counter and from set pieces.

The three to stop: Peter Christiansen, with 19 league goals and seven assists; captain Zlatko Tripić, 33, with 11 goals and more than 20 assists this season; and striker Erik Botheim, signed from Malmö. Viking have scored in 24 straight matches, so Atlético’s clean-sheet run will be tested.

Europa Press reported left-back Kristoffer Haugen and Christiansen as ill on Tuesday, although the same preview listed Christiansen in Viking’s probable XI, so treat his status as uncertain. The weak point is plain: 2.5 goals conceded per game in this competition. The clubs have never met.

Transfer News

January list: two full-backs first

Matteo Moretto says Simeone wants reinforcements on both flanks, with a midfielder only if someone leaves.

Atlético’s winter priorities have taken shape. Speaking on La Pizarra de Quintana on Tuesday, Matteo Moretto said the club wants a left-back and a right-back, and would add a central midfielder only if players leave, as reported by El Desmarque. Certainty: concrete interest, no named deal.

The possible exits on Moretto’s list are Alexander Sørloth, Ademola Lookman, Obed Vargas and Arnau Ortiz. How much the club can spend reportedly depends on what majority owner Apollo is willing to invest. Marca had flagged the left-back priority on November 10 and 11.

The name most often attached to that slot is Villarreal’s Sergi Cardona, whose contract ends in June 2027. Cardona said he had “zero news” about it. Certainty: concrete interest, per Marca.

Sørloth: valued by Simeone, wanted elsewhere

Sørloth has not scored this season. Marca reported on November 10 that Atlético were considering a January sale; his contract runs to 2028 and Villarreal hold a 20% sell-on share. Juventus have renewed interest and AS reports interest from Stuttgart. Certainty: concrete interest; no bid reported.

El GolDigital, single-sourced, says Atlético rule out a loan and want a permanent sale. Simeone was candid on Tuesday: “His future is not something I can decide, he has to. I don’t mind him playing 20 minutes; maybe he does.” He added that the squad has no other player with his characteristics.

Álvarez changes agents; Romano sees no January move

Julián Álvarez is switching representation from Fernando Hidalgo to Pablo Barquero, who also represents Rodri, according to Fabrizio Romano on DAZN. Romano said there are “no movements” to sign Álvarez in January, that Arsenal tried late in the summer and that the forward did not want to return to England.

Romano also denied any swap involving Arsenal’s Martín Zubimendi. Reports of PSG preparing a January offer, with a €130M price, come from low-reliability accounts relayed by aggregators. Treat them as rumour.

Lookman’s first year turns uncertain

Lookman, signed in February 2026 for €35M plus €5M in variables and contracted to 2030, has one goal in 460 minutes, per Fichajes.net. He was benched against Barcelona, then sprained a knee with Nigeria on November 13 and is out for about a month. Moretto lists him as a possible sale. Certainty: rumour; no offer reported.

The Academy

Torres’s Madrileño turn it around in Barcelona

Fernando Torres’s Atlético Madrileño came from behind to win 2-1 at Sant Andreu on Saturday in Primera Federación Group 2. Jeremy de León put the hosts ahead after 11 minutes and was sent off on 29. Romeo Hueso equalised with a deflected shot on 48, and Koke Mota won it from outside the box five minutes later, per VAVEL.

That is two wins in a row and 17 points, tenth in the group and four off the promotion play-offs. Next is a trip to UD Ibiza on Saturday at 16:30 in Spain (7:30 AM Pacific), per Futbolme.

Juvenil A

The under-19s are unbeaten in the UEFA Youth League: a 2-2 draw at Liverpool, then wins over Manchester United (3-0), Stuttgart (4-2) and Bayern Munich (4-1), when Gabriel Obama scored twice and Joel Sánchez added his third of the campaign, per Telemadrid. They host Viking’s under-19s on Wednesday (TUDN); a kickoff time had not been published.

In the División de Honor they won 2-0 at AD Mérida on Saturday and sit second, six points behind Real Madrid, per Futbolme. During the break six Juvenil A players, among them Sergio Peragón and Gonzalo Benito, trained with the first team at Majadahonda.

Women’s Team

Jensen’s double keeps Atlético in the chase

Ten league goals in 11 games for the Norwegian, and a sixth straight match without defeat.

Atlético de Madrid Femenino won 3-1 at Eibar on Saturday to stretch their Liga F unbeaten run to six games. After a goalless first half, Synne Jensen scored on 51 and 61 minutes, the second set up by Fiamma Benítez, and substitute Kathrine Kühl added a third on 75, again from Fiamma. Mireia Masegur replied for Eibar, per EFE.

Jensen now has ten league goals in 11 games, second in the scoring chart behind Clàudia Pina, and Fiamma has six assists, third in the league. Six days earlier the team won 2-1 at Sevilla through Celia Segura and Rosa Otermín.

David González’s side are third on 25 points, behind Barcelona (31) and Real Madrid (27), per Liga F. They briefly went second on Saturday before Real Madrid beat Granada 5-1.

Absences and what is next

Lola Gallardo is on maternity leave, Silvia Lloris and Tere Abelleira are recovering from cruciate ligament injuries, and Otermín picked up an adductor problem on November 20, per VAVEL. There is no league game this week because of the international window.

Spain have called up Fiamma and Lucía Rodríguez for friendlies against Chile in León on Saturday and Venezuela in A Coruña on December 4. Atlético enter the Copa de la Reina in the last 16; the draw is on Thursday. The next league game is at home to Costa Adeje Tenerife on December 8 (Liga F). Atlético are not in Europe this season.

Off the Pitch

Gil Marín steps away from LaLiga’s top table

The CEO cites European commitments; Spanish media point to a deeper rift with Javier Tebas.

Atlético chief executive Miguel Ángel Gil Marín resigned on Tuesday as LaLiga’s first vice-president and from its board, Infobae reports. The club told EFE that his duties with UEFA and the European Football Clubs association made the roles hard to combine. He remains Atlético’s CEO.

Other readings exist. El Español links the move to LaLiga’s funding of the Union of European Clubs, a rival body to the EFC. Atlético keep their membership and vote in LaLiga’s Assembly.

Radio MARCA’s Antonio Sanz reported, single-sourced, that Simeone’s future is tied to Gil Marín’s and that two-year extensions to 2029 for both are possible (via Esto es Atleti). Treat that as unconfirmed.

Shares, stadium and the salary cap

A capital increase of €2,566,745, recorded on November 13 and published in the official BORME registry on November 20, lifts share capital to €47.54M (Esto es Atleti). Apollo Sports Capital has been majority owner since March.

The squad toured the Ciudad del Deporte works beside the Metropolitano on November 12. The club says the project is on schedule, with the training centre due to become the team’s base from 2027-28 and a 6,000-seat mini-stadium planned (iusport).

Atlético’s LaLiga squad-cost limit for 2026-27 is €361.287M, third in the league, per LaLiga’s September figures. No update has been published since.
