The Lead

Lautaro and Thuram return as Inter restart the chase in Bergamo

A point behind Lazio, Cristian Chivu gets his captain back for a trip to Maurizio Sarri's patched-up Atalanta, with Stuttgart three days later.

Two weeks after beating Como, Inter resume on Sunday in Bergamo, one point behind Lazio and one ahead of Roma. Kickoff at the New Balance Arena is 20:45 local time, 11:45 a.m. Pacific, and Maresca will referee with Gariglio on VAR, per Sky Sport Italia and Gianluca Di Marzio.

The biggest news of a quiet fortnight came from the training ground. Lautaro Martínez, out since the 4-3 home defeat by Club Brugge on October 13 with a left hamstring problem, rejoined group training on Tuesday for the first time in about a month and a half, according to Sky Sport Italia via FCInterNews.

Marcus Thuram trained alongside him, and both appear in the club's photos from Tuesday and Wednesday (Inter.it, Inter.it). Tuttosport reports that Chivu has recovered almost everyone, with Lautaro, Thuram, Piotr Zielinski and Curtis Jones all available (Tuttosport).

The Esposito question

The return creates a pleasant problem. Francesco Pio Esposito and Thuram started both games before the break, a 3-0 win in Rotterdam and the 2-0 against Como, and Inter kept clean sheets in each. Esposito then scored three times in two Nations League games for Italy and now has 10 goals in 15 caps (Inter.it).

Corriere dello Sport, as relayed by FantaMaster, expects Lautaro to start on the bench and come on. La Gazzetta dello Sport, via SOS Fanta, expects the strikers to share minutes with Stuttgart in mind. Both point the same way: Esposito and Thuram to start, the captain for the last half-hour.

“It's always special to receive an award, especially when it's my fellow coaches who choose the winner.”

Cristian Chivu, on winning the Panchina d'Oro, November 16

Where the game will be decided

Sarri used a 4-2-3-1 in the 1-0 win at Monza, and FantaMaster's probable line-ups expect the same shape: Ederson and Franck Kessié in front of the back four, Charles De Ketelaere behind Nikola Krstovic. Inter's 3-5-2 should have a spare man in midfield, and the pressure will fall on the two holding players.

The flanks look like Inter's best route. A back four has to cope with two wing-backs and two strikers, and Atalanta have conceded 20 goals in 11 league games, seven more than Inter. Federico Dimarco and Djed Spence, who both played during the break, should have room to cross.

The danger runs the other way through De Ketelaere, who drifts into the space between the lines, and Krstovic, who scored the winner at Monza. Manuel Akanji missed Switzerland's second game with muscle fatigue; Tuttosport says tests were negative and that Chivu will decide whether he starts.

The stakes

Four sides sit within five points, and Inter's run is about to get crowded. After Bergamo come Stuttgart at San Siro on Wednesday in the Champions League, Genoa in Serie A on Saturday, November 28, and Genoa again in the Coppa Italia on December 1 (Tuttomercatoweb).

That is four games in ten days with three fit strikers, because Ange-Yoan Bonny is still out. Recent history favours Inter in Bergamo: they won 1-0 there in December 2025 through Lautaro, and the March 2026 meeting at San Siro ended 1-1 (Tuttomercatoweb).

Scouting the Next Opponent

Atalanta: a winning run of one, and players coming back

Sarri's side ended six games without a win at Monza and should welcome back Bellanova and Raspadori for Inter's visit.

Atalanta come into the weekend 12th, on 14 points from 11 games, with 17 goals scored and 20 conceded, per Sky Sport Italia's table. Maurizio Sarri's side went six games without a win before Nikola Krstovic's goal after 49 minutes settled the trip to Monza on November 8 (Football Italia via LiveScore).

The run before that was a mess: a 4-4 home draw with Frosinone, 2-2 at Fiorentina, a 3-0 home defeat by Parma and a 1-1 draw with Kairat Almaty in the Conference League (Flashscore, Football Italia via LiveScore).

Players to stop

Nikola Krstovic is the focal point and has scored the goals that mattered lately, at Monza and against Kairat. Charles De Ketelaere is the link between midfield and attack, and Ederson the engine in front of the defence. Marco Carnesecchi was credited with protecting the win at Monza.

The break helped. Tuttomercatoweb reported on Tuesday that Raoul Bellanova and Giacomo Raspadori trained fully with the group, while Gaetano, Isak Hien, Odilon Kossounou and Kristensen were still working on their own and Pompei was in therapy (Tuttomercatoweb).

Inter have not lost to Atalanta in the recent meetings: 2-0 in Bergamo in March 2025, 1-0 in Bergamo in December 2025 and 1-1 at San Siro in March 2026, when Chivu was sent off (Fanpage).

Transfer News

Calhanoglu: Inter decide to renew, and the argument is about bonuses

Three newspapers and Sky agree the club wants to keep him beyond June 2027. The structure of the offer is the sticking point.

Hakan Calhanoglu's contract ends in June 2027, and this was the week Inter moved. On Sunday, Corriere dello Sport's Daniele Vitiello reported that talks had entered the decisive phase: the midfielder earns about six million euros and is open to less, and Inter want part of any new deal tied to results, especially in Europe (Corriere dello Sport).

Sky Sport Italia's Matteo Barzaghi said on Tuesday that a meeting with his agent was due in the coming days (FCInter1908), and on Wednesday Sky's Andrea Paventi said the club had decided it wants to renew him (FCInter1908).

Thursday's Corriere dello Sport, by Pietro Guadagno, adds the detail. Inter would offer a package worth about 6.5 million euros, but with a smaller guaranteed salary and bigger bonuses for trophies, Champions League progress and appearances. That bonus structure is what still has to be agreed (Corriere dello Sport).

The alternatives

Turkish daily Sabah reported on November 14 that Fenerbahçe plan a January offer worth nearly 10 million euros, per Internews24, and Fabrizio Romano said Juventus could try to sign him on a free transfer if talks fail, via FantaMaster. Corriere says he seems less keen on Turkey. Status: TALKS, offer being shaped.

 

Carlos Augusto: close, but the salary is still open

Sky and Romano say it is nearly done. Tuttosport says there is still a gap to bridge.

Carlos Augusto's renewal to June 2031 has been expected since October and is still not official. Sky's Barzaghi said on November 12 that it was in the final phase (FCInter1908), and Romano called it practically reached on Tuesday, with Matteo Moretto expecting signatures between late November and mid-December, per PassioneInter.

Tuttosport, also via PassioneInter, says the numbers are not settled: he earns 2.4 million euros, Inter first offered 3.3 million and he wants 5 million net, with a compromise near 4.2 million possible. Corriere dello Sport says Inter will close his deal before turning fully to Calhanoglu. Status: ADVANCED TALKS.

 

Stones decides January: a centre-back, not a striker

Inter are watching Lazio's Provstgaard for the summer, and selling could come first.

Bonny's injury revived talk of a fifth striker, but Corriere dello Sport and La Gazzetta dello Sport both reported that Inter will not sign one in January, and that a centre-back is the priority (Corriere dello Sport).

The deciding factor is John Stones. Hurt in his only start, against Udinese, he then missed seven matches, according to Calciomercato.com. If he stays fit, the plan to sign a defender next summer holds; if not, Inter would move in January.

The name in Thursday's Gazzetta is Oliver Provstgaard. Inter have, in the paper's word, booked the Lazio defender for next summer; Lazio want close to 40 million euros, which Inter cannot pay in January, and the Frattesi loan to Lazio could help bring the price down (Football Italia). Status: INTEREST, for summer 2027.

Selling may have to come first. Corriere dello Sport says Inter are open to offers for Luis Henrique, and Tuttosport values him at 20 to 25 million euros (FCInterNews). La Repubblica names Yann Bisseck as the most likely exit if a suitable offer arrives (FCInter1908).

The Squad

Almost a full house, with two gaps

Lautaro, Thuram, Zielinski and Jones are available. Bonny and Stankovic are not.

Cristian Chivu comes out of the break with most of his squad. Piotr Zielinski arrived at Poland's camp with a problem in the sole of his foot and missed the game in Romania, then started against Sweden on Tuesday and played 81 minutes (Inter.it, Fantacalcio).

Ange-Yoan Bonny is the clearest absence. The club confirmed a right hamstring strain on November 10 without a timeline (Inter.it); Sky's estimate of three to four weeks rules him out of Atalanta, Stuttgart and the first Genoa game, per Tuttomercatoweb. Aleksandar Stankovic has a right adductor injury reported by Serbia and is listed as out by FantaMaster.

Selection and form

FantaMaster's probable XI keeps the 3-5-2 that beat Feyenoord and Como: Josep Martínez; Akanji, Bastoni, Carlos Augusto; Spence, Barella, Calhanoglu, Mkhitaryan or Zielinski, Dimarco; Pio Esposito and Thuram. Nicolò Barella captained the side against Como in Lautaro's absence.

Stones and Curtis Jones were unused substitutes against Como and trained throughout the break. Stones also appeared at an Inter Store event on Wednesday (Inter.it). Thuram leads Inter's league scorers with six, ahead of Lautaro on four.

Chivu himself collected the Panchina d'Oro at Coverciano on Monday, voted the best Serie A coach of 2025-26 by his peers after his first season brought the league and cup double (Inter.it). SempreInter reports he received 16 of 43 votes.

The Academy

A quiet break for the Under-23s; Frattesi scores for Italy

Vecchi's side return against Potenza on Saturday, and Inter's best-known loanee had a big night at San Siro.

The Inter Under-23 side had no game during the international break. They sit seventh in Serie C Group C on 20 points from 13 games, per FotMob, after a 1-0 home win over Salernitana on November 7 in which Matheus Charlys scored (Inter.it). They host Potenza on Saturday.

The weekend's youth results were mixed, according to Inter.it. The Under-18s lost 2-1 at home to Como, and the Under-16s and Under-15s both lost to Milan. The girls' teams were in better form: the Under-19 women won 4-0 at Hellas Verona with two goals from Saragoni, and the Under-15 women beat Ternana 8-0 with four from Bosio.

Out on loan

Davide Frattesi, on loan at Lazio with an option to buy, started for Italy against France at San Siro and scored the goal that made it 2-1, before going off after 73 minutes; Tuttomercatoweb rated him 7.5 (Tuttomercatoweb). He then withdrew injured before the Belgium game.

Kristjan Asllani, on loan at Al Jazira, started both of Albania's wins, 2-0 against Finland and 2-0 against Belarus in Budapest, per Fox Sports' box score. The Digest could not confirm international or club minutes for Akinsanmiro (Monza) or Massolin (Cagliari) this week.

Inter Women

Five minutes undo Inter Women, then ten players come up short in Madrid

A 2-0 lead against Juventus became a 3-2 defeat, and a red card in the Champions League cost Sassarini's side again.

Inter Women were 2-0 up against Juventus at the Stadio Breda on Sunday with the 89th minute about to begin. Raphino had scored two headers, after 54 and 74 minutes, and Bugeja had hit the bar. Then Sarriegi scored after 89 minutes, Chiba in stoppage time off the post and Redondo after 94 minutes to win it 3-2 (Inter.it).

Inter's penalty appeals were turned down even after two review requests. The defeat ended an unbeaten start; Inter stay second on 16 points from seven games, five behind Roma, per FotMob.

Real Madrid 3-1 Inter Women

On Wednesday in the Women's Champions League, Beerensteyn put Real Madrid ahead after 28 minutes. Inter then lost Vilhjálmsdóttir to a red card after 41 minutes, when VAR upgraded a yellow. Cambiaghi still equalised from a corner routine in first-half stoppage time, before Bruun scored after 85 minutes and from a penalty in the 94th (Inter.it).

David Sassarini called the display decidedly positive. “Eleven against eleven we were on equal terms, and that is why there is regret,” he said, adding that in the Champions League you cannot get anything wrong (Inter.it).

Inter have four points from five league-phase games. Their last is at home to Arsenal on Wednesday, December 16, at 21:00 in Italy (12:00 p.m. Pacific). Next is a trip to Ternana in Serie A Women on Sunday.

Players Away

Esposito's three goals, Sucic at Wembley, and Italy go out

Inter's 11 internationals scored four goals between them, according to the club, but Italy and Croatia both missed the Nations League last eight.

Francesco Pio Esposito had a break of highs and one low. He scored twice in Italy's 3-1 win over France at San Siro on November 12, a close-range finish and a penalty, with Nicolò Barella winning the ball for Frattesi's goal and Federico Dimarco, on as a substitute, providing the assist (Inter.it, AGI).

In Brussels on Sunday he scored again after 11 minutes, but Belgium hit four and Lammens saved his stoppage-time penalty. Barella and Alessandro Bastoni started both games. Italy lost 4-1, finished third and are out of the March quarter-finals (Football Italia via LiveScore).

The rest of the group

Petar Sucic scored Croatia's equaliser at Wembley after 59 minutes before England won 2-1, then played 90 minutes in a 2-1 win over Czechia; Croatia also finished third (Sports Mole). Djed Spence was unused against Croatia, per the FA match centre, and came on after 83 minutes in England's 3-2 win in Madrid.

Andy Diouf came on for France in San Siro and started at left-back in a 2-1 win over Türkiye, in which Hakan Calhanoglu started and went off after 81 minutes; Türkiye finished bottom (Inter.it). Manuel Akanji captained Switzerland to a 0-0 draw in Slovenia that secured promotion to League A, then missed the 4-0 win over Scotland with muscle fatigue (Inter.it).

Zielinski missed Poland's first game with a foot problem and played 81 minutes in the 1-1 draw with Sweden. Carlos Augusto played 90 minutes at left-back as Brazil lost 3-2 to Japan in Singapore, then was an unused substitute in a 7-0 win over Singapore (Inter.it).

Off the Pitch

A new foundation, a stadium timetable and the price of Spence

Marotta talked up a second straight profit, and Milan's president set out when San Siro's replacement could start.

Inter launched Fondazione Inter on Wednesday, a club foundation that continues the work of Inter Campus, the social project begun in 1997, and is due to start operating in the coming weeks (Calcio e Finanza).

Speaking at the RCS Sport Industry Talk the same day, Beppe Marotta said owners Oaktree did not overturn the management, that the club had made a profit for a second straight year and that competence can match investment (Calcio e Finanza). Inter's 2025-26 accounts showed a profit of 22.7 million euros (Calcio e Finanza).

Calcio e Finanza also published what Inter paid for Djed Spence: 30.75 million euros in three instalments to 2028, with bonuses of up to 5.125 million and a 10.25 percent sell-on for Tottenham (Football Italia).

The new stadium

Milan president Paolo Scaroni said on Wednesday that the clubs now own San Siro and will decide on its demolition. The environmental review is due to start in March 2027, permits by September 2027, and building late in 2027 or early in 2028 (Calcio e Finanza). Seven city councillors have asked Mayor Sala to suspend the review (Calcio e Finanza).
