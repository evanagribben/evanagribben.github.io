AT A GLANCE

Outside Lands packed up on Sunday and the city exhaled — which makes this the good part of a San Francisco August. The crowds thin, the fog thickens, and the free stuff comes out. Stern Grove closes its 89th season this weekend with Public Enemy on Saturday and Al Green on Sunday, and then that's it until next June. Around it: the Perseids peak Wednesday night, the 49ers open the preseason at Levi's on Thursday, and the Giants play seven home games in the next week alone. Late August belongs to the neighborhoods — Oakland Chinatown and Palo Alto both throw street festivals on the 22nd–23rd, the Castro blocks off Market on the 29th — and Labor Day weekend hands you Millbrae's art-and-wine sprawl plus a kite festival at Candlestick. Six of the picks below are free. Two are only-in-the-Bay (marked ★).

WEEKEND WEATHER — SAN FRANCISCO

★ PICK OF THE MONTH
Stern Grove Festival, closing weekend: Public Enemy & Al Green
SAT AUG 15 & SUN AUG 16 · SIGMUND STERN GROVE, 19TH AVE & SLOAT, SUNSET · FREE (GA LOTTERY) · ALL AGES

Why this one: because it is the last of it. Stern Grove is in its 89th year and this is the final weekend of the 2026 season — the other eight concerts, June 14 through August 9, are all marked sold out, so Aug 15 and 16 are the only remaining chances to see a free show in the eucalyptus amphitheater this year. The billing is also unusually good even by Grove standards: Saturday is Public Enemy with Vallejo's LaRussell and KPFA's Davey D — a genuinely Bay-rooted hip-hop bill, not a touring package dropped into a park. Sunday is Al Green, 80 years old, with Goapele, The Glide Ensemble and KALW's Wonway Posibul opening. Two American institutions, one meadow, no ticket price.

How to actually get in: general admission is free but distributed by lottery, with reserved tables sold separately and a community box office and partner giveaways alongside. Entry windows open roughly six weeks ahead and close about a week later, so the finale lotteries have likely already run — check the ticket page today for waitlist or day-of release, and buy a table if you want certainty. Getting there: parking around 19th & Sloat is a known disaster; take Muni (K/M to St. Francis Circle, or the 23/28 buses) and walk in. Gates open well before the music, and the lawn fills early — arriving two hours out is normal, not paranoid.

OUTDOORS · SPORTS · ACTIVE
OUTDOORS
Perseid meteor shower — peak nights — the year's most dependable meteor shower, peaking overnight Wed Aug 12 into the small hours of Thu Aug 13. Free. You have to leave the city: the Marina Headlands (Hawk Hill, Battery 129), Mount Tamalpais' Rock Spring, or Mount Diablo's summit road. The catch is fog, not clouds — the coastal marine layer often sits below the ridgelines, so going up is usually the answer. Check the summit webcams before driving, dress for 45°F, and give your eyes 20 minutes to adjust. Local listing good for: a midnight drive and a thermos
Fee-free day across the National Park Service — the NPS birthday waives entrance fees nationwide, which locally means Muir Woods, Point Reyes and Yosemite all drop admission. Tue Aug 25. Free. Important: fee-free does not waive Muir Woods' mandatory parking or shuttle reservation, which is separate and books out — reserve now if that's the plan. Point Reyes has no entrance fee anyway, so the real win there is that a Tuesday in late August is empty. Listing good for: a weekday you take off on purpose
★ Kite by the Bay Festival — Candlestick Point's ferocious afternoon wind, which ruined a stadium for forty years, turns out to be excellent for exactly one thing. Giant show kites, free flying lessons, and a stretch of shoreline most San Franciscans have never walked. Sun Sep 6. Candlestick Point State Recreation Area, Bayview. Free, all ages. Hard to reach without a car; the T-Third to Bayshore plus a rideshare is the realistic route. Wind is the feature — wear a real jacket. Listing good for: the best use anyone has found for Candlestick
Dahlia Society Competition & Show — the official flower of San Francisco, judged and displayed by the people who obsess over it, in the park where the city's dahlia garden lives. Sat Aug 15 & Sun Aug 16. Golden Gate Park, SF. Free. Pair it with the Dahlia Dell by the Conservatory of Flowers, which peaks right about now. Listing good for: an unexpectedly moving hour
Sunday Streets: Bayview — a mile of Third Street closed to cars, handed over to bikes, skates, food and neighborhood organizations.
