The Lead

Kane signs on to 2029 as Bayern lead the table into a bruising break

Bayern have tied their Ballon d'Or winner down at a reported club-record salary. The harder question is what the deal asks of a squad without its coach, its goalkeeper and three attackers.

The announcement came on Thursday, November 5, and it settled the one question that had hung over FC Bayern since the summer. Harry Kane has extended his contract to June 30, 2029, ten days after winning the Ballon d'Or in London. He has scored 156 goals in 161 games for the club, per Stuttgarter Zeitung.

The price is the story German media chased all week. Sport1 reports about 25 million euros gross a year plus bonuses. Abendzeitung says he can earn more than that, a club record. Neither figure is official; the club has not published terms.

How the deal was done

According to Sport1, talks turned concrete in August. CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen negotiated with Kane's brother Charlie and father Pat, and the hold-ups were image rights rather than money. The supervisory board signed off on Tuesday, November 3, and Kane put pen to paper the next day.

Abendzeitung adds one detail that matters for the long view: Kane wanted a deal to 2030, and Bayern did not give him that year, nor an option on it. He will be 35 when this contract ends. Bayern have paid at the top of the market, but they have capped the exposure.

He is worth every cent.

Jan-Christian Dreesen, as reported by Volksstimme/dpa

Dreesen admitted the club had stretched to the limit, and president Herbert Hainer called it a special moment. Not everyone agreed. Sky pundit Dietmar Hamann called the deal a risk, which drew a sharp reply from Max Eberl: in Germany, there is always someone who has to spit in the soup.

The striker is now the playmaker

The numbers explain why Bayern paid. Kane has 10 goals in 15 games in all competitions, per Sportsmole, though none in his last four. The more striking figure is creative: eight Bundesliga assists after nine matchdays, the most at this stage since data collection began in 2004-05, according to the club.

Saturday at Mainz showed the shape of it. Kane won the ball from a loose pass for Joshua Kimmich's opener and his blocked shot fell for Lennart Karl to make it 2-0. With Jamal Musiala, Serge Gnabry, Ismael Saibari and Luis Díaz out, Kane dropping in to create has been as valuable as Kane finishing.

What it means for the next six months

The deal arrives in an odd week. Head coach Vincent Kompany has not been on the bench since collapsing on October 23 with meningeal irritation, and Manuel Neuer is out with a partial tendon tear. Assistant Aaron Danks has gone four wins and a draw in five games in charge, yet Bayern only lead Dortmund on goal difference.

Kane's signature removes one variable from a crowded spring. The next is Michael Olise, whose deal also runs to 2029; Dreesen said the club will talk at the appropriate time, and Fabrizio Romano calls it the next mission. Keeping both is the plan. Paying both at the top of the scale is the test.

Neuer's record day turns sour, and Urbig's moment arrives

A 550th Bundesliga appearance ended with a partial tendon tear. At 40, the question is no longer only when he returns.

Saturday was meant to be a milestone. At 40 years and 225 days, Manuel Neuer became Bayern's oldest Bundesliga player in his 550th league appearance, per the club. Before half-time he flapped at the cross that led to Lee's disallowed goal and was hurt in the same play.

On Sunday the club confirmed a partial tendon tear in his right foot and gave no timeline. Bild expects about two months, per ran. Eberl said it was the same problem he had in the foot before; Neuer had already missed three games with tendon inflammation.

The worrying trend predates the injury. Opta data cited by TimesLIVE counts five errors leading to goals in his last 17 games, against ten in the previous 248. Neuer's contract ends in June 2027. For now, Jonas Urbig is in goal, and Eberl answered a flat no when asked if the hierarchy would change.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Scouting Köln: a side that cannot win on the road

René Wagner's team arrive at the Allianz Arena 15th, without a league win in four and without an away league win in more than a year.

1. FC Köln are 15th on 9 points (two wins, three draws, four defeats, goals 10:14), a place above the relegation play-off spot. Their last league outing was a 2-1 home defeat by Leverkusen on November 8, per ZDF, their first home league loss of the season.

The away record is the headline: 17 Bundesliga away games without a win, the last on October 3, 2025. Their form line, newest first, reads L 1-2 Leverkusen, D 1-1 at Union, W 5-0 Union in the Pokal, D 0-0 Schalke, L 0-1 at Frankfurt.

Who to stop

Said El Mala is the danger: a Germany youngster now in the No. 10 shirt, he scored late against Leverkusen. His older brother Malek El Mala scored on debut at Union. Captain and goalkeeper Marvin Schwäbe is in strong form, and Linton Maina was decisive in the cup rout of Union.

Coach René Wagner, who replaced Lukas Kwasniok in the spring, is under growing pressure. Striker Ragnar Ache has a thigh problem, but Wagner expects him back for Munich, per Fox Sports. We could not verify Köln's preferred formation, so we leave it out.

Where Bayern can hurt them: discipline and set pieces. Köln took six of the eleven yellow cards against Leverkusen and conceded from a seventh-minute free kick. They do create chances (1.59 expected goals at Union), so this is not a side that will only sit deep.

Transfer News

The left-wing hunt: summer targets, no January rush

Bayern are scouting wide forwards for 2027, but Eberl says the injury list is not yet a reason to buy in winter.

Bayern officially plan no winter signings, Sky reported on November 2, but they are watching the left-wing market. Before Mainz, Max Eberl said Musiala and Díaz return after the break, and any thoughts on winter would come then, including doing nothing at all.

Ngumoha: the summer favourite

Bild's Christian Falk reports Bayern were ready to pay about 70 million euros for Liverpool's Rio Ngumoha last summer, and that talks with the player's camp have continued. SI notes Liverpool called him not for sale, his deal runs to 2028, and his agent is discussing a new one. Certainty: concrete interest, no club-to-club talks.

Pietuszewski, Nusa and Mohya

Porto's Oskar Pietuszewski (18) has a reported 60 million euro release clause. Bayern sought accreditation to scout him, but A Bola says Porto have had no approach. Certainty: scouting interest.

Eberl admires Leipzig's Antonio Nusa, but Sky's Florian Plettenberg calls a move very, very unrealistic: Leipzig will not sell in January and want 70 to 90 million euros in summer, per a Yahoo summary of his report. Gladbach's Wael Mohya (17) is being monitored, Eberl confirmed. Certainty: interest only.

Barcelona ask about Tah; a centre-back is on the 2027 list

Bayern have no intention of selling, but the defensive picture could shift in summer.

Barcelona enquired about a winter move for Jonathan Tah, Sky reported on November 9. Bayern see no reason to sell and Tah, under contract to 2029, does not want to leave. Certainty: enquiry.

Falk and colleague Julian Agardi say Bayern want a centre-back next summer, naming Benfica's Tomás Araújo, valued at 35 to 40 million euros, via FCBinside's summary of their podcast. That likely depends on Kim Min-jae or Hiroki Ito leaving. Certainty: concrete interest, single-sourced.

Olise is next on the contract list

With Kane done, the club's most coveted attacker is the priority.

Michael Olise is tied to 2029, and Dreesen said on November 5 that the club would sit down at the appropriate time. Romano reports Bayern rebuffed a summer approach from Real Madrid. No formal talks are confirmed this week.

The Squad

Kompany back at Säbener Straße; Musiala and Díaz close in

The head coach is recovering on site, but nobody will put a date on his return to the bench.

Vincent Kompany has been back at the training ground since the start of this week, the club said in a statement quoted by FCBinside. He collapsed on October 23 and was treated in hospital for meningeal irritation until late October. Whether he coaches against Köln remains open.

Honorary president Uli Hoeneß told BR on November 8 he expected Kompany back in two or three weeks. Until then Aaron Danks stays in charge. At Mainz he made two changes from the Atlético win, bringing in Pavlović and Brown for Bischof and Davies.

Returns and absentees

Jamal Musiala joined part of team training on November 5, Bild reported, after a torn muscle fibre in his thigh. Danks hopes he and Luis Díaz can fully rejoin training after the break. Ismael Saibari had ankle surgery and is out for about three months; Serge Gnabry is expected out until around Christmas.

Hoeneß stirs the dressing room

The same BR appearance caused a stir. Hoeneß called Musiala's situation a dilemma and publicly doubted whether he would regain his old level after the health problems he has disclosed. Sky's Kerry Hau wrote that the comments harm team unity.

Hoeneß also said Karl, 18, lacks humility, after the teenager's dismissive celebration against Atlético, per dpa. Karl answered on the pitch with his second goal in two games.

Form lines

Michael Olise leads the squad with six Bundesliga goals and four assists, joint third in the league scoring chart behind Niclas Füllkrug (nine) and Patrik Schick (eight), per Ligainsider. Kimmich, back since the BVB game after missing three matches following his father's death, scored his first goal of the season at Mainz.

The Campus

Assomo's double keeps Bayern II rolling; the U19s stay top

Dante's reserves are nine league games unbeaten, and Leonhard Haas's U19s are already through to the next stage.

FC Bayern II beat SpVgg Bayreuth 3-1 at the Grünwalder Stadion on November 7. Bastian Assomo scored twice and David Santos Daiber added a stoppage-time third, per the BFV. Dante's side are eighth in the Regionalliga Bayern on 24 points. Saturday's game at Ansbach has been called off, the club says.

The U19s won 1-0 at Ingolstadt through Kuba Horoszczak's 57th-minute goal despite a missed penalty and a red card for Xaver Pucci. They lead their DFB-Nachwuchsliga group by six points and have qualified for the main round, per the club's weekly Campus round-up. In the Youth League they lost 4-1 at Atlético on November 3.

Debutants and departures

Lennart Karl is the Campus success story of the autumn, and he is now in Germany's squad. Tim Binder made his senior debut in the Pokal win over Magdeburg, and Campus staff marked it with a traditional Weißwurst breakfast.

Off the pitch, youth scouting lead Florian Zahn is leaving by mutual agreement after seven years, announced on November 11. It follows the November 2 news that head of scouting Nils Schmadtke will leave at the end of January.

Loan watch

Arijon Ibrahimović started for Augsburg in a 5-2 defeat at Leipzig and was withdrawn at half-time. Javi Fernández played in Nürnberg's 2-0 win over Darmstadt, which keeps them second in 2. Bundesliga. Felipe Chávez came on at half-time in Magdeburg's 4-1 win over St. Pauli, per a single lineup source.

We could not confirm this weekend's minutes for Deniz Ofli (Karlsruhe), Bryan Zaragoza (Espanyol), Magnus Dalpiaz (Hartberg), Richard Meier (Meppen) or Julien Yanda (Eldense).

FC Bayern Women

Bayern Women win in Paris, then head to Wolfsburg as leaders

Nine wins from nine in the league and a statement result at the Parc des Princes set up Saturday's top-of-the-table game.

FC Bayern Women won 2-1 at Paris Saint-Germain on Tuesday in the Women's Champions League. A Pernille Harder cutback was turned in by Katarzyna Kiedrzynek for an own goal, and Klara Bühl made it 2-0 before half-time; PSG's penalty came after the break, per the club.

That leaves José Barcala's side on eight points from four games. On Friday, November 6, they beat Werder Bremen 3-1 at home: Edna Imade headed in a Sarah Mattner cross, and Linda Dallmann and substitute Jovana Damnjanović scored late, per the club.

Gwinn returns, injuries mount

Giulia Gwinn returned from injury against Bremen. Lena Oberdorf is out with a reaction in her knee, Vanessa Gilles faces several weeks with a muscle injury, and Kassandra Potsi and Sophie Proost did not travel to Paris, per the club's preview. Bühl now has ten Champions League assists since the start of last season, more than anyone.

Bayern lead the Frauen-Bundesliga on 27 points, three clear of Wolfsburg, and are unbeaten in 51 league games. Saturday's trip to the Volkswagen Arena is first against second.

Players Away

Sixteen on the road: seven for Klopp's Germany

No Bayern player had played for his country by Wednesday; here is who is where.

Sixteen Bayern players are on international duty, per the club. Germany coach Jürgen Klopp called up seven: Urbig, Tah, Kimmich, Bischof, Brown, Karl and Pavlović, for games at Serbia and against the Netherlands, per Sportschau.

Harry Kane leads the Nations League scoring chart with six and faces Croatia, and his clubmate Josip Stanišić, at Wembley on Thursday. Upamecano and Olise play for France, Laimer for Austria, Ito for Japan, Davies for Canada and Kim Min-jae for South Korea. Santos Daiber is with Portugal's U20s.

Musiala and Gnabry were not selected because of injury, and Neuer's foot rules him out. No Bayern Women players are away this week; Germany's women next play in late November. Kickoff times in Pacific are in What to Watch.
