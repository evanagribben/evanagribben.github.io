The Lead

Eight wins in nine, and no striker needed: Como go third in Europe

Headers from Ramón and Da Cunha and a Milla curler beat Cagliari and AEK Athens. Now Spalletti’s Juventus visit, with Kean out for months.

Last week the worry was goals. Moise Kean had been taken to hospital, and Tasos Douvikas was the only fit centre-forward. Then Como played twice in four days, scored three times and conceded none. Not one of the goals came from a striker.

A centre-back and an attacking midfielder headed in against Cagliari on Saturday. A holding midfielder curled in the winner against AEK Athens on Tuesday. The results lift Como to fifth in Serie A on 26 points, one behind Milan, and third in the Champions League on 12.

The goals came from elsewhere

Against Cagliari, Yan Couto crossed for both. Jacobo Ramón headed the first after 24 minutes and Lucas Da Cunha the second after 56, per the club’s match report. Como had 63 per cent of the ball and 677 passes to 335, and six shots on target to one, per ESPN.

Cesc Fàbregas called it a “trap game” and said Ramón played with “more bite than a striker”, per Tuttosport. Da Cunha only started because Maxence Caqueret had a minor problem the day before.

AEK Athens were harder. They shut the middle, and Da Cunha slipped and put a penalty wide around the half-hour, per Football Italia. Sánchez made key saves around half-time. On 57 minutes Luis Milla curled in from outside the box after a pass from Martin Baturina, per Sky Sports.

Como finished with 19 shots to seven and an expected-goals tally of 2.02 to 0.53, per FootItalia. Nico Paz hit the base of the post in the second half. The margin was thin; the control was not.

“It is not normal for Como to win eight games out of nine.”

The Juventus test

Now comes the biggest league game of the autumn. Juventus visit the Sinigaglia on Sunday at 11:45 a.m. Pacific. Luciano Spalletti’s side are sixth on 21 points, five behind Como, and beat Venezia 4-0 last weekend. Como won both meetings last season 2-0.

The Digest’s reading of the key battles, based on Fantamaster’s probable line-ups: Couto’s crossing against Juan Cabal; Milla and Máximo Perrone against a Juve midfield of Koopmeiners, McKennie and Douglas Luiz; and Douvikas against Bremer.

The selection questions are real. Fàbregas picks his goalkeeper, Butez or Sánchez, match by match. Caqueret is doubtful. Assane Diao and Jayden Addai may make the bench. Cremonese follow in the Coppa Italia three days later, so expect rotation somewhere.

What is at stake

A win would take Como to 29 points and open an eight-point gap over Juventus. In Europe, three games remain: Real Betis away on December 9, then PSG at home and Barcelona away in January. The top eight go straight to the last 16.

Opta’s model, reported by SI, projects Como to finish sixth on about 15 points. Fàbregas was careful: qualification “has not arrived yet.” For a club in its first Champions League season, that is a sentence worth savouring.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Juventus: Spalletti’s 4-3-3, a Kolo Muani threat and two straight defeats to Como

Juve arrive sixth and five points behind, fresh from a 4-0 win, with a Europa League game in between.

Juventus are sixth on 21 points from 12 games with a goal difference of +7. Their last five completed games read W 4-0 Venezia (Nov 22), D 2-2 at Fiorentina, W 2-1 at AZ in the Europa League, W 2-0 Napoli and L 0-2 at Genoa, per statz.ai. They host Omonia in the Europa League tonight, so they will have two fewer days of rest than Como.

Manager, system and the men to stop

Luciano Spalletti is expected to use a 4-3-3. Fantamaster’s probable XI is Vicario; Çelik, Bremer, Kelly, Cabal; McKennie, Douglas Luiz, Koopmeiners; Kolo Muani, Woltemade, Boga.

Randal Kolo Muani has seven goals in all competitions and Nick Woltemade five, per statz.ai (single source). Koopmeiners, with four, is the midfield runner Como must track. Locatelli and Thuram are reported out, with Kalulu and Nico González to be assessed, per Diario del Web (single source).

Where Como can hurt them

The Digest’s view: without Locatelli, Juve’s midfield may struggle to match Como’s passing volume, and a Europa League game three days earlier adds legs to the question. Couto’s deliveries from the right have just produced two goals in a game, and Ramón is a set-piece threat.

History is on Como’s side lately. They won 2-0 at home in October 2025 and 2-0 in Turin in February 2026, after Juve had won the two meetings before that. JuveOggi reports Maurizio Mariani as referee (single source).

Transfer News

Camello stays first choice as Kean’s absence stretches to months

Gazzetta names the Rayo striker as Como’s January priority, and the alternatives are thin.

Kean’s injury has made January urgent. Gazzetta dello Sport, via TuttoCagliari, reported on November 21 that Sergio Camello of Rayo Vallecano is Como’s first choice, with an offer of about €10 million under consideration.

Camello, 25, has seven goals and two assists in 11 Liga games, and his contract runs to June 2027 with renewal talks stalled. Nicolò Schira reported the same interest. Certainty: concrete interest. No bid is confirmed.

The alternatives are less advanced. Atalanta’s Gianluca Scamacca is on the list but not in active talks, per Corriere della Sera via Chiamarsi Bomber. Gazzetta adds that Como wanted Sebastiano Esposito in the summer, but Napoli lead that race.

Kean’s terms do not change

The Fiorentina deal is reported as a €5 million loan fee with a €30 million obligation and €5 million in bonuses, per GOAL. Tuttosport adds that Fiorentina keep 10 per cent of any resale. Reported terms, not official.

COPE joins the chorus on Real Madrid and Nico Paz

A second Spanish outlet says Madrid have decided to use the €80m buy-back. Nothing is official, and the window is not until July 2027.

Last week Mundo Deportivo said it. This week Spanish radio network COPE, relayed by Bernabéu Digital on November 24, said Real Madrid have made a “final decision” to trigger Nico Paz’s €80 million buy-back.

The clause can only be used from July 1 to 15, 2027. Certainty: rumour, now from two Spanish outlets. Sporting director Carlalberto Ludi has called the Paz deal a “win-win” for both clubs, per Di Marzio. On the pitch, he hit the post against AEK.

The Squad

Kean out for months, Caqueret a doubt, and a squad stretched thin

Fàbregas had eight players unavailable against AEK. Diao and Addai are the reinforcements in sight.

Moise Kean was hurt in training on the evening of November 17 and taken to Sant’Anna hospital. On November 20, Cesc Fàbregas said he would be out for “a few months, not weeks” with “a bone problem” that Como want to resolve “definitively”, per Corriere dello Sport.

One outlet describes a fissure in the right tibia (single source; the club has not published a diagnosis). Fantacalcio, citing Corriere dello Sport, says his season may be compromised.

Availability for Juventus

Out: Moise Kean (tibia, months); Jesús Rodríguez (upper quadriceps, “at least three weeks” more, per Fàbregas)

Doubtful: Maxence Caqueret (muscle; missed both games this week, chances “difficult” per La Provincia); Assane Diao (hamstring); Jayden Addai (thigh)

Back: Trevoh Chalobah, who started against AEK and was planned for 80 minutes

Suspended: none, and nobody is one booking from a ban, per TifoJuventus

Reinforcements in sight

Fàbregas said he hopes to have Diao and Addai for Juventus, per TuttoMercatoWeb. Gazzetta, via Shango, says Diao may make the bench or play the final minutes.

After AEK, Fàbregas said eight players were unavailable, counting injuries and those not on the UEFA list, per 11contro11. He also said the absent wide men hurt: “With Jesús and Diao we would have had more fun.”

Goalkeepers, leaders and the young

Butez started against Cagliari and Sánchez against AEK. Fàbregas says the choice is his, match by match: “They are two great goalkeepers,” per SOS Fanta.

He called Milla “a leader in the dressing room, loved by everyone”, per Tuttosport. Da Cunha keeps the armband, and Fàbregas defended him after the penalty: “We cannot judge everything on the penalty.”

The shape was a 4-2-3-1 in both games. With the squad short, Fàbregas said Ricci and Liberali are ready for more minutes; Liberali started against Cagliari.

The Academy

A heavy Primavera defeat, a Youth League point, and Morata scores again

Falsini’s side lost 3-0 to Sassuolo and drew with AEK in Europe. On loan, Morata scored and limped off.

Como’s Primavera lost 3-0 at home to Sassuolo on November 21, with Frangella scoring once and Kulla twice, including a penalty, per MondoPrimavera. They are 11th in Primavera 1 on 15 points after 11 games, per the league table. They visit Parma on Saturday.

UEFA Youth League

The under-19s drew 1-1 with AEK Athens on November 24. AEK led through a 70th-minute header; Baralla equalised from the spot three minutes later, and goalkeeper Sgarbi saved a penalty on 77, per MondoPrimavera.

“The draw feels very harsh on us,” said coach Gianluca Falsini, per Il Saronno. Como have five points, likely outside the top 22 places that reach the knockout rounds, and finish at Real Betis on December 9.

Academy in the first team

Mattia Liberali, 19, started in Serie A against Cagliari. Matteo Papaccioli, who scored at Lens, was an unused substitute against AEK, as was Liberali. There were no new debuts this week.

Loan watch

Álvaro Morata set up one goal and scored another in Leganés’s 3-1 win at Andorra on November 20, then went off injured on 70 minutes, per ElGolDigital. There is no word yet on the injury.

Iván Azón started Getafe’s 3-0 home defeat to Atlético and played about 67 minutes, per LaLiga. Emil Audero played 90 minutes in Rayo’s 1-0 loss to Valencia. Alieu Fadera, back from injury, came on for Cagliari against Como around the 79th minute, per Unione Sarda. Andrés Cuenca started for Sporting Gijón against Las Palmas.

Women's Football

Como 1907 lead 2-0, lose 4-3 at Fiorentina; four called up by Italy

Mazzantini’s side were overturned by Fiorentina. The separately owned Como Women lost to unbeaten Roma.

A reminder: Como 1907 Women are the club’s own team, coached by Selena Mazzantini. Como Women are a separate club owned by the Mercury/13 group. The Digest covers both.

At Viola Park on November 22, Giacinti (7’) and Bonfantini (33’) put Como 1907 2-0 up. Then Fiorentina scored four: a Durante own goal from Ómarsdóttir’s shot (40’), Johansen (46’), Ómarsdóttir (53’) and Hurtig (74’), per Fiorentina’s report. Wullaert’s stoppage-time penalty made it 4-3.

Como 1907 have 14 points from eight games. On the figures we found, that is fifth, behind Roma (24, eight wins from eight), Inter 19, Fiorentina 16 and Juventus 15. They next host Parma on Sunday, December 13, at 6:00 a.m. Pacific in Erba, per the club.

Como Women

Paolo Tramezzani’s side lost 2-0 at home to Roma: Thøgersen scored on 30 minutes and Pilgrim chipped a second on 82, after Roma goalkeeper Baldi had been sent off, per Espansione TV. They are seventh on eight points and visit Juventus on December 12.

Called up by Italy

Four Como 1907 players are in Italy’s squad for the World Cup play-off against Finland: goalkeeper Francesca Durante, Aurora De Rita, Giuseppina Moraca and Agnese Bonfantini, per TuttoMercatoWeb. The legs are in Helsinki on December 1 and Taranto on December 5.

Players Away

No men’s window, but four Como 1907 players head to a World Cup play-off

The men’s November window closed last week, and no Como men’s player was on international duty in the last seven days. The international story this week is on the women’s side.

Durante, De Rita, Moraca and Bonfantini join Italy for the play-off against Finland: Tuesday, December 1, in Helsinki at 8:30 a.m. Pacific, then Saturday, December 5, in Taranto at 12:15 p.m. Pacific. We could not confirm call-ups for either Como women’s team from other countries.

In Europe, loanee Iván Azón’s Getafe are scheduled to play in the Conference League today; the result was not in when we went to press.

Off the Pitch

The Sinigaglia project is filed, and the clock is ticking

The stadium redevelopment is with the city at last. A councillor calls it eleven months lost.

Como have filed their Sinigaglia redevelopment project with the city, councillor Vittorio Nessi revealed at the November 23 council session, per ComoZero. ComoZero dates the filing to November 10; Calcio e Finanza, citing La Provincia, says November 13.

Nessi spoke of “11 months lost”. The original targets were the east and west stands by October 2027 and completion by August 2028. Mayor Alessandro Rapinese said he would act once city offices pass him the file.

The president’s week

Mirwan Suwarso was named Manager of the Year at the Social Football Summit 2026, accepting by video, per Disway.

Como were fined €8,000 for starting the second half against Cagliari about four minutes late, treated as a repeat offence, per TifoJuventus (single source).
