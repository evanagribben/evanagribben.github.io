The Lead

Fragile at the Bridge: Leeds rout lays bare what Alonso's Chelsea lack

Chelsea led through Emmanuel Emegha, then fell apart once Moisés Caicedo limped off. The numbers say this was no fluke, and Friday brings a desperate Forest.

For 25 minutes on Saturday, Chelsea looked like the side Xabi Alonso has been promising. Morgan Rogers nipped past Nico Elvedi and slipped in Emmanuel Emegha, who crashed home his first Premier League goal on his first start. Ninety minutes later the Bridge was booing. Leeds had scored four, and Alonso was talking about a team in a fragile place.

The final score, 4-1, was not flattering to Leeds. ESPN's numbers had them on 2.52 expected goals to Chelsea's 0.58, and both Sky Sports and SI counted seven Leeds big chances to none for the home side. Chelsea had more of the ball, about 57 per cent, and three shots on target. Leeds had eight.

The hinge: Caicedo's exit

Everyone in the building could see the turning point. Caicedo landed on his back in a challenge with James Justin and went off after 22 minutes, replaced by Jordan Henderson. Within six minutes Leeds were level. ESPN described Josh Acheampong misjudging a bounce in midfield, Dominic Calvert-Lewin feeding Noah Okafor, and Okafor scoring.

Alonso said as much afterwards. “What went wrong was after their equalizer, after the change with Moi, we lost the momentum completely,” he told reporters. It is a revealing admission. A midfield assembled at this cost should not depend on one player to stay upright, and Chelsea's does.

“We are in a fragile moment mentally.”

Xabi Alonso, after the Leeds defeat (Sky Sports)

The second half was worse. Rogers was penalised for handball when Jayden Bogle's shot struck his arm, and Calvert-Lewin converted after a long VAR check on 53 minutes. Okafor's second came five minutes later from a deflected Ethan Ampadu cross. Substitute Brenden Aaronson's late shot squeezed through Emiliano Martínez's legs in stoppage time.

A pattern, not a blip

This was the third league defeat in four games, after the 1-0 loss at Sunderland on November 8 and a home defeat to Tottenham. Sky noted it is the first season in 67 years that Chelsea have lost at home to both Leeds and Spurs. The Analyst logged it as the club's 100th home Premier League defeat.

The underlying numbers back the gloom. Chelsea have 20 league goals from 14.6 expected goals, and sit 12th on expected points, per a CFCDaily analysis. They have also conceded 20 in 11 games. The 4-2-3-1 and the 3-4-2-1 have both leaked, and Saturday's high line was punished repeatedly.

Leeds pressed better and ran further. Sky's data had them winning the ball back in the final third nine times to Chelsea's once, and Calvert-Lewin said Leeds outran them by six kilometres. “When we receive a punch, we didn't recover that strong, that tough enough,” Alonso said to Sky Sports.

What Friday demands

The trip to Nottingham Forest on Friday night is the kind of game that defines a week like this. Forest are 17th, but they beat Manchester City 3-1 at the City Ground this month. Alonso is likely to be without Caicedo, João Pedro, Reece James and Maxence Lacroix, which leaves the spine of the team thin.

The selection questions write themselves. Does Emegha keep his place after the one bright moment of Saturday? Does Alonso return to a back three to protect a defence that conceded 2.52 expected goals? And can Cole Palmer, anonymous against Leeds and left out of England's squad this month, find the game that has gone missing?

 

Inside the club: backing for Alonso, a touchline row and a January plan

The owners are not wobbling, according to the best-sourced reporting. The dressing room is a different question.

The Telegraph's Matt Law reported on Monday that Clearlake and the club's football leadership still back Alonso. The club is keen to avoid a repeat of last season's mid-season change, when Enzo Maresca left on New Year's Day. Law also reported that defence is the priority for January after poor afternoons for Fofana, Colwill and Acheampong.

Dean Jones of Flashscore reported the same backing on Monday, adding that it depends on results and that a couple of additions are very likely, via Tribal Football. Talk of a dressing-room “mutiny” comes from Football365 and Football Insider only, and the Daily Mail's Kieran Gill reported that Alonso is not under threat of a sudden sacking.

The flashpoint was at the final whistle. The Athletic's Simon Johnson and the Mail's Gill both reported an angry exchange between Palmer and the injured João Pedro, relayed by TEAMtalk. Law added that Palmer ignored Willie Isa, whom the Telegraph calls the club's cultural architect, and did not applaud the fans. Chelsea were booed at half-time and full-time, the Telegraph reported.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Nottingham Forest: Glasner's strugglers with a sting in the counter

Seventeenth, short of goals and wounded by Bournemouth, but the side that beat Manchester City 3-1 a fortnight ago.

Nottingham Forest sit 17th on nine points from 11 games (two wins, three draws, six defeats, goal difference minus six), per GOAL. They lost 2-0 at Bournemouth on Saturday despite 2.14 expected goals, the highest of the season by a team that failed to score, reported AOL.

Oliver Glasner took over in July, replacing Vítor Pereira, Sky Sports reported. A November preview projects his familiar 3-4-2-1, with Morgan Gibbs-White and James McAtee behind Liam Delap. Forest have no European football and no midweek game, per their fixture list, so they arrive rested.

The players to stop

Liam Delap is the threat in behind, with three league goals, two of them in the 3-1 win over City on November 7, per The Analyst. Morgan Gibbs-White leads Forest with three assists and is their highest-rated player on FotMob. Forest's wing-backs, Muñoz and Williams, push high to support them.

That height is the vulnerability. Analysts point to space behind Forest's wing-backs and to weakness at set pieces and second balls. Glasner himself said after Bournemouth: “When it's more a physical, intense game, we are struggling.” He hinted at January additions. Forest have scored just nine league goals.

Team news is cloudy. Nicolás Domínguez is out with a recurring hamstring problem, per a Wales247 preview, and Glasner called Schlager and Williams doubtful last week, per Nottingham Forest News. Recent history favours nobody: Forest won 3-1 at Stamford Bridge in May, months after Chelsea won 3-0 at the City Ground, per Football Web Pages.

Transfer News

Alex Scott tops the midfield list as the price talk climbs past £90m

The Bournemouth midfielder is the most heavily sourced January thread. Nothing is close to agreed between the clubs.

Alex Scott remains the name most often attached to Chelsea. Fabrizio Romano said on his YouTube channel that the 23-year-old is “very high” on the club's list, via FootballTransfers. Football.London reported that Bournemouth want more than £100m after rejecting a £64m bid in the summer, via AOL.

TEAMtalk's Fraser Fletcher reported on November 19 that personal terms are agreed and that Bournemouth expect bids near their valuation, in an exclusive. That personal-terms claim has not been confirmed by a tier-one outlet. Scott has rejected a new contract, is currently out with a thigh injury, and Liverpool and Arsenal are also interested. Certainty: concrete interest; personal terms reported, single source.

The rest of the midfield shortlist

Monaco's Lamine Camara is back in the frame. A deal was agreed on deadline day in September before Monaco pulled out, and TEAMtalk reports contact has been maintained, relayed by FootballTransfers. GOAL linked Chelsea with a double winter swoop for Scott and Camara in a Monday report. Certainty: concrete interest, no French origin source this week.

Alonso's admiration for Arsenal's Martín Zubimendi is real, Romano said, but Chelsea are not negotiating and a January move is unlikely, per Football365. Napoli's Scott McTominay has been linked by TEAMtalk, relayed by CaughtOffside. Certainty: rumour, single source.

Exits: Gusto, Gittens and a rebuffed PSG

Malo Gusto has told the club he would like to leave in January, the BBC's Nizaar Kinsella reported earlier this month, via Yardbarker. Law wrote on Monday that he could go if a suitable bid arrives. Certainty: player wants to leave; sale considered. That would open a right-back vacancy.

Roma have made contact over a January loan for Jamie Gittens, Corriere dello Sport reported, via Fantacalcio. Chelsea will not let him go without compensation. TEAMtalk reported that Chelsea turned away a PSG approach for Estêvão, relayed by FootballTransfers. Certainty: talks (Gittens); approach rejected, single source (Estêvão).

The Squad

A thin spine and a striker auditioning

Injuries now cut through the middle of the team, and Alonso's two systems have both conceded too much.

Chelsea lined up in a 4-2-3-1 against Leeds, with Emmanuel Emegha leading the line because João Pedro is out. SI noted the side has leaked goals in both that shape and the 3-4-2-1, in its ratings piece. Emegha's goal was a rare positive and he has earned another look at Forest.

The injury list is the story. Reece James had minor knee surgery and is out for around three months, per Sky Sports. The Evening Standard's Monday update lists João Pedro and Lacroix as January returns, Hato with no date, and Caicedo's knock still being assessed.

Form and roles

Morgan Rogers and João Pedro lead Chelsea's league scoring with five each, ahead of Palmer and Pedro Neto on three, per ESPN. Rogers has carried that form into November, scoring for England against Croatia. Palmer has three league goals and three assists, but Thomas Tuchel left him out of the England squad this month.

With James injured, the captaincy is a live question. None of the major match reports named who wore the armband against Leeds, so the Digest cannot confirm it. Set pieces brought nothing on Saturday: Chelsea won three corners and created no big chances from them or anything else.

Cobham

Watson and Ezenwata run riot as the U18s go top

A 5-0 win over Arsenal puts Dan Hogan's side three points clear, while a centre-back waits on the first-team bench.

The Chelsea U18s beat Arsenal 5-0 at Cobham on Saturday, per the club report. Reggie Watson scored in the seventh minute and again with a long-range free kick late on. Chizaram Ezenwata added two before half-time and Mathis Eboue made it five. Goalkeeper Freddy Bernal saved a penalty.

The win takes Dan Hogan's team top of the U18 Premier League on 24 points, three clear after 11 games. Ezenwata also scored a penalty for England U19s in a 5-0 win over Belarus during the international break, and Watson started England U17s' 2-0 win over Poland, per chelseafc.com.

U21s and the first-team door

The U21s had no game this week. They sit second in Premier League 2 on 16 points from seven games, per FotMob, and visit Norwich on Friday. Centre-back Olutayo Subuloye was an unused substitute against Leeds, still waiting on a debut, while Geovany Quenda won his first senior Portugal cap against Wales.

Chelsea Women

Buurman's late winner sinks PSG; Liverpool hold the leaders

Sonia Bompastor's side banked a Champions League knockout place, then dropped two points in the title race.

Chelsea Women beat Paris Saint-Germain 1-0 at Plough Lane on Wednesday, with Veerle Buurman scoring from a corner in the 87th minute, per Sky Sports. UEFA named her its player of the match and noted a club-record sixth straight Champions League clean sheet.

“She's a top talent. She's very mature, both on and off the pitch,” Sonia Bompastor said of the Dutch defender to chelseafc.com. Chelsea are unbeaten in the league phase with three wins and two draws, and UEFA says the knockout place is secured. They finish the phase at OH Leuven on December 16.

Stalled at Liverpool

Saturday's WSL trip ended 0-0, with Liverpool goalkeeper Khiara Keating making seven saves, per Sky Sports. “What we are missing is being clinical and ruthless in the box,” Bompastor said. Substitute Lola Brown impressed off the bench, according to the club report.

Chelsea still lead the table on 27 points from 11 games, two ahead of Manchester City, who lost to Spurs, per Sky Sports. Mayra Ramírez is still rehabbing away from the squad and Manaka Matsukubo has a glute problem, Hayters reported. The next league game is London City Lionesses at Stamford Bridge on December 12.

Players Away

Rogers shines for England; Neto scores and sees red

The November window ended on the 17th. Several Chelsea players came back in form, one with a suspension to think about.

Morgan Rogers scored the opener in England's 2-1 win over Croatia and played the full game as England won 3-2 in Spain, ending Spain's 43-game unbeaten run, per chelseafc.com. Estêvão scored once and set up two in a big Brazil win over Singapore on November 17, after starting a 3-2 loss to Japan.

Pedro Neto scored and hit the post in Portugal's 3-2 win in Wales, then was sent off for two yellow cards in two minutes, per Yahoo Sports. Geovany Quenda made his Portugal debut in the same game, and Aarón Anselmino debuted for Argentina in a 7-0 win over Costa Rica, Yahoo reported.

Moisés Caicedo scored and captained Ecuador to a 2-0 win over Costa Rica, then asked off at half-time against Bolivia. Roméo Lavia played 76 minutes of Belgium's 4-1 win over Italy, per chelseafc.com, and Malo Gusto started France's 3-1 defeat in Italy.

Women on international duty this week

The women's window opens this week. Hannah Hampton, Lucy Bronze, Keira Walsh, Lexi Potter and Aggie Beever-Jones are in England's squad for the World Cup play-off against Ukraine, per England Football. Alyssa Thompson (USA), Ellie Carpenter (Australia) and Sandy Baltimore (France) are also in action, per chelseafc.com.
