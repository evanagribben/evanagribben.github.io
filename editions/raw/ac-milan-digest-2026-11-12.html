The Lead

Amorim's split season: a Serie A contender, a Europa League emergency

Eleven league games in, Milan are two points better off than at the same stage last season. In Europe they have lost three of four and fallen outside the play-off places. Amorim says that is on him.

The week told both halves of Milan's season in five days. On Thursday, Ferencváros came to San Siro and won 2-0, Lenny Joseph scoring twice after the break. On Sunday a rebuilt side went to Marassi and beat Genoa 2-1, with Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic scoring inside 19 minutes. The league table smiles. The Europa League table does not.

The numbers in Serie A are good. Ruben Amorim, appointed in June after Massimiliano Allegri was dismissed following a fifth-place finish, has 24 points from 33: seven wins, three draws and one defeat. Gazzetta's comparison, relayed by Milanreports, puts that two points ahead of Allegri at this stage last season and seven ahead of Paulo Fonseca's start.

Milan are fourth, four points behind Inter, whom they beat 3-1 in the October 31 derby, and five behind Lazio, who are unbeaten on 29. The gap to fifth-placed Como is a single point, which is the more relevant margin for a club whose finances, Tuttosport argued this week, depend on returning to the Champions League.

The European problem

Europe is another story. Milan have one win and three defeats from four league-phase games: home losses to Benfica and Ferencváros, a 3-0 defeat at Bournemouth and a lone 4-0 win in Salzburg. Three points leave them 28th of 36. Flashscore called it Milan's worst start to a European league phase.

The Ferencváros defeat exposed the depth problem. With Mike Maignan suspended after his red card at Bournemouth, 21-year-old Lorenzo Torriani made his first senior start. Amorim also started Filippo Terracciano, Davide Bartesaghi, Ardon Jashari and Omari Hutchinson. Milan had 62 percent of the ball at half-time and two shots to Ferencváros's seven, per Vavel.

Both goals came from Kristoffer Zachariassen's work. First he stole in after Torriani fumbled a back-pass; then he squared on the counter for Joseph's second, according to Football Italia. Amorim made a triple change at half-time, sending on Pulisic, Diego Moreira and Mario Gila. Bamidele Yusuf's 77th-minute red card for the visitors changed nothing.

“We are not ready to be competitive on two fronts. Probably, I didn't do enough to make sure the team was ready.”

Ruben Amorim to Sky Sport Italia, November 5

Amorim did not hide. In his press conference he added: “We have to improve, otherwise it's over,” according to SoccerNews. Gazzetta reported a blunt dressing-room meeting afterwards in which Cisse, Jashari, Yunus Musah and Adrien Rabiot were among those discussed.

What Genoa told us

The reply at Marassi was efficient rather than dominant. The club's own tabellino lists a 4-4-1-1 with Musah at left-back and Cisse wide on the left. Rabiot crossed for Cisse's first-time finish on 13 minutes, and Gonçalo Ramos pounced on Alessandro Marcandalli's back-pass to set up Pulisic on 19, per Football Italia.

After that, Genoa had the shots: 17 to Milan's six, five on target to two, according to Vavel. Maignan's save from Vitinha on 73 minutes mattered before Mikael Ellertsson's 88th-minute goal set up a nervous finish. Milan picked up five yellow cards. That is a pattern worth watching against Frosinone, who shoot more than almost anyone in Serie A.

The verdict

The honest reading is that Milan's league results have outrun their control of games, much as Statz argues Lazio's have at the top. The squad lacks a second layer good enough for Thursday nights. Corriere dello Sport reports the club wants a centre-back, a left-back and a striker in January. Four Europa League games remain to climb back into the top 24.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Frosinone: the newcomers who shoot first and ask later

Picked by Statz for 18th in August, Massimiliano Alvini's side arrive at San Siro sixth on 20 points, two places and four points behind Milan.

Frosinone are the season's surprise. Six wins, two draws and three defeats have taken them to sixth with a goal difference of +9 (24 scored, 15 conceded), per Vavel. Their last five read W D W W L, the defeat a 1-0 loss at Cagliari on November 7, settled by Michel Adopo's 38th-minute strike, per AGI.

Coach Massimiliano Alvini used a 4-2-3-1 in Sardinia. Statz paints a chaotic, aggressive side: 20.1 shots a game (second in Serie A), the fewest passes in the league at 337 a game, and the most tackles (20.3) and fouls (14.5). They also concede the most expected goals, 2.1 a game, and have let in 15 from chances worth 23.3.

The players to stop

Giacomo Calò, 29, is the hub. Statz rates the defensive midfielder as Serie A's best performer so far (7.86), with 47 chances created and five goals, one a penalty. Antonio Raimondo, 22, has eight goals in Statz's count, but AGI reported he missed the Cagliari game injured. Giorgi Kvernadze has five, per Statz's match preview.

The vulnerability is obvious. A side that concedes this many chances should suit Ramos, Cisse and Pulisic, provided Milan keep the ball better than they did at Genoa. Frosinone's fouling also hands Milan set pieces. Gabriele Bracaglia already has five bookings. The last two meetings were Milan wins: 3-1 at San Siro in December 2023 and 3-2 in Frosinone in February 2024, per Statz.

Transfer News

Tresoldi: Milan's January striker hunt has a price, and it is steep

Gazzetta says Club Brugge want €60m for a striker with 14 goals in 17 games. Talks have not started.

Milan's clearest January target is Club Brugge striker Nicolò Tresoldi. La Gazzetta dello Sport, via Football Italia on November 12, reports that Club Brugge would consider a winter sale only at €60m. They are in no hurry, and no talks have started. Tresoldi has 14 goals in 17 games this season and is under contract until June 2029.

Matteo Moretto confirmed the interest on November 11, saying Gerry Cardinale “likes him a lot”, but called it a difficult pursuit, SempreMilan reports. Brugge want to keep him until the summer, and Moretto suggested €50m may not be enough. Corriere dello Sport put Brugge's demand at €40m and framed him as cover for Ramos. Interest from Germany, Spain and England is reported.

Certainty: concrete interest. No bid, no talks. The price is the obstacle, and the numbers reported range from €40m to €60m.

Cisse: Brighton told no, a new deal on the way

Milan rejected a verbal January offer from Brighton for Alphadjo Cisse, according to Moretto and Calciomercato, as reported by Football Italia. No figure was given. The 20-year-old's contract runs to June 2030, and the club plans an improved deal beyond that date with a significant pay rise. He has eight goals in 13 Serie A and Europa League appearances.

MilanNews reported this week that Allegri had been annoyed by the club's €8m deal for Cisse last season. That looks like a footnote now. Certainty: offer rejected; renewal planned, not signed.

The right side of defence

Amorim wants a right-sided defender, and Feyenoord's Givairo Read is the No. 1 target, per TuttoMercatoWeb on November 11. TMW also has Milan and Como monitoring Chelsea right-back Malo Gusto. Corriere dello Sport's wider plan for January is a centre-back, a left-back and a striker. Certainty: rumour, single-sourced on Read.

Endrick: a long shot with a price tag

Football Italia reports Real Madrid could consider selling Endrick for more than the up to €60m, add-ons included, they paid in 2024, with Roma and Milan both on alert. The 20-year-old said: “I don't know what will happen in January.” Milanreports quoted Fabrizio Romano saying Endrick “has not said no to Milan”. Certainty: interest only; a loan looks unlikely.

The Squad

A week off, three returns in sight

Amorim gave the squad time away after Genoa. When they reassemble on Monday, the infirmary should be emptier.

Amorim gave the players not on international duty a week off after Genoa. Training resumes on Monday, November 16, with the injured working at Milanello in the meantime, per SOS Fanta citing Gazzetta. With 13 players away, Milanello has been close to empty.

Gazzetta, via SempreMilan, sees a triple boost before Frosinone. Fikayo Tomori is aiming for the game but will not be rushed. Ruben Loftus-Cheek, who missed the last couple of games, is close. Shape and selection

The Genoa line-up showed Amorim's pragmatism. Musah at left-back and Cisse in a wide-left role let Milan play Luka Modrić and Rabiot centrally behind Pulisic and Ramos. Maignan, the club captain, returned from suspension; Rabiot led the side against Ferencváros. Amorim has described his training as built around games: “We train by playing.”

Discipline is a growing theme. Milan were booked five times at Marassi: Musah, Diawara, Rabiot, Pulisic and Gila. Rabiot also criticised the referees after the game, Football Italia reported.

Form guide

Cisse is the story of the autumn: eight goals in 13 appearances, including a hat-trick in Salzburg. Ramos, the €74m summer signing, has fewer goals than his fee suggests, but Gazzetta praised his “big sacrifices”, and his interception at Genoa made the winner. Moreira, scorer and star of the derby, said he will remember that night for the rest of his life.

The Academy

The Primavera hit five again; Futuro make it three in a row

Both of Milan's development sides won big at the weekend, and one Primavera graduate went straight into a Europa League start.

The Primavera beat AlbinoLeffe 5-1 in Primavera 1 on November 8, per acmilan.com. Balentien scored on six minutes, Ibrahimović struck twice (10' and 38'), and Anastasio and Zaramella added second-half goals. Gamba replied for the visitors. Coach Renna's side “hit five again”, in the club's words.

Milan Futuro beat Virtus CiseranoBergamo 3-0 in Serie D, SempreMilan reports. Fabio Pandolfi scored twice and Yahya Idrissi-Regragui finished a late counter. Sergio Navarro's side are third, two points off the top, and unbeaten in six. That followed a 2-1 midweek win over Piacenza and a 3-2 win over Virtus Verona the Sunday before. Next is Tritium on November 22.

Bonera looks back

Daniele Bonera, Futuro's first coach, told Fanpage the project “wasn't ready to compete” in Serie C, calling the problem “primarily structural”, per SempreMilan. The team was relegated in its first season. He named Camarda and Bartesaghi among the players whose value grew.

Debuts and loans

Lorenzo Torriani, 21, made his first senior start in goal against Ferencváros. Four young Rossoneri are with youth national teams this break: Bartesaghi and Camarda with Italy U21, Diawara with France U21 and Christian Comotto with Italy U20. On loan, Fofana has a goal and three assists in nine La Liga games for Sevilla. We could not verify current form for the other loanees this week.

Milan Women

Two defeats, no goals: a hard week for Bakker's side

Milan Women lost the derby 3-0 to Inter after defeat at Como, and finished the derby with ten players.

AC Milan Women lost 3-0 at home to Inter on November 7, per acmilan.com. Two Bugeja penalties and a Glionna goal decided it. Goalkeeper Estévez was sent off on 65 minutes for handling outside the area. Earlier, Sorelli's 32nd-minute red card was downgraded to a yellow after a video review.

Coach Suzanne Bakker set up in a 4-3-3 with Sesay, Grimshaw and Stokić among the attacking names. The derby followed a 1-0 defeat at Como on November 1, where Bernardi scored on 54 minutes, per the club. That is two league games without a goal.

Bakker called the next game “an important match”. The club had not confirmed the opponent or date in the pages we checked, so we will carry it next week.
