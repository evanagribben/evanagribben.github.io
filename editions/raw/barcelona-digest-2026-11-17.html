The Lead

Back from the break · La Liga, Matchday 13 preview

A costly break: Cubarsí a doubt as Barça face seven games in 21 days

No Barça player came home badly hurt, but the window took a toll: a hip flexor, a sore back, a red card and a reminder that the busiest stretch of the season starts on Sunday.

Top of La Liga and three points clear, Barcelona spent the international break without a club game for 14 days. They return to a run that Mundo Deportivo counts as seven matches in 21 days: Villarreal, Sabah, Deportivo, Atlético Madrid, Celta, Manchester City and Málaga. Hansi Flick will start it without at least one of his first-choice centre-backs fully fit.

That player is Pau Cubarsí. The Golden Boy winner started for Spain against England at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano on Sunday, conceded a penalty for a late challenge on Harry Kane on the stroke of half-time and was replaced by Dean Huijsen at the break (beIN Sports; 101 Great Goals). England won 3-2.

Barça's statement, as relayed by beIN, was short: Cubarsí "is suffering from tightness in his hip flexor (iliopsoas)", and "his recovery will determine whether he is available for the match against Villarreal." Barça Universal, citing AS, calls him a serious doubt. Tests described the problem as a minor overload.

“His recovery will determine whether he is available for the match against Villarreal.”

FC Barcelona medical statement on Pau Cubarsí, via beIN Sports

The damage report

Elsewhere the news was mixed rather than bad. Frenkie de Jong felt his back in the Netherlands' 7-0 win over Greece and was sent home before their trip to Berlin as a precaution (The False 9). Netherlands coach Xavi Hernández called it "very minor". Without him the Dutch lost 5-0 to Germany.

Lamine Yamal missed Spain's 2-0 win in Prague with a knock to his right knee, picked up at Atlético (Sports Mole). He then played the full game against England, according to Fox Sports' box score, won the foul that got Elliot Anderson sent off and crossed for Nico Williams's goal (Fox Sports).

Raphinha is the other story. The captain was sent off, along with Brazil coach Carlo Ancelotti, for protesting in a 3-2 friendly defeat by Japan in Singapore on Saturday (Al Jazeera). "I'd like to take this opportunity to apologise for my behaviour and for being sent off," he said. The ban kept him out of Tuesday's game against Singapore.

There was good news at the training ground. Dani Olmo, out since injuring his left adductor against PSG on October 20, trained with the group on Monday, and the club are counting on him for Sunday if he responds well (Goal). Jules Koundé, who sprained his left ankle on October 17, is still working apart from the group.

Why Sunday matters

The table gives Barça a cushion but not a big one. They have 33 points from 12 games, Real Madrid 30 and Atlético 28. Madrid are due to host Celta on Saturday, the day before Barça play, so the leaders could kick off level on points.

The back line is the worry. With Rodri, Koundé and possibly Cubarsí missing, Flick's options at centre-back are Gerard Martín, Andreas Christensen and Eric García, all of whom played at the Metropolitano before the break. Christensen has just returned from injury, and Goal reported in October that he will be managed carefully for the rest of the season.

Villarreal will test exactly that. Iñigo Pérez's side had 73 per cent of the ball against Getafe before the break, and they won 5-3 at Barça's home ground as recently as 2023-24, according to the club's head-to-head record. The full scouting report is below.

The key battles

The first is Yamal against Villarreal's left side. Carlos Romero, an attacking left-back, scored against Getafe, and the space behind him is where Yamal does his best work. The second is in midfield, where Pape Gueye anchors Villarreal's double pivot and Barça will again be without Rodri's control.

The third is selection. Flick has used Brian Fariñas and De Jong at right-back in Koundé's absence. If Cubarsí cannot start, the most experienced remaining pairing is Christensen and Eric García, with Gerard Martín able to cover at left-back. The run of seven games means rotation is coming either way.

Secondary feature

€300m secured to finish Spotify Camp Nou

The money is in, the contractor is confirmed, and the club is preparing for a season at Montjuïc.

FC Barcelona announced on Friday, November 13, that it had finalised a €300 million issue to pay for completing the stadium. Members approved the financing at the Ordinary General Assembly on September 19, as part of a €510 million package: €300 million for construction and €210 million to cover cash-flow pressure from delays (Barça Universal, citing Mundo Deportivo).

Limak, the Turkish builder, will complete the final phase. The first two tiers are done, and the third tier and VIP boxes are due to be finished by the end of this season (SB Nation via Yahoo). The 3,000-tonne roof goes up after the season ends.

That is why Barça have provisionally reserved the Estadi Olímpic Lluís Companys at Montjuïc for the first half of 2027-28, according to Mundo Deportivo, with a return to a finished Camp Nou expected in early 2028. The stadium is already booked for the 2029 men's Champions League final, and Joan Laporta has joined the city's bid for the 2030 World Cup final.

Scouting the Next Opponent

Next: Sunday, November 22

Villarreal: plenty of the ball, too many absences

Iñigo Pérez rotates more than Flick does, but four of his players have muscle problems going into the trip to Camp Nou.

Villarreal arrive eighth in La Liga with 15 points from 12 games: four wins, three draws and five defeats, with a goal difference of plus two (Sports Mole). They have scored 17 and conceded 15. Iñigo Pérez, appointed in the summer after his work at Rayo Vallecano, has them in a front-footed 4-2-3-1.

Their last game before the break was a 2-0 win over Getafe in which they had 73 per cent of possession but needed until the 80th minute to score. Before that they lost 6-0 at home to Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League. That pattern of control without a cutting edge, and a defence that can be opened up, is the one Barça will expect.

Who to stop

Pape Gueye is the midfield anchor and one of the most-used players in the squad, with 1,163 minutes, according to Castellón Plaza. Renato Veiga has played 1,350 of a possible 1,530 minutes (88.2 per cent). Attacking left-back Carlos Romero and winger Tajon Buchanan scored the goals against Getafe, and Peter Gulácsi is in goal.

Pérez spreads the load more than most. Castellón Plaza counts 27 players used in 17 games, with the most-used eleven taking 62.6 per cent of the minutes. For Barça, the same figure is 71.7 per cent. It has not prevented injuries: Ilias Akhomach, Gerard Moreno, Juan Foyth and Krepin Diatta all have muscle problems, and the paper says four players could miss the trip.

Where Barça can hurt them

Villarreal's full-backs push high, which leaves room for Yamal and Gordon to run into. Their record against Barça is poor: five wins in 39 meetings since 2011, according to the club. The last two meetings were Barça wins, 2-0 at La Cerámica in December 2025 and 4-1 at Spotify Camp Nou in February.

Transfer News

The big thread

The centre-back search reopens, and Gvardiol's name appears

Barça asked Manchester City about him. Nobody in Spain thinks a permanent deal is possible.

The injuries to Koundé, Rodri and now Cubarsí have pushed the January defender hunt back up the agenda. AS reported this week that Barcelona asked Manchester City about Josko Gvardiol during the summer talks that brought Rodri to Spain (Africa Soccer, citing AS). The left-footed Croat, 24, has just renewed to 2031.

Juan Jiménez, editor-in-chief of AS, was blunt: a move "barring a loan, seems impossible unless the atmosphere at City continues to deteriorate" (Football365, November 17). Status: enquiry; long shot.

The same AS report says Sky Sport Germany has revived Jonathan Tah of Bayern Munich, who shares minutes with Dayot Upamecano and Kim Min-jae, but describes a deal as difficult (Mundo Deportivo carried the Sky report on November 9). Status: link. Alessandro Bastoni has been dropped from the list.

The rule that decides it

La Vanguardia reported in early November that January signings will only happen if players leave. Barça are still operating under La Liga's 1:1 rule, spending one euro for each euro they save or earn, and Marca describes the finances as "quite complicated" (Barça Universal, citing Marca).

Raphinha's new contract helps this season because his pay rise only begins next summer. The agreed renewals for Xavi Espart, Marc Bernal, Brian Fariñas and Gerard Martín, by contrast, add to the wage bill.

Outgoing

Balde is the leading candidate to leave

Alejandro Balde is "the leading candidate to leave" in January, according to Marca, and Barça believe he could eventually agree to go. The offers received so far have not met the club's expectations. Jules Koundé has lost prominence through his ankle injury; Chelsea are named as admirers, and any exit would depend on the size of the offer. Status: club open to sales; no bids reported.

On loan

Ter Stegen keeps his options open

Marc-André ter Stegen, on loan at Ajax until June 2027 with no option to buy, told Sport Bild that "it is only November, so I really cannot say what will happen in the summer" (Africa Soccer). He is under contract at Barça until June 2028.

Speaking to COPE, he added: "I haven't been in contact with Hansi recently, unlike some players I still speak to from time to time" (Africa Soccer). Status: no change; future to be decided in the summer.

The Squad

Olmo's return, Koundé's wait and a thin back line

Flick gets an attacking midfielder back just as his defence runs short.

Hansi Flick's squad reassembled at the Ciutat Esportiva on Monday, and the headline was Olmo. The Spain midfielder has been out since the 2-4 Champions League defeat by PSG on October 20, and his response to group training will decide final clearance. A Barça Universal report carried by Yahoo Sports, citing AS, expects him to start on the bench.

Koundé is the frustration. His left-ankle sprain against Betis on October 17 was expected to cost him a week; it has now cost him more than four. With six days to go until the match, his situation "remains undecided", Goal reported, citing Sport.

The right side and the middle

Without Koundé, Flick has used academy midfielder Brian Fariñas at right-back for three straight starts and moved De Jong there at half-time at Atlético. Rodri's absence has put Marc Bernal into the holding role, where he played 74 minutes at the Metropolitano. Barça Universal reports that Rodri is unlikely to return before December.

Raphinha's week adds a second concern. Mundo Deportivo reports unease at the club about his workload with Brazil, despite an agreement to manage his minutes. Ancelotti praised him for staying with the squad in Singapore while suspended, calling him a leader.

Form and captaincy

Before the break Raphinha led La Liga with 15 goals and Yamal led for assists. Raphinha wears the armband, with Pedri, Eric García, De Jong and Yamal the other captains. Fermín's two goals at Atlético took him to eight for the season in all competitions.

La Masia

Barça Atlètic's unbeaten home record ends, but they stay top

Fourteen absentees, a penalty save and ten men at the finish against Girona B.

Juliano Belletti's Barça Atlètic lost for the first time at the Estadi Johan Cruyff this season, beaten 1-0 by Girona B on Saturday, November 14 (club report). Ricard Artero scored after 54 minutes. The club counted 14 players missing through injury and international call-ups.

It got worse as the game went on. Dani Ávila went off injured after six minutes, and Ibrahim Diarra was hurt after all five substitutions had been used, so Barça Atlètic finished with ten. Goalkeeper Max Bonfill kept the margin at one with a penalty save from Javi González with about ten minutes left. The club report says they remain top of their Segunda Federación group on 25 points.

Juvenil A

The Under-19s' next UEFA Youth League game is away to Sabah on Wednesday, November 25, the same day as the first team, and the league phase ends at home to Manchester City on December 8 (club schedule). They had five points from four games after a 3-3 draw with Aston Villa.

Out on loan

Diego Kochen, on loan at Lyngby, started in goal as the United States beat Haiti 6-0 in Miami on November 14 in the first leg of their Concacaf Nations League quarter-final (US Soccer). The second leg is in Cincinnati on Tuesday.

The other loanees had no club games during the break. Ter Stegen's comments on his future are in Transfer News, and Ronald Araújo (Liverpool) and Marc Casadó (Deportivo) resume club duty this weekend.

Barça Femení

Two draws, two lessons, and still four points clear

Graham Hansen rescued a point against Arsenal at Camp Nou. Van Asten rescued another at Riazor in the 102nd minute.

Pere Romeu's side were held 1-1 by Arsenal at Spotify Camp Nou on Thursday, the first meeting since the 2025 final (ESPN). Frida Maanum put Arsenal ahead after 22 minutes. Caroline Graham Hansen equalised on 59, from Esmee Brugts's assist according to Vavel.

Barça had 69 per cent of the ball and forced Arsenal goalkeeper Misa Rodríguez into saves from Graham Hansen and Sydney Schertenleib. Aitana Bonmatí came off the bench after 56 minutes. Ona Batlle, who left Barça for Arsenal this summer, came on for the visitors on the hour.

The draw leaves Barça on ten points from four games, level with OL Lyonnes and second on goal difference, with Arsenal on eight, per ESPN's table.

Rescued at Riazor

Sunday's Liga F trip to Deportivo Abanca was closer still. Romeu rested Aitana, Clara Serrajordi and Marta Torrejón and gave chances to younger players, a plan that "did not work", Europa Press wrote (Infobae). Vega put Dépor ahead early in the second half.

Barça were close to a second league defeat of the season when Renée van Asten headed in an equaliser in the 102nd minute. The Dutch centre-back, who also started against Arsenal, is the subject of a Mundo Deportivo profile as the young defender exciting the club.

Real Madrid could not take advantage: they drew 3-3 at home to Badalona, so Barça stay four points clear at the top. By our count from last week's table, that is 28 points from 11 games to Madrid's 24.

Next: Athletic Club at the Estadi Johan Cruyff on Sunday, November 22, at 10:00 AM Pacific (LaLiga). Kerolin (calf) was expected to miss about four weeks from early November.

Players Away

A bad week to be a Barça international

Spain, the Netherlands, Brazil and Portugal all lost. England were the exception.

Spain

Spain won 2-0 in Prague on Thursday, with Álex Baena scoring a penalty and Marc Pubill adding a late second against ten men (Karlobag, citing AFP; PSG club report). Cubarsí and Fermín started, Cubarsí hitting the woodwork with an offside header, and Pedri came off the bench. Yamal was absent with his knee knock.

Sunday's 3-2 defeat by England in Madrid ended a run that AFP put at 44 competitive games without defeat (SuperSport). England led 3-0 through Kane's penalty, Ezri Konsa and Ben White. Spain hit back after Anderson's red card through Mikel Oyarzabal's penalty and Nico Williams. Joan García was an unused substitute.

England, the Netherlands, Brazil

Anthony Gordon and England beat Croatia 2-1 at Wembley on Thursday, Kane's record 126th cap (Al Jazeera); we could not confirm Gordon's minutes. He started in Madrid and played 82 minutes. England top the group, and both they and Spain are in the March quarter-finals.

De Jong played in the Netherlands' 7-0 win over Greece before his back flared up (Al Jazeera). Without him, the Dutch lost 5-0 in Berlin, their heaviest defeat in an official competitive match, according to NL Times. Raphinha's red card against Japan is covered in The Lead.

Portugal and the rest

João Cancelo's Portugal lost 4-1 to Denmark in Braga on Saturday in their first game since Cristiano Ronaldo's international retirement (Al Jazeera). We could not confirm Cancelo's involvement. Portugal visit Wales on Tuesday. Kochen's USA start is in La Masia. Barça Femení's players had no international window this week.
